All three verified live. **Fixed** in `refs.bib` (keys unchanged; OpenReview ids retained in `note`).

**[moderate] M3 — Pre-registration incompleteness + RQ5 power arithmetic.** (a) The draft's Experimental Setup named datasets but not the frozen design constants that `paper.md` (topic root) pre-registers — pilot models (Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct), pool size N=16/64, budget grid, seeds, test machinery, and the power-≥0.80/INCONCLUSIVE thin-cell rule. Standalone, the draft's RQs were unreproducible and the "3 of 4 configurations" votes unanchored. (b) Power arithmetic for RQ5: the two aggregation rules disagree only on instances where an elimination flips the vote, so per-config discordance is plausibly ~5–10%; at n=500 and discordance 8%, the paired SE is ≈ √(0.08/500) ≈ 1.27 accuracy points — a 1.0-point effect floor has well under 50% power per cell (one-sided power ≈ 0.2). The per-config "3 of 4" criterion as written was likely to fail even under a true effect. **Fixed**: design constants + power rule mirrored into the draft; RQ5's primary test re-registered as the pooled paired comparison (n≈1,664–2,000), per-config results demoted to descriptive under the INCONCLUSIVE rule. No numbers fabricated — the 8%→1.3pt figure is stated as design arithmetic, and all values imported were already pre-registered in `paper.md`.

**[minor] m1 — C3's full-verification analogues unnamed.** AlphaCode example-test filtering (Li et al. 2022) and CodeT (Chen et al. 2022) eliminate candidates via cheap checks before aggregation; the draft's elimination-aware aggregator is their budgeted-partial-coverage counterpart and would be strengthened by conceding them. Not fixed (minor-only; would add 2 citations — left as camera-ready polish).

**[minor] m2 — RQ3 power is marginal, not broken.** At a true 2.0-point effect with ~6% discordance, per-cell one-sided power ≈ 0.5–0.6; the pre-existing INCONCLUSIVE rule (now mirrored into the draft) already covers under-powered cells. No change beyond M3's mirroring.

**[minor] m3 — PRM-as-binary-verifier thresholding.** The realistic tier maps a PRM *score* to a {0,1} verifier; the thresholding rule is implied by the FPR/FNR audit gate but never stated as a concrete procedure. One sentence at camera-ready.
