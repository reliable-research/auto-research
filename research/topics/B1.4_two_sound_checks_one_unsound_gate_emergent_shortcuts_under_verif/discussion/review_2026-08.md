### Framing firewall + verdict

Stripped of verbs, the surviving objects are: a facet-/budget-relative soundness definition
`Sound(V,t,Π)`; an emergent-label attribution procedure (re-attack rule); two new estimands
(composed-vs-strictest-single FAR under matched budget; kill-set-overlap complementarity as
predictor). These are real objects, not connective tissue — consistent with the 🟢 taste audit.

**Overall stance: 🟡→🟢 — novel-in-formulation-and-estimand, with one repositioning required.**
The paper is *carried* by rows 3–4 (prevalence + predictor on realistic stacks) and *sunk*, if
anything, by leaning its headline on row 1 (the constructed existence result). Honest delta
sentence: *"Relative to classical compositional-verification folklore and to R2-Guard's
single-guardrail leakage observation, this paper's real contribution is the first matched-budget
measurement of whether — and at what rate, and predictably where — Boolean composition of
individually certificate-carrying, heterogeneous LLM-era verifiers admits shortcuts neither
component admits."* The draft's own framing is close to this but still opens with the
construction; see finding F1.

**Temporal check (t_audit = 2026-08-15).** All 15 refs.bib arXiv IDs verified live with
matching titles. No method twin found in: the draft's own citations, the 11,683-paper corpus

## 2. Feasibility verdict — **FEASIBLE, with one statistics defect (fixed)**

- **Budgets (from digest N6 / paper.md, sanity-checked):** pilot: CPU-only exhaustive
  enumeration ≤10⁷ candidates/task, ~2 person-weeks, no GPU — a genuine minimum-viable
  kill-test exists (Stage-0 pilot + B0-P placebo + PG-A6 10-task grid gate before the sweep).
  Scale-up: ~3×10⁶ attacker LLM calls (~$10k–30k at small-model prices), ~10³ CPU-hours of
  embarrassingly parallel mutation/differential execution (200 tasks × 10³ mutants × 10⁴
  inputs ≈ 2×10⁹ executions — consistent with ~10³ CPU-hours at ~ms granularity), ~20h human
  adjudication of ~100 flagged shortcuts. No training runs (hardened-verifier and RM arms
  instantiated training-free, with the fidelity concession already in the Conclusion).
- **Power arithmetic:** defective as written — see F2. The 0.8-power/7pp claim was computed at
  nominal α=0.05 but the decision procedure is Holm-corrected across 12 comparisons; at the
  Holm floor (α/12) the same n retains ~0.8+ power only in the near-one-sided-discordance
  regime, which holds structurally for OR gates (monotonicity) but not for AND gates. The
  150-task thin-cell floor inherited the same uncorrected arithmetic (the corrected one-sided
  floor is ≈160). Fixed in draft+tex with pre-registered assumptions and fallbacks — no
  invented results.
- **Assumption load:** moderate and honestly gated (A2 DSL richness — flagged in Hand-off;
  A4 verdict stability — 3-of-3 rule existed in paper.md but was undefined in the draft, see
  F4; A6 grid instantiation — PG-A6 pilot gate). Task supply is adequate: MBPP+ (~378) +
  HumanEval+ (164) + BigCodeBench (1,140) + LiveCodeBench post-cutoff comfortably cover 200
  tasks/cell and the ~340-task enlargement fallback.

## 4. Findings (numbered, severity-tagged)

- **F1 [major] — Headline rests on the weakest row; the repo's own portfolio retired the topic
  over exactly this.** The pilot existence result (RQ1) is conditional on the pre-registered
  space-extension rule and is near-demonstrable-by-construction; the portfolio's [vacuous]
  retirement targets precisely this "hand-constructed existence gadget" reading. The
  defensible weight is RQ3 (rate on realistic stacks, no constructed extension) and RQ5
  (pre-computable predictor). *Fix applied:* explicit evidential-role repositioning in
  Abstract, Problem Definition, RQ1 hypothesis, and Conclusion (concede-and-claim; pilot =
  instrument validation + regime-conditional existence; headline = prevalence + predictor).
- **F2 [moderate] — Power claim does not survive its own correction procedure and omits its
  discordance assumption.** "0.8 power for 7pp at 200 tasks/cell" was nominal-α arithmetic,
  while the test is Holm-corrected across 12; the claim is valid at the corrected floor only
  under near-one-sided discordance (structural for OR via monotonicity; not for AND). The
  150-task thin-cell floor inherited the uncorrected arithmetic (corrected one-sided floor
  ≈160). *Fix applied:* RQ3 Setup and thin-cell policy rewritten with the assumption stated,
  OR/AND cases separated, corrected-threshold arithmetic, and pre-registered fallbacks
  (enlarge AND cells toward ~340 tasks from the BigCodeBench pool, else report the corrected
  minimum detectable asymmetry). `paper.md`'s N8 power note and E2 thin-cell clause should
  inherit this fix before Phase B (noted in pipeline.md; paper.md itself left untouched this
  pass per scope).
- **F3 [moderate] — Missing nearest classical neighbor: assume-guarantee / rely-guarantee
  compositional verification** (plus the adaptive-attack budget-matched evaluation discipline
  as methodological ancestor). The classical-literatures paragraph named ensembles-of-weak-
  defenses, N-version programming, and defense-in-depth but omitted the literature whose
  founding observation has the same logical shape as the paper's premise — a high-likelihood
  FM-reviewer attack. *Fix applied:* paragraph extended with named concede-and-claim
  (uncited by name-only, consistent with the repo's citation-governance rule that formal
