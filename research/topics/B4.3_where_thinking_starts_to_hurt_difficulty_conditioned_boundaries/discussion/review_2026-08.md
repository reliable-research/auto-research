## 3. Feasibility findings (se-paper-reviewer lens)

- **[major] F0 — headline gap claim falsified by prior work** (same evidence as C1; counted once). Abstract sentence "yet none of it publishes where the boundary lies or whether it can be anticipated cheaply", Intro ¶3, and Related Work ¶1 were factually wrong at t_audit. Fixed via concede-and-claim (see §5).
- **[moderate] F1 — power arithmetic mismatch in RQ2.** The pilot is "sized to detect a 5-percentage-point accuracy drop… power 0.8", but RQ2's expected outcome requires detecting a ≥**3**pp peak-to-max contrast, Holm-corrected across 5 strata × 3 arms. A design powered at 5pp is underpowered for its own 3pp success bar, and Holm across ~15 tests erodes it further; per-stratum n=100 (MATH-500 quintiles) × 8 rollouts makes the paired-bootstrap SE of an adjacent-length difference ≈1.5–2.5pp, so a 3pp step sits near the detection floor. Fix applied: scale-up sizing now explicitly re-targets the 3pp bar (16 rollouts, pre-committed enlargement), and the pilot's 5pp target is labeled as pilot-only.
- **[moderate] F2 — no compute budget stated anywhere.** Neither `paper_draft.md` nor `experiment_design.md` carries a token/GPU/dollar line. Arithmetic: pilot = 500 items × 7 lengths × 8 rollouts ≈ 28k rollouts/arm ≈ 130M thinking tokens/arm (grid sum 32,512 tokens per item-set pass), ×2 interventional arms ≈ **0.26B tokens**; scale-up = ≥3 models × ~2,500 items × 7 lengths × 16 rollouts × ≥2 arms ≈ 1.7M rollouts ≈ **5–10B thinking tokens** — days-to-weeks on a multi-GPU node self-hosted, or a mid-4-figure-dollar API bill. Feasible but must be declared; fix applied (budget paragraph added to Experimental Setup).
- **[minor] F3 — GPQA-Diamond stratification thinness.** 198 items → ~40 items/quintile; per-item stratified statistics there are as thin as AIME's (which the draft explicitly demotes to curves-only) but no analogous concession was stated for GPQA. Not revised (minor); flagged for the next design pass.
- **[minor] F4 — RQ7's verification-step detector is a keyword-and-structure heuristic** with no stated validation sample; construct validity of "verification/backtracking density" rests on it. Suggest a 100-trace human-labeled spot check before trusting RQ7 contrasts. Not revised (minor).
- **[minor] F5 — kill-tests present and adequate.** Every RQ carries an explicit falsification condition, RQ2's null reframe and the permutation placebo are genuine kill-tests; no revision needed (recorded as a strength).
