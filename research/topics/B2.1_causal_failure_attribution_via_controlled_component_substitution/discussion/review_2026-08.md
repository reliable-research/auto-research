### Framing-strip check (claim 1, the scooped row)

Stripped of "manufactures forward / rather than backward annotation" framing, the surviving
object is: *a per-class decision procedure (flip ∧ no-op-null ∧ identity-stability [∧ bypass])
that admits or rejects an injected label, plus the measured rejection rate*. That object exists
in no prior work, including Who&When Pro. The scooped part is only the framing that forward
manufacture itself is the contribution.

### Overall stance

**Novel-with-mandatory-concession.** The paper is carried by claim 2 (the certification gate +
premise-validity measurement, RQ2/RQ3), not by claim 1's manufacture-forward framing, which
Who&When Pro (2026-07-10) now occupies at 40× the scale. Honest one-sentence delta: *relative
to Who&When Pro, this paper's contribution is that its injected labels are certified against a
matched control lattice rather than assigned by convention — and the certification pass/reject
rates are themselves the first measurement of the substitution premise the entire
injection/intervention literature assumes.* Temporal check vs t_audit = 2026-08-15: Who&When
Pro (Jul 10) falls inside a 4-month concurrency window for an Oct 2 FSE submission —
concurrent work that **contextualizes but does not negate** — but at SE venues a hidden
method-twin on the headline claim is fatal and a conceded one is credited, so cite-and-position
is mandatory, not optional. Decay risk: this cluster's publication velocity is the highest in
the attribution area (portfolio: fastest-crowding tag); the certification-validity delta is
real today with a short half-life — the Aug 21 walking-skeleton go/no-go and Oct 2 window are
the binding clock.

## 2. Feasibility verdict (se-paper-reviewer, R1 dimension)

**FEASIBLE at small-lab scale, conditional on the walking-skeleton gate.** Arithmetic checked:

- **Compute/cost.** RQ2 arms: 3 classes × (≥100 fault + ≥100 no-op) + ≥100 identity ≈ 700
  trials × k = 4 replays ≈ 2,800 replays + base-run reproducibility screening + attribution
  battery ≈ 15,000–30,000 agent runs ⇒ $2k–6k API at Stage-2 scale (matches
  `experiment_design.md` and digest N6's estimate; not contradicted by anything in the draft).
- **Annotation.** RQ1's 200-trajectory human audit ≈ 15–25 annotator-hours; by-construction
  labels need no other annotation. The contamination control's "≥30 base tasks not present in
  the benchmark's public release" is the one unbudgeted authoring cost (finding 6).
- **Power.** RQ2 is powered against a true gap of 0.30 (not the 0.20 floor), with the
  conjunctive-criterion caveat stated and a pre-declared INCONCLUSIVE rule for
  under-0.8-power cells — correct arithmetic honesty. RQ1's per-category n≈67 gives ~±10pp
  CI half-width at 80% agreement: adequate for a gate, thin for a headline number (minor).
- **Assumption load.** Two live single-points-of-failure, both with pre-registered fallbacks:
  (a) base-task reproducibility yield (PG-1: ≥60 tasks succeed ≥4/5 at temperature 0; <30
  triggers scaffold redesign then global fallback); (b) the no-op flip bound ≤0.10 / identity
  ≤0.05 (violation routes to harness-fix or the pre-named negative-result pivot, which is
  itself publishable). Assumption load is high but every assumption has a named exit.
- **Minimum-viable kill-test.** Exists and is cheap: the walking skeleton (discussion.md:
  ~20 GAIA-style tasks, k = 3, four arms, ≈$21–105, <1 day, go/no-go **Aug 21**) falsifies
  the two killer assumptions before any scale spend. **Consistency check the task asked for:**
  the *plan* is walking-skeleton-first (discussion.md Aug 17–21; portfolio milestone), but the
  pre-revision draft referenced "the pilot" in RQ2 without ever defining it — fixed
  (finding 5). Timeline to FSE Oct 2 is tight but survivable given the descope ladder.
