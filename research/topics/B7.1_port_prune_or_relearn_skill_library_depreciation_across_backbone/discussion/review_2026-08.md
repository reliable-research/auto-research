## 2. Feasibility + accuracy findings (se-paper-reviewer lens)

| # | Severity | Finding |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | **[moderate]** | **Library Drift mischaracterized.** Draft called its treatment "environment/codebase drift" (Intro ¶3, Related Work ×2). The live abstract (2605.19576v3) defines library drift as *unbounded skill accumulation without outcome-driven lifecycle management* (MBPP+ hard-100, 100 rounds) — an accumulation/lifecycle axis, not codebase drift; the +0.0pp/+16.2pp figures are quoted there from SkillsBench. The environment axis actually belongs to SkillGuard (packages/services/APIs evolving) and SkillOps (dependency debt). Also unconceded: Library Drift's per-skill contribution scores (nearest neighbor to C2's ledger). Cheap reviewer kill as written; the 2026-08-15 supervisor spot-check missed it. |
| F2 | **[moderate]** | **"Model-generation axis remains unmeasured" overclaim.** The abstract asserted an unmeasured axis while Related Work concedes AFTER as "the only prior result on the backbone axis." AFTER's abstract additionally reports the qualitative heterogeneity observation (some skills transfer broadly, others lose effectiveness) — precedent for RQ4 that the draft nowhere conceded. Needs concede-and-claim: axis measured *functionally*, unmeasured *economically*. |
| F3 | **[moderate]** | **Token Economics (2605.09104) characterization unsupported.** It is a *survey*; its abstract conceptualizes *tokens* (production factors, exchange media, units of account), not "skills as capital goods," and does not visibly frame "acquisition as investment." Draft's sentence risked attributing a claim the source may not make. |
| F4 | **[moderate]** | **SkillsVote (2605.18401) undersold as "governance vote rather than measured value."** Its abstract describes outcome *attribution* (to skill-guided execution / exploration / environment / result signals) and evidence-gated updates, plus offline transfer via frozen libraries. The honest delta: attribution as a curation signal, not a cost-amortized economic estimand; no backbone-upgrade trigger. (Also folded in: SkillOps *does* have a "utility" health dimension — conceded, with the delta that it is not backbone-conditioned.) |
| F5 | **[moderate]** | **No measurement-budget arithmetic anywhere.** For a study whose thesis is token accounting, neither `paper_draft.md` nor `experiment_design.md` states total episode counts or a cost envelope. Derived from the stated targets: ≈5 library-level arm-regimes × 1,500 episodes under M_1 per (stream × pair) + M_0 baseline arms + per-skill ledger (up to 80 × 30 × 3 × 2 backbones) + interaction probe ≈ 2–3 × 10⁴ episodes per cell, low-10⁵ for the full grid — dominated by the SWE-bench stream (per-episode tokens orders of magnitude above MBPP+). Unpriced, this is a silent kill risk at execution time. |
| F6 | **[moderate]** | **Clustered replications treated as independent pairs.** 1,500 "paired episodes" = 300 tasks × 5 seeds; McNemar-style inference assumes independent pairs, but seeds within a task are correlated → effective N < 1,500 and the ≈0.8 power claim is optimistic at the stated budget. Needs pre-registered cluster-robust inference (cluster bootstrap over tasks) with power re-evaluated against pilot within-task correlation. |
| F7 | **[moderate]** | **Acquisition/evaluation split policy unstated; stream capacity unexamined.** If evaluation tasks overlap acquisition tasks, the M₀ asset baseline (gate A3) is inflated by memorization. And two streams cannot fund disjoint partitions from their canonical subsets: SWE-bench Lite has exactly 300 instances; ALFWorld's unseen eval split has 134 games — while the design wants an acquisition stream + a 300-task eval stream + RQ7's held-out split. Sourcing rule needed (larger pools; partition hashed pre-run). |
| F8 | [minor] | **Related-work staleness (verified, not assumed):** the newest cited work is 2606.23127 (June). The 2026-07/08 lifelong-learning wave (ASPIRE 2607.00272; GSE 2608.06153) is absent. GSE is also supportive positioning for the additivity probe. → Was logged as citation debt; resolved at the 2026-08-21 resumption (both verified with full author lists and added — see §5). |
| F9 | [minor] | **Activation-floor arithmetic tension.** 40–80 skills vs. a 300-task eval stream: at ~3 retrievals/task, mean retrievals/skill ≈ 11–22 — many skills sit below the floor of 20 outside ALFWorld, so the floor-eligible set (and RQ4's 20% tails) may be small. Already partially covered by the pilot gate and the "unmeasured at skill granularity" reporting rule; no text change forced. |
| F10 | [minor] | Draft's References-section preamble still said "the arXiv API was unreachable from this environment" — stale after the 2026-08-15 repair. Cosmetic; updated alongside the F1–F4 edits. |

**Power arithmetic check (verified):** at 300×5=1,500 nominal pairs, discordance 12–15% → 180–202 discordant pairs; detecting a 3pp marginal delta ≡ binomial 0.61–0.63 vs 0.5 → power ≈ 0.85–0.92 ≥ 0.8. The stated target is arithmetically sound *under independence* — F6 is about the independence assumption, not the arithmetic.

**Kill-test check:** present and real — walking skeleton (MBPP+ × one pair, ~1 day), pilot gates PG-1…PG-4, pre-committed INCONCLUSIVE labeling, global pivot wording for the no-flip null. Good.
