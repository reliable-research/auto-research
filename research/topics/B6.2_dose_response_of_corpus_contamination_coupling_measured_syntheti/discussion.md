## Stage 1 — Problem Definition

Core question (seed): what is the functional relationship between the synthetic fraction of a training corpus and downstream model degradation — is there a safety threshold, or is degradation monotonic (存在安全阈值还是单调退化)? Post-absorption, the question widens on the outcome axis: degradation now spans (i) capability, (ii) output diversity/tail preservation, and (iii) **memorization-and-privacy harm** — and on the treatment axis: the dose is not just *how much* synthetic text enters, but *which rarity stratum of human data it displaces*.

Formulation: a controlled-mixture continual-pretraining study. *Manipulated variables*: (1) exactly-known synthetic fraction ρ on a log-spaced grid (0–100%); (2) displacement target — synthetic tokens replace human tokens drawn from rare strata vs. common strata vs. proportionally (the absorbed B6.4 factor). *Held fixed*: total token budget, compute, starting checkpoint (frozen pretrained backbone whose original corpus predates ChatGPT), hyperparameters, shuffling policy. *Outcomes*: per-metric dose-response curves with seed-level uncertainty; a statistical verdict per metric among {flat, linear/monotone, convex power-law, change-point}; an equivalence-tested "statistically harmless" low-dose region if one exists; a memorization/privacy dose-response (canary extraction, membership inference, per-stratum memorization); and a forensics-coupling analysis quantifying how detector-estimated ρ̂ (instead of known ρ) distorts the threshold verdict — 从"测多少"走到"多少有害".

**Critic:** (1) The absorbed design now has two treatment factors and three outcome families — the problem statement risks becoming a grab-bag; it must state the single organizing claim that unifies capability loss and privacy harm under one dose axis. (2) "Synthetic data displacing rare strata" needs a precise operationalization or it is unfalsifiable: rarity of *what* (tokens? documents? domains?) at *what* granularity? (3) The frozen-backbone continual-pretraining proxy is a stated cost constraint, but the problem definition should admit upfront that the estimand is "dose-response *under continued pretraining at small scale*," not the from-scratch production curve (digest C5), or reviewers will read overclaim. (4) The privacy outcome is asserted but the mechanism linking contamination dose to privacy harm is not articulated — why would synthetic fraction change memorization of *human* data at all?

**Refined:** The unifying claim: **contamination and memorization are two faces of one budget.** Under the fixed-capacity view (~3.6 bits/param [VERIFY]), synthetic text is entropy-poor and tail-truncated; mixing it in (a) reduces the effective diversity of the training signal, degrading capability/coverage, and (b) changes what fills the memorization budget — in particular, when synthetic text *displaces rare human data*, the surviving rare examples become rarer still and (per the rarity-driven memorization result [VERIFY]) are memorized *harder*, so contamination plausibly **increases per-example privacy risk for the residual minority data even as aggregate capability degrades**. That is one dose axis with two coupled harms, and it is the mechanistic reason rarity must be a designed factor rather than a covariate. Operationalization of rarity: document-level strata assigned by (i) mean token rarity (bottom-decile-frequency token density) and (ii) topic-cluster population size (minority-domain proxy), pre-registered, with per-stratum canaries as the controlled probe (Stage 5). Estimand honesty: the paper's claims are scoped to "fixed-backbone continued pretraining at ≤1.4B scale, fixed budget"; scale/regime transfer is measured as a trend (two backbones) but not claimed resolved.

## Stage 2 — Motivation

1. **From "how much is present" to "how much is harmful."** Detection/forensics work scores whether documents are AI-generated (e.g., DandR-style black-box detectors) but never couples the detected quantity to a training-harm quantity; curators who can measure contamination still cannot act on it because no dose-response curve exists to consult (seed: 把取证接到 model collapse 上).
2. **The collapse literature brackets exactly the deployed regime.** Foundational results operate at the extreme dose — full recursive replacement across generations (Shumailov et al., Nature 2024) — or contrast two discrete regimes, replace vs. accumulate (Gerstgrasser et al., 2024). Mixing-ratio theory derives closed-form optima only in regression abstractions (PreventCollapseMixing). Nobody has swept a controlled within-generation fraction grid at LM scale and statistically adjudicated threshold vs. monotonic — the question every curator actually faces (is 3% tolerable?).
3. **The rarity-memorization refresh raises the stakes.** The ICML 2026 result [VERIFY — see header] establishes a fixed memorization capacity (~3.6 bits/param) with rare/minority tokens disproportionately memorized. If contamination shifts the rarity profile of the surviving human data, then dose-response is not only a capability question but a **privacy** question: the same curve that tells curators "how much capability you lose" may also tell them "how much more extractable your minority-language or long-tail user data becomes." No existing work couples contamination dose to memorization outcome. This is the audit's rationale for absorbing B6.4 here.
4. **Timeliness and uniqueness in the portfolio.** Web corpora post-2022 are increasingly contaminated and provenance filtering is failing; B6.2 is the only B6 topic that survived the audit as a build target, so it must carry the mechanism layer (机制 label) of the contamination line alone.

**Critic:** (1) Motivation 3 leans on an unverified citation for the central refresh — if the ICML 2026 paper turns out to say something narrower (e.g., capacity measured only on from-scratch GPT-class runs, or "rare sequences" rather than minority tokens), the privacy motivation must degrade gracefully; state the fallback motivation now. (2) Motivation 2's "nobody has swept a fraction grid" is a strong absence claim — reverse-digest searches are corpus-limited; a fresh literature check for 2025–26 dose-sweep papers (the idea is obvious enough that parallel work is likely) is mandatory before submission and the motivation should acknowledge the race. (3) Motivation 1 assumes curators would act on a threshold; a reviewer may object that any measured "safe" threshold is generator- and scale-specific — pre-empt by framing the deliverable as the *methodology + curve family + direction of detector bias*, not one magic number.

**Refined:** Motivation 3 gains a citation-independent floor: even without the capacity result, the mechanism argument stands on established findings — memorization increases with example rarity/duplication (Carlini et al. 2022 quantifying-memorization line [VERIFY exact citation]) and synthetic text is measurably lower-entropy than human text (CloserLookCollapse's entropy-decline indicator) — so "contamination reshapes what gets memorized" is testable regardless; the ICML 2026 result upgrades the motivation from plausible to quantitative but is not load-bearing. A pre-submission novelty sweep for 2025–26 contamination-dose papers is added as an explicit gate (G0, Stage 6). Motivation 1 is reframed: the contribution is a *transferable measurement protocol and a shape verdict* (threshold vs. monotonic vs. convex, and the direction detector noise bends policy — predicted: toward permissiveness), with any specific threshold number explicitly scoped to the studied scale/generator; this survives the "your number won't transfer" objection because the number is not the claim.

## Stage 4 — Sub-Problems

1. **SP1 — Confound-free dose axis.** Provenance-clean human pool (pre-2022 C4/Pile slice); synthetic pool from an open instruction-tuned generator, distribution-matched on length/topic; fixed token budget, checkpoint, hyperparameters, shuffle policy across all ρ — synthetic fraction as the *sole* manipulated variable (digest C1).
2. **SP2 — Rarity-factor design (absorbed from B6.4).** Stratify the human pool into rarity strata (rare-token-density deciles × topic-cluster size); implement displacement targeting: at equal total ρ, synthetic tokens replace (a) rare-stratum, (b) common-stratum, or (c) proportionally sampled human documents — with duplication profiles matched across (a)–(c). *Hard part*: keeping the rarity factor orthogonal to dose and duplication — rare documents differ in length, topic, and quality, so naive displacement confounds rarity with everything else; requires matched-pair displacement (each displaced rare doc matched to a retained common doc on length/quality proxies) and a pre-registered stratum definition.
3. **SP3 — Dual outcome batteries.** *Degradation*: clean held-out perplexity (primary), few-shot suite, distinct-n/Self-BLEU/embedding coverage, rare-token recall + tail-decile perplexity, NN differential entropy. *Memorization/privacy*: per-stratum canaries (secret-sharer exposure) at controlled duplication (1/4/16), prefix-prompted verbatim extraction rate on natural rare documents, calibrated MIA AUC per stratum. *Hard part*: canaries must probe rarity without perturbing the training distribution they measure (canary mass ≪ 0.01% of tokens; canary style matched to stratum register).
4. **SP4 — Threshold identifiability under noise.** "Small nonzero effect at low dose" vs. "true zero below threshold" are statistically adjacent (digest C4); requires log-spaced low-dose points, seeds, four-family curve comparison (flat/linear/convex/change-point) with AICc + Davies test, TOST equivalence margins pre-registered, pilot-based power analysis. **This is the named overall hard part: at ≤1.4B scale and 1–2B tokens, low-dose effects may sit below the seed-noise floor, making the seed's headline question unanswerable regardless of design.**
5. **SP5 — Forensics coupling & scale transfer.** Re-estimate the dose axis with real detectors (ρ̂ calibration curve; re-fit all curves on ρ̂; report threshold shift and verdict flips — predicted direction: permissive bias). Scale trend via second backbone; from-scratch 160M replication on a 3-point sub-grid as the regime check.

**Critic:** (1) SP2's matched-pair displacement is elegant but reduces the effective rare pool; with rare strata being by definition small, high-ρ × rare-targeted cells may exhaust the stratum — the design needs a stated ceiling on ρ for the rarity factor. (2) SP3's per-stratum MIA needs a holdout: membership inference requires matched non-member sets *per stratum*, which must be reserved before training — easy to forget, fatal to bolt on. (3) SP4 names the risk but the mitigation currently lives only in Stage 6; the sub-problem should own the design lever (unbalanced budget: more seeds/tokens at low doses). (4) The privacy outcome on *canaries* is clean but synthetic; the natural-document extraction outcome will be noisy and confounded by pre-training exposure (Pythia saw the Pile — rare "held-out" docs may not be truly unseen).

**Refined:** SP2 caps the rarity-targeted factor at ρ ≤ 50% (rare-stratum displacement is only meaningful while a residual rare pool survives; beyond that the factor degenerates) and reports the achieved stratum-exhaustion fraction per cell. SP3 adds a pre-registered per-stratum member/non-member split frozen before any training run, with non-member sets drawn from the same provenance-clean slice; and the natural-document extraction analysis conditions on backbone pre-exposure by restricting to documents absent from the backbone's original corpus (post-Pile, pre-2022 sources for the extraction probe [VERIFY availability]) — canaries remain the *primary* privacy endpoint precisely because they are exposure-controlled by construction. SP4 absorbs the unbalanced-design lever as part of its statement: token budget and seed count are dose-dependent by design (denser replication at ρ ≤ 5%), preserving total compute while buying low-dose power — pre-registered so it cannot look like post-hoc fishing.

## Stage 5 — Methods

**5.1 Mixture design: known ratios × rarity strata.**
- *Pools* (M1): human = fixed pre-2022 C4/Pile slice (0% synthetic by provenance), stratified into rarity strata (SP2 definition, pre-registered); synthetic = Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct regenerations/continuations of a *disjoint* slice, rejection-filtered to match length/topic distributions; second generator (Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct) in the scale-up for provenance robustness.
- *Mixer* (M2): fixed total token count; dose grid ρ ∈ {0, 0.5, 1, 2, 5, 10, 20, 35, 50, 75, 100}% (pilot subset {0, 1, 5, 10, 25, 50, 100}%); displacement factor ∈ {proportional, rare-targeted, common-targeted} crossed at ρ ∈ {5, 25, 50}% (capped per SP2); document-granularity mixing, fixed shuffle-seed policy; ground-truth ρ and displacement manifest logged per run.
- *Canary lacing*: per-stratum canaries at duplication {1, 4, 16}, total canary mass < 0.01% of tokens, identical canary sets across arms (so canary rarity is defined relative to the *surviving* human distribution — the treatment).

**5.2 Training protocol (M3).** Frozen-backbone continued pretraining: identical starting checkpoint (Pythia-410M primary; Pythia-1.4B scale arm — both Pile-trained, pre-ChatGPT), identical hyperparameters, fixed budget (1B tokens pilot / 2B scale-up), k seeds per cell (2 pilot / 3 scale-up; denser at low dose per SP4), config hashes + loss curves logged. Controls: ρ=0 CPT control (primary reference), no-CPT frozen backbone (anchors CPT drift), accumulate-regime arm at matched synthetic token count (Gerstgrasser contrast placed on the dose axis), entropy-maximizing selection at ρ=50% (mitigation baseline).

**5.3 Degradation outcome battery (M4).** Capability: clean held-out perplexity (pre-registered primary endpoint; human validation slice + WikiText-103), lm-eval-harness few-shot (LAMBADA, HellaSwag, ARC-E, PIQA). Diversity: distinct-2/3, Self-BLEU, mean pairwise embedding distance over 5k continuations of held-out human prompts. Tail: rare-token recall, tail-decile perplexity, NN differential entropy of generations. Every discontinuous metric paired with a continuous counterpart (threshold verdicts never rest on a discontinuous metric alone).

**5.4 Memorization/privacy outcome battery (M4b — absorbed axis).** (i) Canary exposure (secret-sharer protocol) per stratum × duplication level × dose × displacement arm; (ii) verbatim extraction rate: greedy + sampled continuation of 50-token prefixes from training rare-stratum documents, ≥50-token verbatim match threshold [VERIFY protocol constants against Carlini extraction papers]; (iii) MIA: Min-K%++ AUC per stratum against the frozen per-stratum non-member splits. Predicted signature (falsifiable): rare-targeted displacement at fixed ρ *raises* canary exposure and MIA AUC on surviving rare-stratum data while *lowering* rare-token recall — the privacy-capability scissors that is this paper's headline mechanism figure.

**5.5 Curve fitting & adjudication (M5).** Per metric: four-family fits (flat / linear / convex power-law / segmented change-point), AICc comparison, Davies change-point test, Jonckheere–Terpstra monotone trend, TOST equivalence vs. ρ=0 with pre-registered margin (δ = 0.5 × ρ=0 seed-SD), Holm–Bonferroni across the battery; mixed-effects dose × displacement × scale models for the factorial questions.

**5.6 Forensics coupling (M6).** Black-box detector sweep (DandR-style recoverability + Binoculars-style score) over fixed document samples from every mixture; ρ̂(ρ) calibration curve; re-fit all M5 models on ρ̂; report threshold shift Δρ* and verdict flips.

**Critic:** (1) The full crossed design (11 doses × 3 displacement × 2 generators × 2 backbones × 3 seeds) is combinatorially impossible — the method must state the actual sparse design matrix, not imply a full factorial. (2) The privacy battery on 410M models may be too *weak* to show extraction at realistic duplication (memorization scales with model size); if canary exposure at ρ=0 is already floor-level, the whole absorbed axis is unmeasurable at this scale — this needs a pilot gate of its own, not discovery at analysis time. (3) The "privacy-capability scissors" prediction is compelling but rests on the displacement factor's orthogonality (SP2's hard part); one manipulation-check analysis (verify realized rarity profiles of surviving corpora per arm) must be reported before any scissors claim. (4) Entropy-maximizing mitigation baseline at a single dose point is decorative; either give it a dose sub-grid or cut it.

**Refined:** Explicit sparse design: (A) full dose grid at proportional displacement, primary backbone, generator-1: 11 × 3 seeds = 33 runs (pilot runs 7 × 2 = 14 of these); (B) displacement factor: {rare, common} × ρ ∈ {5, 25, 50}% × 3 seeds = 18 runs; (C) scale arm: 1.4B backbone, proportional, ρ ∈ {0, 5, 25, 50, 100}% × 2 seeds = 10 runs; (D) generator-2 arm: ρ ∈ {5, 25, 50}% × 2 seeds = 6 runs; (E) controls (no-CPT, accumulate ×3 doses, ρ=0 extra seeds) ≈ 8 runs. Total ≈ 75 runs (61 at 410M, 10 at 1.4B, plus 160M from-scratch 3-point check ≈ 6 cheap runs). The mitigation baseline is cut from the ICLR-window design and deferred to the journal/extended version. Privacy-measurability gate added to the pilot (G2, Stage 6): canary exposure at duplication-16 in the ρ=0 arm must clear the detection floor at 410M; if not, the privacy axis moves wholly to canaries at higher duplication {16, 64} and the natural-document extraction claim is dropped (graceful degradation of the absorbed axis, pre-declared). The manipulation check (realized rarity histograms per arm) is a required figure preceding any scissors result.
