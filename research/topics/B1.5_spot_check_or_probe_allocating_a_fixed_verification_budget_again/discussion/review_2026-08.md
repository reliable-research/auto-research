### Overall novelty stance

**🟢 overall, earned on the estimand/instrument rows (claims 2, 3, 5), with claim 1
repositioned.** The composition-as-defender-variable formulation survives a cited-first
comparison and a 2026-08 temporal sweep; no method twin found. The one genuine defect:
the draft treated "a single predictable channel degrades under an aware attacker" as its
own open hypothesis, when Terekhov et al. (arXiv:2510.09462) had already demonstrated it
empirically for LM-monitor-based control protocols in **October 2025 — nine months before
this draft was assembled**. That is a concede-and-claim repositioning (RQ4's contribution
is the *matched-cost portfolio-vs-single-channel degradation comparison*, not the
existence of the degradation), not a kill.

**Honest one-sentence delta:** relative to the AI-control line (AI Control, ControlTax,
Ctrl-Z, Games for AI Control, adaptive attacks/attack selection), this paper's real
contribution is making the *composition* of a fixed verification budget over structurally
distinct executable check flavors a measurable defender variable — with a shared per-call
cost meter, a simplex-swept miss-rate frontier, and a structure-vs-novelty attribution
control — where all prior work optimizes the *size or threshold of one homogeneous channel*.

**Decay/concurrency annotation (never alters stances above):** the AI-control cluster is
publishing fast (≥6 directly adjacent papers Oct-2025→Aug-2026: 2510.09462, 2602.04930,
2602.10153, 2606.06529, 2607.06596, 2608.08709). Calibration-Family Overfit
(2607.06596, Jul 2026) is inside the concurrency window and independently motivates
check-source heterogeneity (monitors overfit to lineages). At current velocity the
composition gap's half-life is short; the draft is Tier-3-held in `portfolio_2026-08.md`,
which compounds scoop risk the longer it waits for B1.2-style substrate.

## 2. Feasibility verdict (se-paper-reviewer, R1 focus)

**FEASIBLE at the pre-registered scale, with two spec defects fixed in this pass.**

- **Compute/data budget:** pilot ≈ 100 tasks × 3 families × 5 policies × 4 budgets × 3
  seeds ≈ 18k episodes, ≤8 sandboxed calls each → ~10⁵ cheap executions + 10³–10⁴ LLM
  attack-generation calls (reused across policies/budgets); scale-up ≈ 4–6×. Single
  multi-core CPU server + low-thousands-USD API class. No annotation labor (ground truth
  by construction). Realistic. (These planning numbers lived only in `digest.md` N6; a
  condensed resource-plan sentence is now in the draft, labeled as an estimate.)
- **Power arithmetic:** ≥600 shortcut episodes/cell (pilot) sized for a 10 pp miss-rate
  difference at ~50% base rate, power >0.9, with DEFF/ICC cluster adjustment estimated
  from pilot data and the thin-cell INCONCLUSIVE rule. Sound and pre-registered.
- **Kill-test exists:** walking-skeleton gate (flavors must separate on scripted attacks),
  instrument positive control (≥90% detection of own attack class at exhaustive budget),
  and RQ3's oracle-aware manipulation check (≥10 pp degradation floor) — three genuine
  early-abort points. Good.
- **Assumption load:** moderate and disclosed (implanted-shortcut fidelity; aware-attacker
  information-set modeling choice). Both carried in Threats to Validity.
