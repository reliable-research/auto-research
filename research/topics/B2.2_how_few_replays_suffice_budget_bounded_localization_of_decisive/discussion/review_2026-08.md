## 3. Per-claim justifications (compressed)

- **Claim 1.** Framing-strip: remove "identification and direct accounting" → the surviving
  object is a concrete cost unit ((n−k+1) resumed LLM calls per probe) plus a rank-stability
  protocol across three cost axes. Nothing in the cited set or the 2026 sweep prices
  localization probes positionally. CAR's "budget-bounded" is an estimator-internal sample
  count (confirmed against its abstract and the 2026-07-04 full-PDF read logged in
  `pipeline.md`). Novel, load-bearing. Caveat: the draft then violates its own unit in the
  RQ7 budget grid — see Finding 2 below; the claim is novel but currently self-inconsistent.
- **Claim 2.** No searched neighbor measures a monotonicity-violation rate of a replay
  predicate. ddmin is the conceptual ancestor and the draft already concedes it correctly.
- **Claim 3.** The crossover is a real, unclaimed measurement, but its scoring is contingent
  (p-axis support pre-condition), so it cannot carry the paper; 🟡.
- **Claim 4.** The word "first" survives the strip *for the estimand as stated* (cost-accuracy
  curves of index-structured replay search at matched position-dependent budgets): Who&When
  Pro benchmarks *who attributes well*, not *how few replays suffice*; CHIEF/HPFA/MARS reduce
  cost but report single operating points, not budget curves. However, the draft's broader
  framing sentence — "every existing replay-based attribution system spends its replay budget
  exhaustively, one step at a time" — is **no longer true** at t_audit (CHIEF prunes with
  oracle-guided backtracking; HPFA names exhaustive counterfactual testing as its motivating
  enemy; MARS budget-trims rollouts). The claim is novel; the framing needed narrowing
  (fixed in this pass).
- **Claim 5.** Concede: Who&When Pro built the large injected-fault golden-label corpus
  first (2026-07-10). Claim: their pipeline verifies the successful prefix, not decisiveness
  (no both-directions outcome-flip round-trip, no sham-injection control, no layer balance,
  and their trajectories are not packaged for per-checkpoint suffix-replay search). The
  corpus row cannot be presented as an unqualified contribution any more; as machinery with
  the stated delta it stands.

## 4. Overall novelty verdict

**🟢 overall, carried by the estimand rows (Claims 1, 2, 4), not by the corpus row.**

accuracy-vs-replay-budget characterization of index-structured replay search — billed in
position-dependent replay units and stratified by a measured monotonicity-violation rate —

## 5. Feasibility verdict (se-paper-reviewer pass)

**FEASIBLE — conditional on Finding 2's budget-axis repair (applied in this pass).**

- **Compute/data budget:** pilot ≈10⁴ LLM calls, <$250, <1 day (walking skeleton, Step 9);
  scale-up ≈5×10⁵ calls, ~$1–3k at small-model pricing; zero training; GPU only if
  self-hosting open weights. These orders of magnitude check out against the design (300
  trajectories × mean length 30 × exhaustive-scan domination of the sweep).
- **Annotation budget:** ~20–30 annotator-hours (10% injected-fault spot-check + A8's
  κ≥0.7 two-rater natural-slice relabeling gate). Realistic.
- **Power arithmetic (verified):** pooled n=300 McNemar, ~20% discordance, 10-pt paired
  difference → 60 discordant pairs, 45/15 expected split — power >80% as claimed
  (conservative; closer to 95%). Per-layer n≈90–100 at α=0.017 → the draft's own ~65–70%
  figure is about right, and its ≥2-of-adequately-powered-layers aggregation rule correctly
  avoids the conjunctive-power trap (0.65³≈0.27).
- **Assumption load:** A1–A8 all carry detection + gate + consequence; the two heaviest
  (A2 harness determinism ≥95%; A3 monotonicity) have a pre-pilot gate and a measured
  covariate respectively. The RQ5 noise-support risk and the natural-slice replayability
  risk are both pre-declared INCONCLUSIVE/F-G2 paths, not hidden.
- **Minimum-viable kill test:** exists and is genuinely cheap — the Step 9 walking skeleton
  (20 ALFWorld trajectories, 3 arms + 2 smoke arms, deterministic metrics only) kills the
  project for <$250 if the reproduction gate or the cost-accuracy separation fails.
- **Feasibility improvements since drafting:** Who&When Pro (12,326 injected trajectories,
  golden labels) and StateMAS (1,310 replayable failure trajectories) are candidate
  substrates that could cut corpus-construction cost materially; flagged as an option, not
  spliced into the registered design.

## 7. Numbered findings

1. **[major] Who&When Pro unpositioned (novelty/staleness).** arXiv 2607.09996 (2026-07-10),
   the recorded B2.1 partial-scoop, is an instrument-twin of this paper's M2 corpus module:
   12,326 injected-failure trajectories with golden labels via exact-prefix-replay-then-inject,
   across 26 benchmarks. It does **not** touch the replay-budget search estimand (verified
   at abstract level), but a draft whose abstract leads with "a replay-verified
   fault-injected trajectory corpus" cannot ship without conceding it. **Fix applied:**
   concede-and-claim in Related Work + Intro; corpus delta stated operationally
   (bidirectional decisiveness round-trip, layer balance, per-checkpoint replayability);
   flagged as candidate substrate.
2. **[major] Budget-axis incoherence (internal consistency/feasibility).** RQ7's
   confirmatory budget "one-quarter the trajectory length" (B=n/4 replay units) and the
   descriptive grid B∈{2,…,64} replay units are probe-count-scaled while the paper's own
   registered billing is position-dependent: at mean n≈30, B≈8 resumed calls cannot fund
   one mid-trajectory probe (cost n/2+1≈16), and bisection's full-run expected cost
   (≈(n/2)·log₂n≈75 units, r=1) exceeds the grid maximum of 64 — the registered comparison
   point is degenerate for every structured strategy. This silently re-imports the exact
   probe-count mispricing RQ1 exists to expose. **Fix applied:** budget axis restated as
   fractions of the exhaustive scan's replay-unit cost C_exh=n(n+1)/2 (grid
   B/C_exh∈{1/64,…,1/2,1}; confirmatory point 1/4·C_exh — which preserves the evident
   intent, since n/4 uniform probes cost ≈n²/8≈C_exh/4), with a dated registration-amendment
   note; `paper.md` Step 6 (H2) requires the matching amendment before Phase B (logged in
   `pipeline.md` — `paper.md` itself was not edited in this pass).
3. **[moderate] 2026 efficiency-turn literature absent.** CHIEF (2602.23701), FALAT
   (2606.00765), MARS/StateMAS (2607.29055), HPFA (2608.02026), StepFinder (2606.03467) all
   explicitly motivate by attribution/replay cost and prune or restructure the search;
   the abstract/intro's "every existing replay-based attribution system spends its replay
   budget exhaustively" was over-broad. **Fix applied:** new Related Work paragraph +
   narrowed abstract/intro claims (the surviving true claim: none characterizes
   accuracy-vs-budget at matched replay-unit spend).
4. **[moderate] Who&When's binary-search variant unacknowledged.** Who&When's own judge
   family includes a binary-search-over-log-context strategy; a reviewer will pattern-match
   "binary search attribution already exists." **Fix applied:** concede-and-distinguish
   clause (theirs bisects the transcript shown to a judge, no re-execution; ours bisects
   replay outcomes) in Related Work + Baselines parenthetical.
5. **[moderate] Exhaustive arm's vote count unspecified.** H1's ≤25% cost-ratio verdict
   flips depending on whether the exhaustive scan is billed at r=1 (ratio ≈ r·log₂n/n ≈ 48%
   at r=3, n=30 → H1 arithmetically unpassable) or at the same r as bisection (≈16%).
   **Fix applied:** RQ2 setup now states both arms are billed at the same per-probe vote
   count (chosen per RQ4), so the ratio isolates search structure.
6. **[moderate] Pre-draft misses: AgentDebug/AgentErrorBench (2509.25370) and FAMAS
   (2509.13782).** AgentErrorBench annotates failure trajectories on ALFWorld/GAIA/WebShop —
   the paper's exact planned environments — and AgentDebug does counterfactual root-cause
   isolation; FAMAS attributes via repeated-execution spectrum analysis (replay-based,
   un-budgeted). **Fix applied:** both cited and positioned.
7. **[minor] Scheduled vs realized cost.** Billing (n−k+1) assumes the re-sampled suffix
   re-executes in exactly n−k+1 steps; realized call counts can differ (early termination,
   different suffix path). **Fix applied:** one clarifying sentence in the accounting
   method (scheduled cost; dollar axis captures realized deviations).
8. **[minor] Group-testing intervention semantics.** "Replace the group's steps with
   recorded-correct actions" is under-specified when the suffix has diverged from the
   source successful trajectory (state consistency of transplanted actions). Left as a
   logged spec-level to-do for Phase B; no draft change (would require design decisions the
   registration should make).
9. **[minor] Single-decisive-step construct.** MP-Bench (2603.25001) argues MAS failures
   often admit multiple valid attributions — directly strengthens threat t1/A4. **Fix
   applied:** cited in one sentence alongside the existing DoVer-based caveat in Conclusion
   remains unchanged (the limitation was already stated); citation added in Related Work.
10. **[minor] refs.bib entries lack author fields** (pre-existing Phase G Finding 6; still
    open — jsonl schema has no author field). Not fixed here; new entries follow the same
    house style for consistency.

## 8. Anticipated reviewer attacks (defensive tail)

1. *"Who&When Pro already built a 12k injected-failure corpus with golden labels — what's
   left?"* (likelihood: high) — Response: their pipeline certifies the prefix, not
   decisiveness (no both-directions flip round-trip, no sham-injection control), carries no
   per-checkpoint replay packaging, and answers *who attributes well*, not *how few replays
   suffice*; our corpus is the instrument the budget-curve estimand requires. Answerable
   from the revised draft.
2. *"Binary search over a trajectory is trivial/already in Who&When."* (high) — Response:
   Who&When's variant bisects the log context an LLM judge reads — no re-execution, no
   causal signal; we bisect a replay oracle and, critically, measure where its monotone
   premise fails (the violation rate), which is the actual contribution. Answerable.
3. *"CHIEF/HPFA already make counterfactual attribution efficient."* (medium) — Response:
   they report single cost operating points for one method; none yields a cost-accuracy
   curve across strategies at matched position-dependent budgets, which is the provisioning
   question a team actually faces. Answerable.
4. *"Your confirmatory budget point is arbitrary."* (medium) — Response: B=C_exh/4 is the
   unit-coherent restatement of the registered n/4-probe intent, pre-registered before any
   run; the full normalized grid is reported descriptively. Answerable post-amendment.
5. *"Injected single faults ≠ real failures"* (medium) — already conceded (A4/A6, t1,
   natural-failure slice, F-G2); MP-Bench now cited as the sharpened version of this attack.
