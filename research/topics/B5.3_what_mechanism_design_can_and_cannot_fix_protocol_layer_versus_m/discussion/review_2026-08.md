## 3. Feasibility findings (se-paper-reviewer lens)

- **[moderate] F1 — pilot cannot reach either verdict under the pre-registered rule; invariance verdict marginal even at scale-up.** The verdict rule (Methodology §Category-Indexed Scenario Taxonomy) requires, for protocol-invariant, MSR ≥ 10% at P3 with the 95% CI excluding 5%. At pilot n = 30/cell, p̂ = 0.10 gives a Wilson 95% CI of ≈ [0.035, 0.26] — it cannot exclude 5%, so *no category can be classified invariant at pilot scale by construction* (and the ≤5% fixable bound is similarly undecidable at p̂ = 1/30, CI ≈ [0.006, 0.17]). At scale-up n = 100, p̂ = 0.10 → CI ≈ [0.055, 0.174]: the bound is excluded only just, i.e., ~50% power if true MSR is exactly 10%. The draft nowhere said verdicts are scale-up-only, and RQ1's setup applied "the per-category verdict rule ... to every cell" including pilot cells. Fix: pilot re-scoped as go/no-go + instrumentation check; invariance contrasts pre-registered at n ≥ 200 on strictest-rung cells. **Applied to draft (md+tex).**
- **[moderate] F2 — the "~3,600 negotiations" figure did not decompose from the stated factors, and no token/cost budget appeared in the draft.** 2 families × 5 categories × 4 rungs × 30 trials = 1,200; × 2 attacker modes = 2,400 ≠ 3,600; the missing ~1,200 are the scripted truthful-play episodes that estimate S^tru (never stated). The digest (N6) carries a budget (~90k messages, ~10⁸ tokens, mid-hundreds USD pilot / low-thousands scale-up) that the draft dropped entirely — a pre-registration must be budget-auditable. Fix: explicit "Scale, power, and verdict timing" paragraph with the decomposition and order-of-magnitude budget. **Applied to draft (md+tex).**
- **[minor] F3 — RQ6's 0.10-absolute-reduction gate is marginally powered under Holm.** Detecting 0.15 → 0.05 at n = 100/arm is ≈ 65–70% power at uncorrected α = 0.05, less after Holm across (invariant categories × 2 interventions). Not revised (minor; the F1 fix's n ≥ 200 strictest-rung commitment incidentally covers the undefended arm; flag for the eventual run).
- **[minor] F4 — category (e) collusive signaling is underspecified in the draft.** The digest's M1 makes it a 3-agent variant; the draft's formal setup fixes bilateral trials and never says how (e) is instantiated. Not revised (minor; belongs in `experiment_design.md`'s next spec pass).
- **Kill-tests present and adequate:** RQ5's κ ≥ 0.7 gate is a genuine go/no-go; RQ1's falsification condition covers both degenerate splits; the refusal-rate confound has the scripted-attacker floor. No finding.
