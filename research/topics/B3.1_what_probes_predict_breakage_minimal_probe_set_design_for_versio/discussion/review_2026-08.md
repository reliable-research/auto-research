## 2. Feasibility verdict (se-paper-reviewer scope)

**Verdict: feasible as a pilot; conditionally feasible at the confirmatory scale — the |P| ≥ 60 floor is the pinch point, and the draft under-stated its cost.**

- **Compute/data arithmetic.** Pilot (3–5 pairs, ~500 probes, ~10 tasks, ≥3 repeats) is ~20–50k generations — cheap, runnable on 1–2 GPUs, and correctly exercises the riskiest components (P0 placebo, noise floor, 3-family collinearity path) per `design_review.md`. The confirmatory tier is another matter: 60 pairs × (~1,500 probe items + ~4,000 downstream inputs) × 2 versions × ≥3 repeats ≈ **~2M generations**, an order of magnitude above the ~660k the earlier feasibility note budgeted for ~20 endpoints. Feasible on an open-weight backbone with multi-GPU weeks + low-thousands-USD API stratum, but the draft nowhere restated the budget after raising the floor from 20→60. [fixed: budget line added]
- **Effective sample size.** Version pairs within one lineage (e.g., Qwen1.5→2→2.5 at multiple sizes) share update events; treating all 60 as independent overstates power. The Fisher-z arithmetic is honest per-pair but the bootstrap/CV must group by lineage and the power policy should count lineage-grouped events. [fixed: lineage-grouping note added to RQ1]
- **Annotation budget.** κ ≥ 0.7 two-rater plan exists for the breakage classifier (per `paper.md` A3/R2); probe-labeler validity honestly deferred as a stated limitation. Adequate for a spec.
- **Minimum-viable kill-test.** Present and well-formed: the pilot plus gates (P0 null, noise-floor exceedance for ≥1 family, mini-κ) kills the study cheaply if the instrument is broken. This is the strongest part of the design.
- **Assumption load.** Moderate and explicit (A1–A7 in `paper.md` with gates/consequences). The one assumption the draft could not defend — that its named dated-API snapshot examples are reachable — is a staleness defect, below.

## 3. Staleness verdict (as of 2026-08-15)

**Verdict: core estimand still open (not stale as an idea), but the draft was stale in three concrete places — two fixed as [major]/[moderate] revisions.**

1. **Reference set predates the 2026 drift cluster.** The draft's entire comparison set is the local OpenReview corpus (harvested ≤2026-07); it cites none of: Chen et al. 2307.09009 (the canonical longitudinal-drift evidence — the field's standard motivation citation), the black-box change-detection line (2410.19406, 2410.20247, 2504.12335, 2506.06975, 2512.03816, 2504.04715), Behavioral Fingerprints 2603.19022, Test Before You Deploy 2604.27789, DriftBench 2605.10990, or LLM Output Drift 2511.07585. Its "nearest existing work" sentence names BiasSimBSM, which is no longer the nearest neighbor. All 11 verified real on 2026-08-15. [major → fixed]
2. **Dead dataset exemplars.** The Datasets paragraph names gpt-3.5-turbo-0301/0613/1106 and gpt-4-0314/0613 as the dated-API stratum — all retired by mid-2024, i.e. they cannot pass the draft's own reachability gate in 2026. [moderate → fixed]
3. **Classical citations "pending a dedicated harvest."** Yoo & Harrold 2012, Rothermel & Harrold 1997, Gama et al. 2014 are canonical and their DOIs resolve; the placeholder disclaimer was unnecessary staleness. [minor → fixed]

**Circularity disposition (the fleet-review flag).** The flag — "probes are validated against the same drift events they are meant to predict" — was **partially unmitigated** in the pre-revision draft and is treated as [major] (F2):
- *Already mitigated pre-revision:* the P0 label-shuffle placebo (pipeline-leak guard), the within-version noise floor (drift ≠ decoding noise), the attrition-gated corpus (survivorship guard), and probe items being disjoint from downstream inputs *in intent* (never stated).
- *Not mitigated pre-revision:* (a) the headline RQ1/RQ2 verdicts were scored on an **in-sample** joint fit over the same version pairs used to select S*, with the temporal forward check demoted to "reported, not a gate" — so the prospective-use claim rested on in-sample evidence from the very drift events it claims to predict; (b) **no probe/task tautology guard**: the format-preference probes (IFEval/JSON-schema items) and the format-sensitive downstream tasks (extraction-to-JSON) are structurally near-identical, so "format probes predict format breakage" would be self-prediction, not transfer — `discussion.md` (Stage 4/5 refined) explicitly demands diagonal-cell exclusion and held-out evaluation, and the canonical draft predates both.

## 4. Numbered findings

- **F1 [major] — Stale nearest-neighbor set / over-broad gap claim.** Abstract + Intro + Related Work claim no prior work compares behavior "across a version pair"; the verified change-detection line does exactly that (black-box, version pairs), just without a breakage target. Nearest-neighbor naming (BiasSimBSM) superseded by Behavioral Fingerprints 2603.19022 and Test Before You Deploy 2604.27789. **Fix applied:** concede-and-claim rewrite of the Intro nearest-work paragraph and Abstract gap sentence; new Related Work paragraph "Cross-version change detection and update governance" citing all 11 verified works; claim narrowed to the breakage-prediction estimand.
- **F2 [major] — Circular validation substrate (fleet flag), two concrete holes.** (a) Predictive-power claims scored in-sample on the fitting pairs; (b) no axis-matching/tautology guard between probe families and downstream tasks. **Fix applied:** π_f redefined as out-of-sample (grouped leave-one-pair-out, lineage-grouped) in the Problem Definition; new Methodology subsection "Held-Out-Pair Evaluation and Axis-Matching Guard" (LOPO headline scoring; same-axis diagonal cells reported but excluded from headline claims; probe/downstream item-source disjointness stated as a construction constraint); temporal forward check elevated: a large in-sample-vs-forward gap now *voids the prospective-use claim* rather than being disclosed only; Conclusion limitation added.
- **F3 [moderate] — Dead dated-API snapshot exemplars** (gpt-3.5-turbo-0301 etc., retired 2024). **Fix applied:** replaced with "dated snapshots still being served at study start," with retired snapshot ids named as examples of the *attrition risk class* and a note that retired snapshots can enter only via previously released cached-output corpora, which do not satisfy the reachability gate.
- **F4 [moderate] — Confirmatory-tier budget and effective-n not restated after the |P|≥60 correction.** Power floor raised 20→60 pairs without re-costing (~2M generations) and without acknowledging lineage non-independence. **Fix applied:** budget sentence added to Experimental Setup; lineage-grouped bootstrap/CV and event-counting note added to the RQ1 power gate.
- **F5 [minor] — Classical concession lacked verifiable citations.** **Fix applied:** Yoo & Harrold 2012, Rothermel & Harrold 1997, Gama et al. 2014 added (DOIs resolved); "pending harvest" disclaimer removed.
- **F6 [minor] — Cross-generation successions ≠ in-place version updates.** Llama-2→Llama-3-style pairs are full retrains; generalizing from them to API version bumps is an assumption. Noted in the revised limitations sentence; per-stratum reporting already covers the analysis side. No further action needed at spec stage.
- **F7 [minor, logged only] — Portfolio positioning not in the draft.** The portfolio (2026-08) fixes B3.1's strongest form as merged into B3.3/B3.4 with explicit triggers (carried by the *other* lineage's Section 7 module contract). The canonical draft is silent on this. Left as-is: positioning is a portfolio artifact, not paper prose; recorded here so the merge decision is not forgotten at write-up.
