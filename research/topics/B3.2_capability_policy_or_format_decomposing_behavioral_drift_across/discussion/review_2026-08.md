## 3. Overall novelty stance

**🟢 Novel, carried by Claims 1+3 (the calibration-gated black-box attribution protocol and the cross-vendor estimand), with one 🟡 concession required on Claim 2 and a fast-shrinking half-life.** As of 2026-08-15 no published work performs a black-box capability/policy/format decomposition of LLM version-pair drift. The honest one-sentence delta: *relative to the 2026 update-governance and change-detection cluster, which tests updates BY category (2604.27789), detects THAT a model changed (2602.11083), or detects WHICH items reliably changed (2604.27405), this paper attributes WHY — apportioning a version pair's drift across three causes with a calibration gate that must pass before any attribution is made.* Supporting evidence: 2604.27789 explicitly lists "how to detect and explain model drift" under limited provider transparency as an **open challenge** — the strongest external confirmation the slot is unoccupied, and simultaneously the reviewer-findable neighbor the draft (pre-revision) did not cite.

**Temporal check / decay risk:** the nearest-neighbor set is dense and recent (Feb–Jun 2026: 2602.11083, 2604.27405, 2604.27789, 2606.05396, 2606.15474). At this publication velocity the delta's half-life is short — months, not years. Concurrent-work risk HIGH: any submission should expect to treat further 2026-H2 entries as concurrent under venue rules. This is a reason to execute the pilot quickly, not to reframe.

## 4. Staleness verdict (incl. circularity disposition)

**Verdict: methodology current; positioning stale by ~6 months (fixed in revision); fleet-flagged circularity PARTIALLY mitigated in this draft, residual REAL and previously unaddressed (fixed in revision).**

- The statistical machinery (mixed-effects noise floor, permutation tests, Holm–Bonferroni, pre-registered power/INCONCLUSIVE gates) is current 2026 practice. Not stale.
- Related Work was frozen at ~2025-12 (newest cited works: 2512.24063, 2603.03323) and claimed ChatGPTBehavior (2023) as the only close neighbor — false as of 2026-08 (see F1).
- **Circularity disposition.** The fleet flag ("decomposition validated on the same drift events used to define its categories") described the sibling autodiscussion draft's simulator substrate exactly (its validation data was generated by the method's own model — see `autodiscussion_draft_review.md` §M1). The current canonical draft **does not have that form of circularity**: calibration runs on constructed control pairs (separate interventions, not the audited drift events), and a null-pair placebo provides a genuine negative control. **However, a residual, weaker circularity survived unmitigated:** (a) the control pairs are *constructed under the same three-axis taxonomy they validate* — blind recovery shows the instrument detects taxonomy-aligned single-axis interventions, not that real drift is spanned by the taxonomy; (b) all controls are **single-axis**, while real pairs are mixtures — recovery on pure controls over-states reliability for the mixed regime the paper's headline claims live in; (c) calibration happens on small open-weight models but licenses claims about commercial-scale serving stacks — an untested **transfer assumption** (the earlier `discussion.md` design had a mixed-dose pair set and an A5 leakage-vs-scale ladder for exactly these two gaps; neither survived into `paper_draft.md`). Treated as [major] F2 and fixed in revision.

## 5. Feasibility verdict (se-paper-reviewer)

**FEASIBLE at the stated budget class, with two design defects to fix (F3, F4) and one external dependency.**

- **Compute/data/annotation arithmetic** (from digest N6, checked): ~3,000 probe items × 2 versions × 5 samples ≈ 30k calls/pair; ~6 real + ~12 control pairs ≈ 450–550k generations; ≈$2–5k API + ~250–350 GPU-h (7–8B controls + inference) + ~120k cheap judge calls + 30–40 annotator-hours for judge validation (600-item κ check). 1–2 researchers, 2–3 months. Internally consistent; no hidden order-of-magnitude gap (unlike the rejected sibling draft's 5-lineage bridge panel).
- **Power arithmetic:** RQ2's 8/9 binomial gate vs 1/3 chance is fine (p≈0.0009 at 9/9, ≈0.008 at 8/9). RQ5's 4-of-6 / 3-of-6 counting rules are protected by the A8/PG-7 power carve-out. **RQ6 as written is arithmetically broken at the minimum design** (F3): with 3 vendors as ranking units over 3 axes, the exact permutation null gives P(W=1)=1/36≈0.028 and *every* W<1 has p>0.05 — so the pre-registered "W ≥ 0.60 with p<0.05" is unsatisfiable except at perfect agreement. Fixable on paper (rank at pair level, m≥6: χ²≈m(n−1)W=7.2 at W=0.60 → p≈0.027).
- **Assumption load:** moderate and mostly instrumented (A-ledger, PG gates, null-pair placebo, blended-delta baseline). The one previously *uninstrumented* assumption is calibration transfer (F2c).
- **Minimum-viable kill-test:** exists and is cheap — run one format-only control pair + the null-pair placebo + the blended-delta baseline on a single open-weight model (tens of GPU-hours). RQ1 orthogonality and the misattribution demonstration are falsifiable before any API spend.
- **External dependency:** ≥6 adjacent, *simultaneously served* snapshot pairs across ≥3 vendors during one window. Realistic under 2026 snapshot-pinning practice but not guaranteed; the pair inventory must be frozen at pre-registration, with open-weight pairs as guaranteed anchors (the draft already plans this).
