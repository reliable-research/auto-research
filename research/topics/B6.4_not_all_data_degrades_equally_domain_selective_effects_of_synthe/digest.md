- **[MixRatio, OpenReview:9RdhTvYbX0]** — "Preventing Model Collapse Under
  Overparametrization: Optimal Mixing Ratios for Interpolation Learning and Ridge
  Regression." Approach: models recursive training as fitting each generation on a weighted
  mix of fresh real and previous-model synthetic labels; uses random matrix theory in the
  overparameterized regime to derive closed-form long-run risk as a function of the
  real–synthetic mixing weight, and analytically identifies the mixing ratio that prevents
  collapse. `paper_url`: https://openreview.net/forum?id=9RdhTvYbX0 [GROUNDED:corpus]
- **[IterStability, OpenReview:JORAfH2xFd]** — "On the Stability of Iterative Retraining of
  Generative Models on Their Own Data." Approach: formalizes retraining on mixed
  real/synthetic data as a fixed-point operator in parameter space and derives contraction
  conditions on the real/synthetic mixing parameter under which iterative retraining is
  locally stable rather than collapsing; motivated by evidence that "even partial synthetic
  contamination can harm downstream performance."
  `paper_url`: https://openreview.net/forum?id=JORAfH2xFd [GROUNDED:corpus]
- **[CrossModelRecursion, OpenReview:JEU4PBaX85]** — "What happens when generative AI models
  train recursively on each others' outputs?" Approach: models multi-entity recursive
  training through a shared synthetic-data channel (public real data + private real data +
  generation-indexed synthetic pools from many models) with an accumulate-and-subsample
  update rule, deriving closed-form bias–variance dynamics; documents that single-stream
  recursion removes tail events and degrades rare-event coverage.
  `paper_url`: https://openreview.net/forum?id=JEU4PBaX85 [GROUNDED:corpus]
- **[EntropyCollapse, OpenReview:6xCcjYa97j]** — "A Closer Look at Model Collapse: From a
  Generalization-to-Memorization Perspective." Approach: reinterprets collapse under
  recursive synthetic training as a generalization-to-memorization transition driven by
  declining dataset entropy; measures nearest-neighbor generalization and differential
  entropy across self-consuming iterations, and proposes entropy-maximizing data selection
  to slow degradation. `paper_url`: https://openreview.net/forum?id=6xCcjYa97j
  [GROUNDED:corpus]
- **[SpendWisely, OpenReview:aqpHTPC63N]** — "Spend Wisely: Maximizing Post-Training Gains
  in Iterative Synthetic Data Bootstrapping." Approach: formalizes generate–filter–fine-tune
  synthetic-data loops as iterative stochastic optimization with per-iteration synthetic
  sample sizes n_t; analyzes finite-sample gradient noise and proves that exponentially
  growing data schedules are optimal for allocating a fixed synthetic-data budget across
  iterations. `paper_url`: https://openreview.net/forum?id=aqpHTPC63N [GROUNDED:corpus]
- **[DoReMi, OpenReview:lXuByUeHhd]** — "DoReMi: Optimizing Data Mixtures Speeds Up Language
  Model Pretraining." Approach: trains a small proxy model with a Group-DRO minimax objective
  over domain excess losses to derive domain mixture weights, then transfers the averaged
  weights to large-scale pretraining via resampling — establishing that per-domain data
  composition materially shifts training efficiency and downstream performance.
  `paper_url`: https://openreview.net/forum?id=lXuByUeHhd [GROUNDED:corpus]
- **[RegMix, OpenReview:5BjQOUXq7i]** — "RegMix: Data Mixture as Regression for Language
  Model Pre-training." Approach: trains many small proxy models on Dirichlet-sampled domain
  mixtures for a short duration, fits a regression from mixture proportions to validation
  performance, and exploits rank-invariance of mixture quality across scales to pick
  mixtures for much larger models cheaply.
  `paper_url`: https://openreview.net/forum?id=5BjQOUXq7i [GROUNDED:corpus]
- **[SynthWeighting, OpenReview:oI5tZaWkF9]** — "Not All LLM-Generated Data Are Equal:

  derived from a small real dataset, so training on synthetic data approximates training on
  the real distribution — correcting distribution mismatch without hard filtering or
  bilevel meta-learning. `paper_url`: https://openreview.net/forum?id=oI5tZaWkF9

- **[TALR, OpenReview:ru93xpQFi1]** — "SFT Doesn't Always Hurt General Capabilities:

- **[Shumailov et al., 2024]** — "AI models collapse when trained on recursively generated

## N6 Methodology

**Overview** [HYPOTHESIS]: "The method is a controlled continued-pretraining matrix
experiment: a domain-partitioned clean corpus (code / prose / math / dialogue shards from
public per-domain sources) is contaminated one domain at a time by replacing a fixed
fraction of that shard's tokens with in-domain synthetic text, at a sweep of ratios, while
total token count, mixture proportions, base model, and training schedule are held fixed;
downstream capability is measured per domain with domain-native benchmarks plus per-domain
held-out validation loss, yielding a domain × ratio matrix of normalized capability deltas
from which per-domain degradation slopes and a bootstrap-ranked vulnerability ordering are
derived, and the ordering is replicated across two contamination generators.
**Staged plan**: *Stage 1 (pilot — the seed's minimal experiment)*: one base model
(TinyLlama-1.1B-class), one generator, 4 domains × ratios {0, 25, 100}% × 3 seeds on
~0.5B-token continued pretraining per run — enough to detect whether domain × ratio
interaction exists at all and to size effects for power analysis. *Stage 2 (scale-up)*:
full ratio grid {0, 10, 25, 50, 100}%, a second generator family (for C4), a second model
scale (~3B) on the two extreme ratios (for C3's scale interaction), and the
degeneracy-matched control for C1."

**Key components** (all [HYPOTHESIS]; each states what is built/measured, with what
instrument, at what granularity):

- **M1** — *Domain-partitioned clean corpus builder*: four ~2.5B-token shards (pilot:
  ~0.5B each) sampled from public per-domain sources — code: The Stack (permissive-license
  Python/Java subset); math: OpenWebMath; prose: FineWeb-Edu web text; dialogue: OASST1 +
  UltraChat conversations — MinHash-deduplicated within and across shards, with a 1%
  held-out split per shard reserved for per-domain validation loss. Granularity: documents,
  tracked with per-document domain labels and token counts.
- **M2** — *In-domain synthetic contamination injector*: a fixed instruction-tuned generator
  (e.g., an 8B-class open-weights LLM) produces synthetic documents per domain by
  continuation/paraphrase prompts conditioned on real-document prefixes from the same shard,
  with rejection of near-duplicates (MinHash against the real shard) and length matched to
  the replaced documents' token-length distribution (±10%). Instrumented with per-document
  logs of generator perplexity and type-token ratio so that C1's degeneracy covariates are
  measured, not assumed.
- **M3** — *Contamination-ratio sweep with token accounting*: for each (domain d, ratio r)
  cell, exactly r% of shard d's tokens are replaced by M2's synthetic documents; all other
  shards and the global mixture proportions are byte-identical across cells; one shared 0%
  clean-control run per seed serves all domains. Granularity: 17 training configurations at
  the full grid (4 domains × 4 nonzero ratios + 1 shared control) × seeds.
- **M4** — *Domain-matched capability evaluation suite*: code → HumanEval and MBPP pass@1
  (greedy, execution-based); math → GSM8K 8-shot exact-match on the final answer; prose →
  LAMBADA last-word accuracy and WikiText-103 perplexity; dialogue → MT-Bench single-turn
  1–10 score from a fixed frozen LLM judge with pinned version and rubric; plus, for every
  domain, held-out validation loss on M1's 1% split as a benchmark-independent damage
  measure. Every model checkpoint is evaluated on all four domains' suites (to also observe
  cross-domain spillover, though the primary contrast is within-domain).
- **M5** — *Vulnerability ranking and statistics module*: primary DV is the normalized
  capability delta Δ_d(r) = (S_d(0) − S_d(r)) / S_d(0) computed per domain-matched metric;
  per-domain degradation slope β_d estimated by OLS of Δ_d on r across ratios and seeds; the
  cross-domain ranking is the ordering of β_d with 95% bootstrap confidence intervals (10,000
  resamples over seeds × eval items); domain-selectivity itself is tested as the domain ×
  ratio interaction in a linear mixed model with seed as random effect.
- **M6** — *Generator-robustness and degeneracy controls*: (a) Stage-2 replication of the
  full single-domain sweep with a second generator from a different model family and quality
  tier, comparing rankings by Kendall's τ; (b) a degeneracy-matched control arm in which real
  documents are filtered/subsampled to match the synthetic text's measured repetition and
  type-token-ratio profile (from M2's logs), separating 'syntheticness' from generic quality
  degeneracy.

**Challenge arguments** (each cites valid C/M ids):
- `challenge_id: C1` ← `mechanism_components: [M2, M3, M6]` — "Token-accounted single-domain
  replacement (M3) attributes damage to the contaminated domain; M2's per-document degeneracy
  logging plus M6(b)'s degeneracy-matched real-text control arm then argue that whatever
  damage exceeds the matched-degeneracy arm is attributable to syntheticness rather than to
  generic quality degeneracy — a partial, not total, identification." [HYPOTHESIS]
- `challenge_id: C2` ← `mechanism_components: [M4, M5]` — "Domain-native metrics (M4) respect
  each domain's ground-truth structure, and M5's normalization (relative delta against the
  domain's own clean control, plus benchmark-independent validation loss) makes the
  cross-domain comparison a comparison of relative degradation rates rather than of raw
  incommensurable scores; sensitivity of the ranking to the normalization choice is reported."
  [HYPOTHESIS]
- `challenge_id: C3` ← `mechanism_components: [M3, M5]` — "The staged design samples the grid
  where it is most informative: the pilot's coarse {0,25,100}% sweep establishes existence
  and effect size, the Stage-2 grid establishes dose-response shape, and the second model
  scale is run only at extreme ratios to bound the scale interaction — a deliberate partial
