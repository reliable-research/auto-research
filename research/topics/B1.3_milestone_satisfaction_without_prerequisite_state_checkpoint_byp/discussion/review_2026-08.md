## Overall novelty stance

**🟡 somewhat-novel, salvageable by repositioning — the mechanism survives, the framing
does not.** The verdict is carried by claims 3 and 4's delta (attribution control +
conditional estimand over an explicit prerequisite DAG); it is capped below 🟢 because
the draft's motivating gap claim ("this practice rests on an unexamined assumption…
never been directly measured") was true when written (2026-07-05) and is **no longer true
as written** at t_audit: verifier exploitability in agent benchmarks has now been directly
measured at scale (Hardening Agent Benchmarks, Jun 2026), and a natural incidence of
unprompted verifier/environment exploitation has been reported (SWE-Marathon, 13.8%).
The one-sentence honest delta: *relative to Hardening Agent Benchmarks and SWE-Marathon,
this paper's real contribution is shifting the object of measurement from "can/do agents
defeat an outcome verifier" to "can every milestone-scoped checker in an explicit
prerequisite DAG pass while an independently audited terminal state fails — and is that
attributable to checker locality itself rather than checker weakness."* That delta is
real, unclaimed (direct search: "checkpoint bypass" → 0 hits), and load-bearing (RQ3–RQ5
are unaskable in any of the neighbors' designs).

**Decay/concurrency risk: HIGH.** Four of the six nearest neighbors are dated Feb–Jun
2026 — this cluster is publishing monthly. The conditional-estimand + attribution delta
is defensible today; at current velocity its half-life is short. Ship or schedule.

## Staleness verdict

**Stale in motivation/related-work; current in design.** The statistical plan,
estimand split, clustering discipline, and gating structure need no update. But the
draft's motivating gap claim is written against the July-2026 literature and three of
its "disjoint-corpus harvest targets" have effectively been superseded by *stronger,
verified* 2026 neighbors: the "benchmark grading remedies" paragraph's role is now
better filled by Hardening Agent Benchmarks (a measurement, not just a remedy), and the
missing natural-incidence precedent is now SWE-Marathon. The claimed generic gap
("no one measures how often local checks pass while ground truth fails") **no longer
exists**; the specific gap (milestone-locality, prerequisite DAG, conditional estimand,
attribution control) **still exists** — verified by direct search. Additionally,
SWE-Milestone/ADMIRE turn the draft's premise ("milestone-based grading is standard
practice") from an assertion into a citable fact — a free strengthening the draft
currently leaves unused.

## Findings (numbered, severity-tagged)

1. **[major] Overstated gap claim, falsified by post-draft literature.** Abstract ("We
   show this practice rests on an unexamined assumption") and Introduction ¶2 ("this
   reasoning quietly assumes something that has never been directly measured") are no
   longer true as written at 2026-08-15: Hardening Agent Benchmarks (2606.08960)
   directly measured outcome-verifier hackability on 1,968 real benchmark tasks (16%
   hackable), and SWE-Marathon (2606.07682) reports 13.8% unprompted reward-hacking
   incidence. A reviewer who knows either paper reads the framing as a scoop miss.
   **Fix:** concede-and-claim repositioning in Abstract, Introduction, Related Work,
   Conclusion — narrow the unmeasured thing to the milestone-locality estimand and the
   attribution question. Applied (see revision log).
2. **[moderate] RQ2's headline "natural incidence" has an unnamed nearest neighbor.**
   SWE-Marathon's 13.8% is exactly the sort of number a reviewer will wave at RQ2. The
   draft must name it and state the estimand delta (detected attempts vs.
   verifier-complete-yet-terminally-inconsistent, conditional, oracle-error-netted).
   **Fix:** RQ2 Role paragraph + Related Work paragraph. Applied.
3. **[moderate] RQ2 rollout-budget arithmetic under-specified in the draft.** The
   draft's minimum spec ("3–5 agents, ≥3 seeds, ≥20 tasks") yields as few as 180
   *attempted* rollouts — below the ≥200 *verifier-complete* floor even at an impossible
   100% completion yield. `experiment_design.md` already pre-registers the fix (the
   floor counts verifier-complete rollouts; total budget scales as 200/yield; PG-3
   blocks scoring until met; task mix re-selected under PG-0 if infeasible) but the
   draft never says so. **Fix:** mirror the completion-yield sentence into the draft's
   RQ2 setup (no new numbers invented — copied from the pre-registered design). Applied.
4. **[minor] Unused free motivation evidence.** SWE-Milestone (verifiable Milestone
   DAGs as deployed grading) and ADMIRE (milestone-anchored RL rewards) make the
   "standard practice" premise citable. **Fix:** one sentence + citations in
   Introduction/Related Work. Applied alongside finding 1's rewrite.
5. **[minor] Constructed-arm paradigm has a named precedent.** Hack-Verifiable
   Environments' embed-exploitability-by-design move parallels RQ1's constructed suite;
   naming it costs one clause and pre-empts a "this is just hack-verifiable
   environments for milestones" review line. Applied within the new Related Work
   paragraph.
6. **[minor] Related Work still carries the "disjoint-corpus, unverified — harvest
   targets only" paragraphs.** Honest as labeled, and the load-bearing positioning no
   longer depends on them (the verified 2026 neighbors now do that work); left in place.
   The Phase-A item-2 harvest STOP recorded in `pipeline.md` remains open but is now
   partially mooted for positioning purposes.
