## 2. Novelty verdict

**🟢 Novel-at-claim-date, carried on the estimand row (C1) with C3 as the supporting design row.** Not an average: C2 alone would be 🟡 (the literal-vs-behavioral gap is conceded prior art), but the paper does not rest on C2 — it rests on turning that gap into a rate a policy can move. Framing firewall check: the draft's "of which the checkpoint is the special case" is not a cosmetic subsumption verb — the containment is stated operationally (evaluate S at one time and threshold) and the extra structure (censoring, hazard shape, policy covariate) does real work in RQ1–RQ5. Category bar (empirical): met — the named instrument (censoring-aware behavioral-adoption estimator + laundering cell) makes measurable (neutralization *rate*; laundering frequency) what previously was not.

**Named nearest neighbors, ranked:**
1. **MemSecBench (2607.27080)** — closest; confronted head-on in Related Work ¶1; delta = rate vs checkpoint, policy manipulated vs fixed, behavioral vs literal event.
2. **Injection-Execution Dissociation (Leong, 2605.08442)** — second; supplies the dissociation but over short horizons with no persistence-over-time estimand.
3. **State Contamination (Wang et al., 2605.16746)** — laundering as existence result on one pathway; here it becomes a rate scaling with policy aggressiveness.
4. **The Misattribution Gap (Ahad et al., 2605.22842)** — known nearby memory-poisoning work; **boundary:** it is a *diagnosis/attribution* result — poisoned-memory failures get misread as model failure at the moment of failure — with no temporal estimand, no survival machinery, no policy manipulation. Not a method twin; no overlap with any of C1–C4's mechanisms.

## 3. Feasibility findings (severity-graded)

**MAJOR-1 — Probe-as-intervention is unaddressed.** Each acceptance probe is itself an interaction: it can write to memory, trigger consolidation, or re-salientize the poisoned claim (≈324,000 probe calls interleaved with ≈27,000 stream turns means probes dominate the agent's interaction budget). The measurement can move the hazard it measures, in either direction. Nothing in Methodology or Threats states whether probes run against a forked snapshot of *M_t* discarded after reading. **Fix:** pre-register probe isolation — fork the store at each probe time, probe the fork, discard; state it in the Behavioral Acceptance Probe method and Construct validity.

**MAJOR-2 — "Durably not-adopted" is not operationalized, and durability is horizon-truncated.** τ is defined as the first t after which adoption never recurs "through all subsequent probes" — so τ is only certifiable at horizon end, and an event at t close to T has had fewer chances to demonstrate durability than one at small t (a form of informative censoring on the event definition itself; re-adoption after apparent neutralization is exactly what laundered claims might do). **Fix:** pre-register durability as k consecutive not-adopted probes (e.g. k=3), report sensitivity in k, and treat events within k·Δ of T as right-censored.

**MODERATE-1 — TOST equivalence at |log HR| < 0.18 is likely underpowered at n=60/cell.** The sample-size rationale (KM median CI half-width < 0.15·T under ~50% censoring) is anchored to the *poison* estimand, not to equivalence power on the tracer arm; equivalence at half the effect floor typically needs several-fold the n of the corresponding superiority test. The draft's own (correct, added 2026-08-21) rule that an unresolved tracer blocks the causal reading means the study risks blocking itself by design. **Fix:** add an explicit TOST power computation to the design-grid paragraph; if n=60 is insufficient, the pre-committed enlargement rule should cover the tracer arm.

**MODERATE-2 — "Matched-salience" is undefined operationally.** The whole exposure-confound closure hangs on the tracer being salience-matched to the poison, but no salience metric, matching procedure, or manipulation check is specified. **Fix:** pre-register a salience proxy (e.g. retrieval frequency / attention weight in benign turns) and a manipulation check showing poison and tracer are matched on it at t=0.

**MINOR-1 — Within-stream probe interference:** the 120-subject probe battery at each probe time runs inside one agent stream; probing claim *i* may surface claim *j*. Batching order should be randomized and stated.
**MINOR-2 — Estimator switching rule** (Turnbull vs KM-on-reduction) is discretionary; pre-register the decision rule.
**MINOR-3 — κ on imbalanced binary verdicts** can be unstable; report raw agreement and prevalence-adjusted κ alongside.
**MINOR-4 — Nelson–Aalen has no dedicated bib entry** (the tex cites kaplan1958nonparametric at that spot); cosmetic, acceptable for a draft.
**Verified:** compute arithmetic checks out (135 cells × 200 turns = 27,000; 135 × 20 × 120 = 324,000); all 18 bib entries resolve; md↔tex mirror consistent after this pass; every refs.bib key is cited in the tex.
