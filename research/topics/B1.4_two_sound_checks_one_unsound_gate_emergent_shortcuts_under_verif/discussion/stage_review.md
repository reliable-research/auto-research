## Stage 4 — "Experiments" (Analysis/Finding variant: falsification, not correlation)

**STAGE 4 VERDICT: REVISE** *(fired, fixed — see below)*

**Why, quoting the draft:** The one question this stage asks in every archetype — is the claimed cause isolated from its strongest alternative — is well-served *structurally*: RQ2 is a genuine falsification design (re-attacking every "emergent"-labeled shortcut's single-gate origin at 10× budget to see whether the label survives, not just correlating), and RQ4/RQ5/RQ6 each name a specific alternative explanation to rule out. But the first assembled draft's RQ subsections stopped at "**Expected outcome.** ... at least three of the five pilot task families contain at least one such shortcut..." (RQ1) and the equivalent single-sided statement in every other RQ, without ever stating the converse — what result would *refute* that expectation. Per the Analysis/Finding bar: "the strongest finding papers construct a case where, if the claim were false, the result would differ... demand at least one such test" stated as a check, not implied. An "Expected outcome" alone reads as a hope; a stated falsification condition is what makes it a pre-registered, breakable claim. This is exactly the discipline the underlying `../paper.md` already has and the first assembled `paper_draft.md` lost: `paper.md` carries an explicit **Fallback** clause for every hypothesis (H1 at line 61, H2 at line 68, H3a/H3b at line 76, H4 at line 82, plus a global fallback F-G1 at line 89) that never made it into the six RQ subsections of `paper_draft.md`.

**THE ONE FIX (applied):** Add an explicit **Falsification condition** line to all six RQ subsections, stating the specific observation that would refute each expected outcome — mirroring the rigor already present in `paper.md`'s Fallback clauses, not restating the outcome's negation in vague terms. For example, RQ1's fix: "Refuted for a rule if fewer than three of five families contain a certified shortcut for it; if refuted for AND only, the claim narrows to disjunctive gates specifically... If refuted for both rules, the finding pivots to the global fallback: composition preserved soundness in every constructed condition, bounding rather than confirming the folklore composition-risk warning" — a direct restatement of `paper.md`'s H1 fallback and F-G1, now load-bearing in the paper draft itself rather than only in the pre-existing spec.
