## Datasets

| Dataset | Role | Justification |
|---|---|---|
| A pre-2022 slice of C4 or The Pile | Human reference pool | Contamination-free by provenance (pre-dates ChatGPT-era web contamination), and Pythia's own pretraining corpus (the Pile) predates it too — so the backbone's original training distribution and the continued-pretraining human pool are provenance-consistent. |
| Synthetic pool from Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct | Synthetic reference pool, pilot generator | Instruction-tuned LLM prompted to regenerate/continue documents from a disjoint corpus slice, rejection-filtered until distribution-matched to the human pool (document-length + topic-cluster Jensen-Shannon divergence ≤ 0.05 both). |
| Synthetic pool from Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct | Second, independent synthetic generator (scale-up stage) | Two generator families let `rq_scale_generator_stability` (RQ5) test whether the fitted curve family is a property of the phenomenon or an artifact of one generator's specific output distribution. |
| WikiText-103 | Second clean held-out corpus | A second, independently-curated clean held-out corpus alongside the in-domain human validation slice, so that `rq_shape_adjudication`'s (RQ3) primary-endpoint perplexity trend is checked against a source whose vocabulary and register differ from the training pool's own held-out split — guarding against a curve-shape verdict that is an artifact of overfitting to one validation source's idiosyncrasies rather than a property of the model. (This justification is the refiner-corrected version — the initial draft's bare "used for perplexity eval" note was flagged `major` in Stage 6 Round 1 for failing to tie the dataset to a hypothesis, then fixed in the refiner pass.) |
| lm-eval-harness tasks (LAMBADA, HellaSwag, ARC-Easy, PIQA) | Discontinuous capability battery | Standard small-model-scale few-shot capability suite, paired with continuous perplexity per the paired-metric-battery method's design (RQ2). |

## Baselines (sanity-check / confound-control comparators — not outcome-tier competitors)

| Baseline | Role | Justification |
|---|---|---|
| ρ = 0% clean continued-pretraining arm (B0) | Necessary control for every dose-level claim | Anchors every Δm(ρ) at ρ = 0; separates dose effects from continued-pretraining drift itself (t3). If ρ=100% does not differ from ρ=0 by ≥2·SD₀ on any endpoint at matched budget, there is no measurable harm to dose out at this scale, and every downstream claim is void (routes to F-G1). |
| No-CPT frozen-backbone anchor (B0) | Isolates CPT-itself drift | Measures how much continued pretraining itself, independent of any synthetic content, moves each metric — so dose effects are never confounded with generic CPT drift. |
| Human-low-entropy arm (B-alt) | Rival-explanation / confound control for RQ1 | Human text oversampled/duplicated until its measured (nearest-neighbor differential) entropy matches the synthetic pool's, run at doses ρ_eff ∈ {20%, 50%, 100%} through the identical harness. Decision rule (pre-committed): if this arm's dose-response is statistically indistinguishable from the synthetic arm's (overlapping fits, interaction p ≥ 0.05), the causal claim is re-scoped from "synthetic text harms" to "entropy-depleted text harms, and synthetic text is one instance." |
| Accumulate-regime arm | Secondary reference | Synthetic text added on top of, not replacing, the full human set — connects the classic Gerstgrasser et al. replace-vs-accumulate contrast to a continuous dose axis for the first time. Not a confound control; a secondary mitigation/context reference. |
| Entropy-maximizing data-selection arm at fixed ρ = 50% | Secondary mitigation reference | Adapted from CloserLookCollapse's entropy-maximizing selection countermeasure; contextualizes how much of any measured damage a generic mitigation already recovers, without claiming this paper's protocol "beats" it. |

## RQ1 — `rq_confound_control`

- **Tier:** mechanism-tier. **Target sub-problem:** `sp_confound_isolation`.
- **Target hypothesis (verbatim, Stage 5 `m_matched_corpus_harness` → Stage 6 `rq_confound_control`):** "A budget-matched human-text control whose distributional entropy is oversampled to match the synthetic pool's measured entropy will show a dose-response curve statistically distinguishable from the synthetic-fraction curve (non-overlapping fits, interaction p < 0.05) — demonstrating the measured effect is attributable to syntheticness specifically, not to generic distributional narrowing."
- **Setup:** Budget-matched continued-pretraining arms at doses ρ_eff ∈ {20%, 50%, 100%} in which the "contaminant" is human text (not synthetic) oversampled/duplicated from the same corpus until its measured entropy matches the synthetic pool's, run through the identical matched-corpus harness (same starting checkpoint θ₀, token budget, hyperparameters, shuffling-seed policy) as the main synthetic-fraction grid (`paper.md` Mandatory-baselines B-alt). The primary-endpoint dose-response curve fit on this human-low-entropy arm is compared against the curve fit on the synthetic-fraction arm via an interaction term (dose × arm-type) in the pooled mixed-effects model.
- **Sample-size rationale:** Same per-dose seed count as the corresponding main-grid doses — set by assumption A4's pilot power analysis (≥80% power to detect Δm = 1.0 at ρ = 5% with the planned seed count: 2 seeds/dose at Pythia-410M/1B tokens in the Stage-1 pilot, 3 seeds/dose at 2B tokens in the Stage-2 confirmatory grid). Run only at the three doses {20%, 50%, 100%} where entropy-matching is most tractable and where the main curve's dose effect is largest, rather than the full 11-point grid.
- **Statistical test:** Interaction term (dose × arm-type) in the pooled mixed-effects regression on the primary endpoint, significance threshold p < 0.05.
- **Expected outcome:** The synthetic-fraction and entropy-matched-human curves are statistically distinguishable (significant interaction), with the synthetic arm degrading more steeply at matched entropy.
- **Falsification / fallback (pre-committed):** If the two curves are statistically indistinguishable (non-significant interaction, overlapping fits), the finding is not void but re-scoped from "synthetic text harms" to "entropy-depleted text harms, and synthetic text is one instance of it."
- **Metrics:** Dose × arm-type interaction coefficient and its confidence interval on the primary endpoint (clean held-out perplexity); curve-fit overlap between the synthetic and human-low-entropy arms.

## RQ2 — `rq_metric_onset_order`

- **Tier:** mechanism-tier. **Target sub-problem:** `sp_metric_validity`.
- **Target hypothesis (verbatim, Stage 5 `m_paired_metric_battery` → Stage 6 `rq_metric_onset_order`; = `paper.md` H2/E2):** "Diversity and tail/entropy endpoints will show a significantly earlier onset dose (first Holm-significant deviation from ρ=0) than capability endpoints, and any apparent change-point detected on a discontinuous few-shot-accuracy metric will fail to replicate (Davies test non-significant) on its paired continuous metric evaluated on the identical checkpoints."
- **Setup:** The Stage-2 confirmatory checkpoints (11 doses × 3 seeds × 2 backbones, 2B tokens) are re-scored on the full metric battery M4 — capability (few-shot accuracy on LAMBADA/HellaSwag/ARC-Easy/PIQA, paired with continuous held-out perplexity), diversity (distinct-n, Self-BLEU, embedding coverage), and tail/entropy (rare-token recall, tail-decile perplexity, nearest-neighbor differential entropy). Onset doses are compared across metric families by a pre-registered rank rule; any discontinuous metric with a significant Davies change-point test is checked against its paired continuous metric on the identical checkpoints.
- **Sample-size rationale:** Reuses the Stage-2 confirmatory grid (11 doses × 3 seeds × 2 backbones, 2B tokens) sized by A4's pilot power analysis; no additional training runs are needed since this RQ re-scores existing checkpoints on additional metrics rather than requiring new arms.
- **Statistical test:** Per-metric onset dose = first Holm–Bonferroni-corrected significant deviation from ρ = 0 (α = 0.05, corrected across the secondary battery); paired Davies change-point test on each discontinuous/continuous metric pair, Holm-corrected within each metric family.
- **Expected outcome:** In ≥2 of 3 diversity endpoints (distinct-2, Self-BLEU, embedding coverage) and ≥1 of 2 tail endpoints, onset dose is ≥1 grid step below the primary capability endpoint's onset dose; every apparent change-point on a discontinuous few-shot metric (Davies p<0.05) fails to replicate (Davies p≥0.05) on its paired continuous metric.
- **Falsification / fallback (pre-committed):** If all metric families instead yield statistically indistinguishable onset doses and curve shapes (a metric-invariant verdict), that is a negative result on this hypothesis but a positive, practice-relevant one: "a single cheap endpoint (clean perplexity) suffices for contamination monitoring, simplifying deployment."
- **Metrics:** Onset dose ρ_onset(m) per metric (Step-5 definition: smallest ρ∈G with Holm-significant Δm(ρ) ≠ 0); per-family winning curve shape; paired discontinuous/continuous Davies-test agreement.

## RQ3 — `rq_shape_adjudication`

- **Tier:** mechanism-tier. **Target sub-problem:** `sp_threshold_identifiability`.
- **Target hypothesis (verbatim, Stage 5 `m_curve_adjudication` after refiner pass → Stage 6 `rq_shape_adjudication`; = `paper.md` H1/E1):** "On the primary endpoint, the convex power-law family will win the AICc comparison (weight ≥ 0.5) with fitted exponent γ̂ > 1 (95% CI excluding 1), the Davies change-point test will be non-significant (p ≥ 0.05, Holm-corrected), and the TOST-certified harmless region will be non-empty but confined to the lowest 1–2 grid doses — a soft, resolution-limited safe region rather than a genuine mechanistic threshold. If the change-point family instead wins the AICc comparison with a significant Davies test, that is not a failure of this hypothesis but a **stronger and different actionable finding** — a genuine dose threshold at the fitted change-point location, reported as such rather than forced into the convex-family framing. If the flat family wins even at ρ=100% (no detectable degradation at any tested dose), the finding routes to a pre-committed archetype pivot (an equivalence-certified negative result), not a silent null."
- **Setup:** Stage-1 pilot (7 doses × 2 seeds, Pythia-410M, 1B tokens) estimates SD₀(m) and powers the Stage-2 grid. Stage-2 confirmatory grid (11 doses × 3 seeds × 2 backbones, 2B tokens; dose grid G = {0, 0.5, 1, 2, 5, 10, 20, 35, 50, 75, 100}%) fits a mixed-effects regression (seed random effect) per metric on the primary endpoint (held-out perplexity on the in-domain slice + WikiText-103), then compares four candidate response families — flat, linear, convex power-law, segmented change-point — by AICc.
- **Sample-size rationale:** Stage-1 pilot (7 doses × 2 seeds) estimates SD₀(m) and powers the Stage-2 grid; Stage-2's 3 seeds × 2 backbones × 11 doses is sized so low-dose cells retain ≥80% power to detect Δm = 1.0 at ρ = 5% (A4). Per the Power addendum (guidance.md Part 4 situation 5), any dose×seed cell still underpowered after reallocation (budget shifted from dose levels to seeds, continuous endpoints promoted to primary) is pre-declared **INCONCLUSIVE** and excluded from the AICc/Davies/TOST verdict computation rather than folded into a null or PASS reading.
- **Statistical test:** AICc model comparison across the four families (weight ≥ 0.5 to declare a winner); Davies test for change-point existence (α = 0.05, Holm-corrected); Jonckheere–Terpstra monotone-trend test; TOST two-one-sided equivalence test against ρ = 0 with margin δ = 0.5·SD₀(m) fixed before the confirmatory grid is run, multiplicity-corrected across the secondary battery.
- **Expected outcome:** Convex power-law wins (AICc weight ≥0.5, γ̂>1, CI excluding 1); Davies test non-significant; TOST-certified safe region ρ* non-empty but confined to the lowest 1–2 grid doses.
- **Falsification / fallback (pre-committed, per-conjunct):** Change-point family wins with significant Davies test → reported as a genuine, stronger dose threshold at the fitted change-point location. Flat family wins even at ρ=100% → global fallback F-G1 (archetype pivots to an equivalence-certified negative result).
- **Metrics:** Δppl(ρ) (Step-5 primary-endpoint definition); AICc weights across the four families; Davies test p-value; TOST verdict per dose; fitted exponent γ̂ and its 95% CI; certified safe-region boundary ρ*.

## RQ4 — `rq_detector_attenuation`

- **Tier:** mechanism-tier. **Target sub-problem:** `sp_dose_axis_measurement_error`.
- **Target hypothesis (verbatim, Stage 5 `m_detector_coupling` → Stage 6 `rq_detector_attenuation`; = `paper.md` H3/E3):** "Re-fitting on detector-estimated ρ̂ will attenuate the fitted dose-response slope on the primary endpoint (slope ratio b̂(ρ̂)/b̂(ρ) ≤ 0.8) and will widen the TOST-certified harmless region by at least one grid step relative to the ground-truth fit, with the calibration error e(ρ) = ρ̂(ρ) − ρ negative (under-estimating) at low ρ — i.e., detector noise biases a contamination policy toward permissiveness, not merely toward imprecision."
- **Setup:** Two independent black-box detector instruments (a recoverability-style detector per DandR, OpenReview:FiMZSxo4DO, and a Binoculars-style detector) score a fixed 5,000-document sample per mixture; per-document scores are aggregated into an estimated corpus-level fraction ρ̂ via a calibration curve fit on the two pure (ρ=0, ρ=1) pools. The full RQ3 curve-adjudication pipeline is re-fit substituting ρ̂ for the known ρ; per-instrument and pooled verdicts are reported. Gated on assumption A6 (document-level detector AUROC ≥ 0.8 on human-pool vs. synthetic-pool; skeleton gate S2) — if both instruments fail S2, this RQ is dropped to future work.
- **Sample-size rationale:** 5,000-document sample per mixture for detector scoring (fixed, not tied to the training-run seed count) supports bootstrap-resampled calibration-curve confidence intervals; the underlying dose-response refit reuses the same Stage-2 confirmatory grid (11 doses × 3 seeds × 2 backbones) as RQ3, since this RQ re-analyzes those same checkpoints under a detector-estimated dose axis rather than requiring new training runs.
- **Statistical test:** Slope-ratio comparison b̂(ρ̂)/b̂(ρ) against the ≤0.8 threshold; safe-region shift Δρ* measured in grid steps; sign of mean calibration error e(ρ) for ρ ≤ 10% (negative = under-estimating).
- **Expected outcome:** Slope-attenuation ratio ≤0.8; safe-region widens by ≥1 grid step; mean e(ρ)<0 for ρ≤10% (under-estimation at low dose).
- **Falsification / fallback (pre-committed):** If detector-estimated fit does not meaningfully attenuate slope or shift the safe region (thresholds move by less than one grid step), that is a positive, practically useful result — off-the-shelf detectors can be used directly as a policy input today. If the shift is instead toward over-alarming (Δρ* < 0, a narrower certified-safe region) rather than permissiveness, the signed direction — not the existence of a shift — is reported as overturned, while the coupling analysis itself still stands.
- **Metrics:** Calibration error e(ρ) = ρ̂(ρ) − ρ with bootstrap CIs (documents resampled); safe-region shift Δρ*; slope-attenuation ratio b̂(ρ̂)/b̂(ρ).

## RQ5 — `rq_scale_generator_stability`

- **Tier:** mechanism-tier. **Target sub-problem:** `sp_scale_generalization`.
- **Target hypothesis (verbatim, Stage 5 `m_factorial_transfer_check` → Stage 6 `rq_scale_generator_stability`; = `paper.md` H4/E4):** "The AICc-winning curve family on the primary endpoint will be identical across all four tested backbone-scale × generator cells, while the fitted dose-coefficient magnitude will differ significantly by scale (dose × scale interaction p < 0.05)."
- **Setup:** A coarser dose sub-grid {0, 5, 20, 50, 100}%, 3 seeds, run as a factorial design crossing two backbone scales (410M/1.4B) × two independent synthetic-text generators (Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct / Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct) = 4 cells. A pooled mixed-effects model estimates the interaction of dose with scale and with generator identity; the AICc-winning response family from RQ3's adjudication criterion is checked for exact agreement across all four cells.
- **Sample-size rationale:** The coarser 5-point sub-grid (vs. the 11-point main grid) × 3 seeds × 4 cells keeps factorial cost bounded while supporting a categorical exact-match test on winning family (no significance test needed — it is exact agreement across 4 cells) plus a pooled mixed-effects interaction estimate for the coefficient-magnitude claim.
- **Statistical test:** Winning-family agreement across the 4 cells is a categorical exact-match criterion (no test attached). Dose × scale and dose × generator interaction terms from the pooled mixed-effects model, interaction significance threshold p < 0.05 (an interaction is permitted and expected — no pass/fail criterion is attached to the interaction's presence itself, only to whether the winning family stays identical).
- **Expected outcome:** Winning response family identical across all 4 backbone-scale × generator cells; fitted dose-coefficient magnitude differs significantly by scale (interaction p<0.05).
- **Falsification / fallback (pre-committed):** If the winning family itself differs across any of the four cells (e.g., a change-point win at one scale but a convex win at another), no single dose-response law transfers; contamination tolerances must be calibrated per training regime rather than once, and the released measurement harness becomes the deliverable for that per-regime calibration rather than a single portable curve (C5 elevated from threat to finding).
- **Metrics:** Per-cell AICc-winning family (categorical agreement); dose × scale and dose × generator interaction coefficients from the pooled mixed-effects model.
