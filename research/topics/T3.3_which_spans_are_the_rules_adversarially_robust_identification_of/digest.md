## N6 Methodology

**overview** [HYPOTHESIS]: "Staged plan. **Stage 1 (pilot = the seed's minimal experiment):** build GovSpan-Adv, a paired corpus of ~300 constraint spans (sourced from real Tensor Trust defense prompts and agent policy documents) and ~300 length/topic-matched non-constraint spans (sourced from LMSYS-Chat-1M conversational logs, following the distractor-sourcing precedent of [PrefEval, OpenReview:QWunLKbBGF]); apply a validated bidirectional adversarial rewriter (Evade: constraint→casual register; Spoof: chit-chat→imperative register); evaluate five identifier families on original vs. rewritten spans *in isolation*, reporting false-pin and miss-pin rates per condition with paired statistics. **Stage 2 (scale-up):** embed the same pairs at controlled depths inside long multi-session transcripts (PrefEval-style, LMSYS-Chat-1M filler) and require span-*localization* + classification in continuous context, adding an adaptive-attack round where the rewriter gets query access to the best identifier. Throughout, a boundary/ambiguity audit separates ground-truth fuzziness from identifier failure."

**key_components:**

- `id`: "M1", `name`: "GovSpan-Adv seed corpus", **text** [HYPOTHESIS]: "~300 constraint spans drawn from two real pools — human-written defense prompts released by [TensorTrust, OpenReview:fsW7wJGLBd] (each is an actual access-control rule) and agent policy/system-prompt rules — plus ~300 non-constraint spans sampled from LMSYS-Chat-1M single turns, filtered to contain no directive content, then matched to constraints on token length (±20%) and topic (embedding-cluster matching). Every span carries a ground-truth label validated by 2 annotators; items with disagreement go to adjudication, and Cohen's κ on the label is reported (target κ ≥ 0.7)."
- `id`: "M2", `name`: "Bidirectional adversarial rewriting engine", **text** [HYPOTHESIS]: "Two LLM-based attack operators. *Evade(c)*: rewrite constraint c into casual conversational register while preserving its obligation — validity gated by (a) an NLI-style entailment back-check that the rewrite still entails the original obligation and (b) dual human validation; rewrites failing either gate are discarded and regenerated (expected 20–30% rejection). *Spoof(s)*: rewrite chit-chat s into authoritative/imperative register while a validity gate confirms no genuine new obligation was introduced. Rewriter model family is deliberately disjoint from every identifier family in M3 (see N10/t2). Directly instantiates the seed's '对抗改写的约束/非约束配对集'."
- `id`: "M3", `name`: "Identifier suite under test", **text** [HYPOTHESIS]: "Five identifier families spanning the plausible design space: (a) a keyword/deontic-cue heuristic (regex over modals, imperatives, 'always/never/must'); (b) a fine-tuned DeBERTa-v3-base binary span classifier trained on the original (non-adversarial) pairs; (c) a zero-shot prompted LLM-as-judge (frontier model, family disjoint from M2's rewriter); (d) a role/privilege-signal baseline adapted from the instruction-vs-data separation paradigm of [ASIDE, OpenReview:C81TnwHiRM] (does channel/position information alone suffice?); (e) a cumulative-context prompted monitor adapted from [SeqMonitor, OpenReview:HQuboWvFA1] (does trajectory-level context help?). Each emits a pin/no-pin decision plus a confidence score."
- `id`: "M4", `name`: "Paired-difference evaluation protocol", **text** [HYPOTHESIS]: "For each identifier and each attack direction, compare decisions on the *same underlying item* in original vs. adversarial phrasing: report miss-pin rate = 1 − recall on constraint spans, false-pin rate = FP/(FP+TN) on non-constraint spans, robustness gap Δ = adversarial − original error rate, AUROC over confidence scores, and McNemar exact tests on the paired decision flips (analysis plan in N8). Reporting the two directions as separate numbers keeps C3's asymmetry visible instead of hiding it in aggregate accuracy."
- `id`: "M5", `name`: "Boundary/ambiguity audit", **text** [HYPOTHESIS]: "All items on which the best identifier errs, plus a matched random sample of correct items, are re-annotated blind by 2 annotators for constraint-hood *given full context*; errors are then partitioned into (i) genuine identifier failures (annotators agree with the gold label) vs. (ii) contested-label cases (annotators split or flip), yielding a quantified estimate of how much of the measured 'adversarial fragility' is actually C4-type ground-truth fuzziness, and a small taxonomy of contested boundary cases."
- `id`: "M6", `name`: "In-context embedding + adaptive attack stage", **text** [HYPOTHESIS]: "Stage-2 instrument: construct ~100 multi-session transcripts (~8k–32k tokens) of LMSYS-Chat-1M filler, embedding 3–5 GovSpan-Adv items per transcript at controlled depths (early/middle/late thirds), *without* span boundaries given; identifiers must localize and classify (span-level F1 with IoU ≥ 0.5 overlap credit, depth-stratified miss-pin rates).
