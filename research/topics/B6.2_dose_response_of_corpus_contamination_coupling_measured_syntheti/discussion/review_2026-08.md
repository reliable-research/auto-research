## 2. Feasibility findings (se-paper-reviewer lens)

**[major] MA-1 — Missed fraction-continuous collapse line (novelty/positioning).**
The draft's Introduction claimed "no one has actually measured the shape of the relationship" and
Related Work claimed prior collapse work manipulates "never a continuous within-generation
fraction." Both die against Dohmatob et al. 2402.07043 / 2410.04840 (fraction-continuous
scaling-law treatment, with experiments), Seddik et al. 2404.05090, and Kazdan et al. 2410.16713 —
none cited, none in the 150-candidate harvest (verified: zero hits in
`reference_candidates.jsonl`). Strong Model Collapse's "no safe fraction" prediction is moreover a
*rival hypothesis* the adjudication criterion must name, since a linear-family win with an empty
certified-safe region is exactly that world. → Revised (see §4). Bib entries withheld pending
arXiv verification (Citation debt).

**[major] MA-2 — Equivalence-certification power arithmetic.**
`experiment_design.md` pre-registers TOST margin δ = 0.5·SD₀ with 2–3 seeds/dose and claims ≥80%
power to detect Δm = 1.0 at ρ = 5%. Arithmetic: a per-dose two-sample contrast at standardized
effect d = 1.0 needs ~17 seeds/arm for 80% power (3 vs 3 gives ~19%); a per-dose TOST at margin
0.5·SD₀ needs ~49/arm. As specced, the "certified-safe region" — the paper's headline deliverable —
is unattainable by per-dose testing at any feasible seed count. The pooled mixed-effects trend
model (33 runs/backbone) can carry a model-based equivalence bound, but the draft did not say the
certificate is model-based. → Revised: certificate now explicitly computed on the pooled fit, with
pilot-calibrated margin and a pre-declared INCONCLUSIVE route for underpowered certificates
(consistent with the existing A4/guidance Part-4 addendum). Residual to-do for Phase B: re-derive
the margin/power table from pilot SD₀ before locking the pre-registration.

**[moderate] MO-1 — Detector coupling ignored the quantification literature and classical
measurement-error corrections.** Aggregating per-document detector scores into a corpus fraction is
prevalence estimation (adjusted classify-and-count); sibling topic B6.1 already builds on
quantification-corrected estimators while B6.2's module used a naive pure-pool calibration only,
and the signed permissive-bias contribution claim was unscoped — attackable as "fixable by
regression calibration." → Revised: naive + corrected arms both reported; signed-bias claim scoped
to the naive plug-in; residual post-correction bias re-framed as the isolable shared-cause
component (this *strengthens* C1: it converts an objection into a designed contrast).

**[moderate] MO-2 — Portfolio wiring not carried into the draft.** `portfolio_2026-08.md` folds
retired B6.4's domain/rarity factor into B6.2 "as a design note" and records the rarity-driven
memorization result (ICML 2026, per `industry_signal_scan_2026-08.md`) as the refresh that kept
B6.2 alive — but the draft carried no trace of either. → Revised: domain/rarity-stratified
dose-response added to Further Evaluation as the absorbed design note.

**Budget arithmetic (checked, no finding):** Stage-1 pilot 14 runs × 410M × 1B tok ≈ 75–80
A100-h; Stage-2 grid 33 runs × (410M + 1.4B) × 2B tok ≈ 1,600 A100-h; factorial + control arms
roughly double it. Order 4,000–5,000 A100-hours total ≈ low-five-figures USD — feasible for a small
lab; the kill-test (ρ=100% vs ρ=0 must differ by ≥2·SD₀ or all downstream claims void, routing to
F-G1) is pre-registered and cheap to hit first. No revision needed.
