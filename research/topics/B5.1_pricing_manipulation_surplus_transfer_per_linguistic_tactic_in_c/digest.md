## N6 Methodology

### `overview` [HYPOTHESIS]
"A factorial, causal-injection experiment crossing {no-tactic control + four linguistic tactics} × {LLM models in the manipulator and/or counterparty role} × {counterparty sophistication levels} in a simulated single-good bilateral bargaining environment. Each side is privately assigned a reservation price drawn from a controller-fixed valuation function; the total available surplus (seller reservation to buyer reservation, i.e., the ZOPA width) is therefore known exactly to the scorer. One agent ('the manipulator') is prompted to deploy exactly one tactic (anchoring / false urgency / fabricated external offer / emotional pressure) at controlled intensity and timing; its counterpart runs a fixed negotiating prompt. Dialogues run to agreement or a turn cap; the final price (or impasse) is mechanically compared against the matched no-tactic control to compute surplus transfer attributable to that tactic. **Staged plan:** **Stage 1 (pilot = the seed's minimal experiment)** — one manipulator model, one default counterparty, synthetic single-good scenarios with controlled reservation prices, all four tactics + control, enough trials to establish that at least one tactic moves surplus beyond control; **Stage 2 (scale-up)** — full cross-model matrix (both roles), counterparty-sophistication conditions, transfer to a naturalistic bargaining benchmark, and audit of realized tactic fidelity / refusal."

### `key_components`
- `id`: "M1", `name`: "Bargaining environment harness", `text` [HYPOTHESIS]: "Turn-based bilateral single-good negotiation with configurable per-side reservation prices, turn cap (e.g., 8 exchanges), explicit accept/reject/counter-offer action grammar, and deterministic impasse rule. Instantiated over parameterized synthetic scenarios and, for external validity, over an existing benchmark substrate (GLEE-style parameterized economic games; CraigslistBargain / DealOrNoDeal for naturalistic price talk). Logs every turn and the final price at per-dialogue granularity; supports hundreds of trials per condition."
- `id`: "M2", `name`: "Tactic injection module", `text` [HYPOTHESIS]: "A library of prompt templates operationalizing each of the four tactics at ≥2 intensity levels (e.g., anchoring = opening offer at k× the ZOPA-implied fair point for k∈{1.5, 3}), with matched injection timing (first offer vs. mid-dialogue). Templates are the sole manipulated variable; the counterparty prompt and scenario are held fixed. Design mirrors controlled-injection testbeds ([TensorTrust, OpenReview:fsW7wJGLBd], [ChatInject, OpenReview:WVhgFSKniL], and — per the 2026-07-04 situation-6 concession — [DECEPTICON, arXiv:2512.22894] and [TRAP, arXiv:2512.23128], which independently confirm named manipulation-tactic taxonomies steer LLM agent behavior in web-navigation settings) but with an economic rather than string-integrity or task-redirection objective."
- `id`: "M3", `name`: "Reservation-price / valuation controller", `text` [HYPOTHESIS]: "Draws and conceals ground-truth valuations per side per the seed's constraint ('真值 = 分配的估值函数'); values enter only the scorer, never the agents' visible context. Randomizes ZOPA width and midpoint across trials to prevent memorized anchors and to enable surplus normalization."
- `id`: "M4", `name`: "Welfare / surplus-transfer metric", `text` [HYPOTHESIS]: "Per dialogue, computes surplus captured by each side = (agreed price − that side's reservation), and the transfer statistic ΔS = (manipulator's captured-surplus share under tactic) − (matched control share), normalized by total ZOPA. Also computes deal rate, impasse rate, and individual-rationality-violation rate (agreements worse than a side's own reservation). Purely mechanical from M3 ground truth."
- `id`: "M5", `name`: "Cross-model evaluation matrix", `text` [HYPOTHESIS]: "Runs the identical tactic set with multiple LLMs (mix of frontier-aligned and smaller/open models) in both manipulator and counterparty roles, producing a per-model susceptibility ranking and a manipulator-potency ranking on a common footing."
- `id`: "M6", `name`: "Realized-manipulation & fidelity auditor", `text` [HYPOTHESIS]: "Measures the *realized* treatment: an LLM-judge + human spot-check classifies whether the intended tactic actually appears in the manipulator's turns (fidelity), flags manipulator refusals/guardrail deflections, and probes whether a concession is genuine vs. strategically feigned (following the fake-vs-genuine-compliance distinction of [StrategicDishonesty, OpenReview:IbDr8xgUMW]). Turns realized manipulation strength into a measured covariate rather than an assumed constant."
