## 3. Feasibility findings (se-paper-reviewer lens)

- **[moderate] F1 — No total budget stated, and the seed's cost estimate is wrong by >20×.** The pre-registered grid implies ≈15–18k episodes: RQ2 scale-up (36 pairs × 3 families × 50) ≈ 5,400 free-text; RQ3 structured mirror ≈ +5,400; bilateral control ≈ +4,500 at 3 gap levels; scripted-opponent and pilot/calibration cells ≈ +2,500. At 3k–15k tokens per multi-turn episode that is ≈50–270M tokens — versus `paper.md` N6's "~2–3M tokens, tens of dollars." The draft itself states no budget at all, making the pre-registration unauditable and the thin-cell rule untestable in advance. **Applied to draft: scale-and-budget paragraph added (md+tex).**
- **[moderate] F2 — Δ_b circularity.** The endogenous gap rating is "fit from round-robin free-text self-play," and RQ2's scale-up is "a full round-robin over the capability ladder." If the same episodes both estimate the rating and supply the loss observations regressed on it, the regressor is a function of the outcome sample (regression on a generated, in-sample regressor → overstated slope precision, endogeneity). Fix is cheap and standard: pre-register a disjoint split (rating-estimation episodes ≠ measurement episodes). **Applied to draft: disjoint-split sentence added to the gap-quantification method and RQ2 setup (md+tex).**
- **[moderate] F3 — Outbound/inbound channel conflation.** The constrained condition deletes the *weak agent's own* free-text field; the adversary's free text still flows in (only the bilateral control removes it). Yet the abstract/problem-definition mechanism language — "deny a stronger counterparty the surface through which its advantage converts into surplus," "removes its exposure to persuasive exploitation" — reads as closing the persuasion channel, which the unilateral condition does not do: it closes the weak side's *outbound* expressive surface (leakage, exploitable commitments, engagement) while leaving *inbound* persuasion open. RQ5's slope-removal ratio therefore bounds only the outbound-plus-engagement component; the free/structured/bilateral cell triple in fact identifies the two surfaces separately, but the draft never says so. **Applied to draft: surface-decomposition sentences added to Problem Definition, Structured-Offer Constraint, and RQ5 (md+tex) — analysis mapping only, no new cells.**
- **[minor] F4 — Power arithmetic is generic.** n=50/cell "detects d≈0.4 at ~80%" is two-group arithmetic; the actual test is a mixed-model Wald test on a slope whose power depends on gap-level spread and pair-level variance. Acceptable for a spec; flag for the pilot. Not revised.
- **[minor] F5 — Impasse scoring mixes margins.** L=1 at impasse folds the extensive margin into the loss metric; a condition that raises impasse rates could masquerade as (anti-)protection. Agreement/impasse rates are already reported metrics; a conditional-on-agreement sensitivity read is implied but not pre-registered. Not revised (minor; RQ1's existence check partially guards).
- **[minor] F6 — Corpus familiarity.** Deal-or-No-Deal is a 2017 public corpus; frontier models have plausibly seen it. Affects level, not the within-design contrasts. Not revised.
