## 3. Feasibility findings (severity-graded)

Panel: R1 methodology / R2 domain / R3 significance / Devil's Advocate, per `se-paper-reviewer`. F2, F3, F7 are the interrupted pass's labels; F1, F4, F5, F6, F8 reconstructed.

- **F1 [major] — Staleness: the deferred concurrent-work repositioning had never reached the draft.** `stage_review.md` (Phase A item 2 / "Deferred") flagged in July that CAT, HiR, and AgenticSTS are genuine method-twin-adjacent works requiring concede-and-differentiate treatment before any further prose phase; the 2026-07-05 draft contained no trace of them. *Status: **applied** by the interrupted 2026-08-21 pass (Introduction third-paragraph extension + new Related Work paragraph "Concurrent bounded-context and constraint-scaling work"); citations verified against the live arXiv API by the resumption pass.*
- **F2 [moderate] — Power analysis prices the wrong outcome for half the claims.** The stated rationale (450 episodes/cell, 80% power) is sized for a **10-percentage-point task-success** contrast, but RQ1's violation-rate claim targets a **3pp absolute** difference and RQ2's non-inferiority margin is **0.02 absolute** — effects 3–5× smaller than the one the power budget was computed for. At the episode level, 450/cell is nowhere near powered for a 2–3pp contrast at plausible base rates; the design's implicit rescue is that violation-rate inference runs at the (constraint, episode)-pair level, where each episode contributes many applicable pairs — but the draft never says so, and never pre-commits what happens if pilot base rates and intra-episode correlation leave the 0.02 margin under-powered. *Status: **unapplied** at interruption; applied by the resumption pass (power-basis sentences in RQ1 Sample-size rationale and RQ2 Setup, with a pre-registered margin-relaxation fallback tied to the existing INCONCLUSIVE rule — no new numbers invented).*
- **F3 [moderate] — The study's own measurement cost is never budgeted — in a paper about token overhead.** The scale-up grid is 7 count-levels × 6 policies × 3 models × 450 episodes/cell = **56,700 multi-turn tool-use episodes** (arithmetic from the draft's own Setup), with the full-pinning arms carrying constraint blocks that grow linearly to n = 400 in every turn. Nothing in the draft states this total, estimates the token spend, or names a mitigation — an omission the Devil's Advocate flags as self-undermining given the paper's thesis that linear-in-n token cost is the problem. *Status: **unapplied** at interruption; applied by the resumption pass ("Measurement cost" paragraph in Experimental Setup: episode total, why full-pinning cells dominate spend, prefix-caching + pilot-gated scale-up mitigations, and spend-logging as part of the artifact — no fabricated dollar figures).*
- **F4 [minor] — Overclaim: "has never been measured" stated without scope.** The Introduction's gap sentence claimed the failure modes had "never been measured" simpliciter; RECAST-line work does measure count-linked degradation and the concurrent cluster measures adjacent quantities, so the honest claim is the *joint* measurement for *standing normative constraints in a multi-turn agent setting*. *Status: **applied** by the interrupted pass (gap sentence rescoped to "...has never been measured jointly", with the setting named).*
- **F5 [minor, advisory] — Judge-model circularity in the semantic-constraint arm.** The LLM judge that scores semantic-constraint violations may share family or failure modes with the agent models under test; a correlated blind spot would depress measured violation rates precisely where compliance is hardest. Mitigation already in the design (human-calibrated κ-gate, per-class confusion inspection, programmatic-subset restatement fallback) is adequate for the headline claims; recommend (no text change required now) that the Phase-B implementation choose a judge from a different model family than the three swept backbones and log judge identity in the artifact.
- **F6 [moderate] — Applicability labels are load-bearing but their reliability is never audited.** The ground-truth app(c, e, t) labels authored at pool-construction time feed three load-bearing uses — the density factor (RQ3), the violation-rate denominator, and the oracle selector (RQ2) — yet RQ4's calibration machinery audits only viol, not app. Noisy applicability labels would silently distort the oracle upper bound and the density manipulation with no gate to catch it. *Status: **unapplied** at interruption (not in the interrupted agent's referenced list; surfaced by this pass's R1 persona); applied by the resumption pass (one-sentence independent re-annotation check on a stratified app-label sample, added to the sweep-harness Method).*
- **F7 [moderate] — The applicability classifier is an oracle and must be framed as one.** The fifth selector consumes ground-truth applicability labels unavailable at deployment time; without the oracle framing, RQ2's expected outcome reads as "our deployable selector beats similarity", which the design cannot support. *Status: **half-applied** at interruption — the Methodology (Selective-Pinning Policy Family, Method paragraph) got the oracle-upper-bound sentence; RQ2 still presented the contrast without the oracle reading. Completed by the resumption pass (RQ2 Setup and plain-language Hypothesis now carry the oracle-upper-bound framing).*
- **F8 [minor, advisory] — External validity rests on two τ-bench domains.** Airline + retail policy tasks are the entire primary task suite; the Conclusion's limitations paragraph covers scope-of-constraint-set but not scope-of-task-domain. Acceptable for a pre-registered spec (the constraint pool spans IFEval/FollowBench/PrefEval templates), but the Phase-E writeup should name domain coverage as a threat. No change required now.

**Verified strengths (claims checked and confirmed, not just weaknesses):** the falsification conditions are genuinely pre-committed and specific (all four RQs, including the two-sided refutation of RQ1); the INCONCLUSIVE/388-per-arm rule prevents silent null-scoring; the inert-padding arm is a real rival-explanation control, not decoration; the trade-off tracker's hypothesis-free status is disclosed rather than hidden; and the concede-and-claim discipline in Related Work names its twins in the opening paragraph rather than burying them.

**Panel scores (SE rubric, design-stage reading — grading the specification, not results):** Novelty & Positioning 78; Methodological Rigor 80 (post-F2/F3/F6/F7 fixes; 68 before); Evidence & Soundness n/a-prospective (design targets only, honestly labeled); Reproducibility & Artifacts 70 (artifact plan named, nothing built); Threats to Validity 74; Clarity 82. **Meta-reviewer disposition: the design is sound to proceed to Phase B; the dominant risk is not methodological but temporal (see decay annotation).**
