# Discussion: B3.2 漂移的方差分解 — Capability, Policy, or Format? Decomposing Behavioral Drift across LLM Version Pairs

> **Document type:** structured research discussion (auto-research-discussion style: 6 stages, each with an internal critic → refine pass).
> **Topic status from portfolio audit:** KEEP, **Tier-2 (support)**, label [机制] (mechanism). The flagged risk is **INTERNAL**: the orthogonality/identifiability of the three components (capability / policy / format) is methodologically shaky. Per the audit directive, the sub-problems and every critic pass in this document attack identifiability head-on — including whether a format change can masquerade as a capability change under the proposed decomposition, what identification assumptions are required, and how each is tested.
> **Hard constraint (inherited from parent topic B3 跨版本行为漂移 / 纵向审计):** black-box / external-audit only. No weights, no logits, no training data, no changelog trust. Attribution must be inferred from input/output behavior alone.

---

## Stage 1 — Problem Definition

**Setting.** A version pair (v_old → v_new) from one provider, both queryable black-box. Downstream observers see behavioral change but cannot tell *why*: did the model get smarter/dumber (**capability**), did its alignment/refusal policy move (**policy**), or did only its output style/structure drift (**format**)? The seed's core question: 一次版本升级的行为变化中，多少来自能力变化、多少来自对齐/政策调整、多少只是格式偏好漂移？

**Naive formulation.** Design three probe batteries, one per axis; run both versions with repeated samples; fit a variance decomposition apportioning the observed per-item behavioral deltas into capability/policy/format shares; then test whether the share structure is common across vendors (检验分解在厂商间是否有共同结构).

**Outputs.** Per pair: a point on the (capability%, policy%, format%) simplex plus leakage and noise terms; calibration accuracy on control pairs; a cross-vendor structural test.

**Critic (identifiability, head-on):** The naive formulation is ill-posed in at least four ways. (1) **Variance shares are instrument properties, not model properties**: "X% of drift is format" depends on the battery's composition — double the format items and the format share moves; the decomposition as stated quantifies the battery, not the upgrade. (2) **The axes are not observable partitions of behavior; they are latent causes, and the map from cause to observation is many-to-one**: a format change (markdown fences) masquerades as a capability drop under executability scoring — this literally happened in arXiv:2307.09009's code results; a policy tightening masquerades as capability loss when refusals are scored as errors; a capability gain masquerades as policy loosening when a model that now *knows* an answer stops refusing (knowledge-aware refusal, RefusalIndex's core finding). Without further assumptions, the three-cause model is unidentifiable from behavioral deltas alone. (3) **No ground truth exists on the pairs of interest** (commercial, black-box), so identifiability cannot be checked where it matters — only argued via calibration elsewhere, which introduces a transfer assumption. (4) **Basis incompleteness**: real updates also change verbosity, reasoning style, sycophancy, multilingual behavior — forcing three axes on a higher-dimensional change guarantees mis-attribution into whichever probe battery happens to load on the residual.

**Refined:** The problem is redefined so identifiability is *inside* the deliverable, not assumed away. Target: **not** "the true causal shares of an upgrade" but **"axis attribution under a declared measurement model M with an explicit identification-assumption ledger (A1–A5), each assumption tested or bounded, calibrated on version pairs whose dominant change axis is known by construction, and reported with leakage/residual/noise terms that carry the un-attributed remainder."** Shares are defined relative to a *pre-registered, equal-axis battery design* (declared item counts and weights), with sensitivity of shares to battery composition reported as a first-class result. The masquerade phenomena in (2) become named test cases the method must demonstrably defeat — or the paper becomes a measurement critique showing they cannot be defeated black-box (both outcomes are in scope; see Stage 6).

## Stage 2 — Motivation

Blended drift numbers actively mislead. The canonical audit (arXiv:2307.09009) reported code-generation "regressions" partly caused by newly added markdown fencing breaking the executability check — format drift scored as capability loss, in the field's most-cited drift paper. Providers rarely disclose what a version bump changed; integrators experiencing breakage cannot triage: format drift → fix parsers; capability drift → re-benchmark; policy drift → re-review compliance. An axis-resolved drift report converts an undifferentiated alarm into a differentiated action, and — if the decomposition shows common cross-vendor structure — the field gains a shared taxonomy of "what a version bump changes" for longitudinal audits.

Within the B3 portfolio, B3.2 is the **mechanism layer** under the Tier-1 evidence topics: B3.3 finds silent changes and asks which behavior categories they concentrate in — B3.2 supplies the category system with calibrated instruments; B3.4 measures contract breakage — B3.2 tells it whether a breakage wave was a formatting event or a capability event, which changes the accountability story.

**Critic:** The motivation is hostage to the internal risk. If identifiability fails, "axis-resolved report" degrades into "three arbitrary numbers with a taxonomy attached," and the triage story collapses — an integrator misdirected by a wrong attribution (told "format" when it was policy) is *worse off* than with a blended alarm. The motivation as written also proves too much: WildBench and the judge-bias literature already treat style as a confound to control; a reviewer will ask why a *decomposition* is needed rather than simply *format-robust scoring everywhere* (which removes the masquerade without any attribution machinery). The motivation must answer: what does attribution buy beyond confound-removal?

**Refined:** Two changes. (1) The motivation is restated as **conditional with two publishable outcomes**: (a) if the calibrated decomposition passes its identifiability gates, the field gets the first validated black-box drift-attribution instrument; (b) if it fails in the ways the critic predicts, the study delivers quantified masquerade evidence — "here is how many percentage points of reported 'capability drift' are reproduced by a pure format intervention" — a measurement critique that directly corrects how audits like 2307.09009 are read. Outcome (b) is not a consolation prize; it is the internal-risk-honest version of the same contribution. (2) The "why not just robust scoring?" question gets a direct answer that becomes a design principle: robust scoring removes format's *contamination of capability measurement*, but it cannot tell you *that format itself drifted* — which is exactly what breaks parsers downstream (B3.4's subject). Attribution is needed because format drift is a first-class outcome, not only a nuisance.

## Stage 3 — Related Work

**The drift phenomenon and its mis-measurement:** Chen, Zaharia & Zou, arXiv:2307.09009 (canonical longitudinal audit; blended deltas, single vendor, and the markdown/executability incident that seeds this topic's motivation). CARLoRA [OpenReview:5GimteSrgW] (version drift concretely breaks adapters; drift treated as undifferentiated perturbation).

**Detection-not-attribution adjacents (the sweep's three anchors):**
- **arXiv:2504.04715 — model-substitution auditing** [VERIFY title/authors]: detects whether the served model is the claimed model. Binary identity question; no decomposition of *what kind* of change occurred.
- **arXiv:2512.03816 — logprob-based update tracking** [VERIFY]: tracks that/when a model changed via logprob signatures; requires logprob access (violates our black-box floor as a general instrument) and yields change-points, not cause attribution.
- **arXiv:2506.06975 — rank-based uniformity test** [VERIFY]: statistical test that behavior shifted. Upstream of B3.2: it answers "did it change?"; B3.2 answers "change of what kind, in what proportions?" — and can consume such tests as its drift-vs-noise gate.

**Attribution instruments that need white-box access (boundary: inapplicable to commercial pairs):** MoreRLHFMoreTrust [OpenReview:FpiCLJrSW8] (stage-wise behavioral attribution across a *controlled* training pipeline via influence functions); FewTokensDeep [OpenReview:6Mxhg9PtDE] (paired-model per-token KL isolates policy depth — needs logits); DiffInterpretation [OpenReview:6As4wfTB77] (maps weight diffs to behavior descriptions — needs weights).

**Single-axis constructs the batteries build on:** RefusalIndex [OpenReview:9gJBhkLRat] (refusal vs knowledge are separable constructs — also the source of the capability→policy masquerade warning); ContrastiveOverRefusal [OpenReview:GXcN0MuN1q] (policy and superficial lexical form are *coupled* in training — a caution against assuming probe-axis purity); WildBench [OpenReview:MKEHCx25xp] (length/style systematically distorts judged measurement — format-as-confound evidence).

**Decomposition precedents (closest methodological relatives):** "LLMs Get Lost in Multi-Turn Conversation" (aptitude vs unreliability decomposition of degradation) [VERIFY exact citation]; "Are Emergent Abilities of LLMs a Mirage?" (Schaeffer et al., NeurIPS 2023) — attribution of a claimed model property to *metric choice*, the same move B3.2 makes against blended drift scores. **Classical measurement theory:** ANOVA/variance-component estimation and generalizability theory (Cronbach et al.) [VERIFY] — the "shares depend on the facet design" problem is a solved-and-named issue there (the universe of admissible observations must be declared), which B3.2 imports; blind-source-separation/ICA identifiability results as the formal analogy for "latent causes from mixtures need assumptions."

**Gap statement:** no prior work performs a **three-way capability/policy/format decomposition of black-box version-pair drift, with construction-known control pairs as ground truth and a cross-vendor structural test**. The closest works either attribute with white-box access, measure one axis at one time point, or treat style as a nuisance rather than an attributed component.

**Critic:** (1) The decomposition-precedent entries are the ones a reviewer will weaponize: if the multi-turn aptitude/unreliability split is methodologically equivalent (two-way, behavioral, black-box), B3.2's claim narrows to "added a third axis + controls + cross-vendor" — the framing must make the *identification machinery* (control-pair calibration, leakage ledger, masquerade tests), not the axis count, the contribution. (2) Generalizability theory is cited as an import but the text doesn't commit to actually using its formalism — half-importing it invites "reinvented variance components, badly." (3) The [VERIFY] tags on all three sweep anchors and on the classical citations must be resolved pre-draft; in particular 2512.03816 might already attempt cause categorization of detected updates. (4) In-portfolio boundary: B3.1 also computes per-family drift scores from probe batteries — the two topics must not ship near-identical batteries with different claims.

**Refined:** (1) Contribution sentence fixed now: "the contribution is an *identification framework* (assumption ledger + construction-known calibration + masquerade falsification tests) for black-box drift attribution, instantiated on three axes" — the framework, not the trinity, is the claim; axis count is explicitly extensible (Stage 5's residual axis). (2) Commit: the decomposition model is written *in* variance-component/G-theory vocabulary (facets = axis, item, replicate; the declared battery = the universe of admissible observations), which both inoculates against the "shares are instrument properties" critique — G-theory makes the dependence explicit — and connects to a 60-year literature. (3) Pre-draft verification task pinned for all [VERIFY] items with a kill-criterion: any published black-box cause-attribution of LLM version drift ⇒ B3.2 pivots to replication + identifiability critique. (4) Battery-sharing with B3.1 is made explicit and virtuous: one shared probe bank, two analyses (B3.1: predict breakage; B3.2: attribute composition), cross-referenced — cheaper and salami-proof because the claims are disjoint.

## Stage 4 — Sub-Problems (identifiability-centered)

**SP1 — Formal identifiability analysis of the measurement model.** Write the generative model: observed per-item delta y_i = Σ_axis load_i(axis)·δ_axis + interaction + ε_i, with latent axis changes δ and probe loadings. Derive, on paper before any experiment, the **masquerade matrix**: which (true cause → measured axis) confusions are possible under which scoring rules — format→capability under strict parsing; policy→capability under refusal-as-error; capability→policy under knowledge-aware refusal; policy→format via refusal-boilerplate inflating style features. Output: the minimal assumption set under which the model is identifiable, and the named failure modes when each assumption breaks. This sub-problem is the paper's spine; everything downstream instantiates or tests it.

**SP2 — Instrument design for approximate orthogonality.** Build batteries that pin two axes by construction: capability items scored format-robustly (fence-stripping, normalization, execution) so format cannot move the capability score; format items drawn from a pre-verified both-versions-near-ceiling set so capability cannot move format features; policy items stratified (safe-but-seemingly-unsafe / pseudo-harmful / genuinely-harmful) so tightening and loosening separate. Residual leakage is *measured*, never assumed zero.

**SP3 — Drift vs noise floor.** Within-version variance (decoding stochasticity, serving-stack nondeterminism, silent infra changes) must be estimated via k-repeats and interleaved query windows, and axis contributions counted only in excess of it — otherwise sampling noise is "decomposed" into meaningless shares.

**SP4 — Synthetic ground-truth validation (the empirical identifiability test).** Construct version pairs where the true change type is known by construction: **policy controls** — same weights, system-prompt-induced policy tightening, plus a safety-SFT variant; **format controls** — same weights under format-constrained decoding / altered chat template / style-SFT (JSON-forcing, markdown-forcing); **capability controls** — domain SFT (math) and a degradation direction (e.g., quantization) as a stress case. Run the full pipeline **blind**; score dominant-axis recovery, the empirical confusion (masquerade) matrix, and leakage shares. Include **mixed-dose pairs** (compose two known changes at known relative strengths) to test whether recovered shares track constructed dose — recovery of pure pairs is necessary but nowhere near sufficient.

**SP5 — Overidentification and robustness tests on real pairs.** Since ground truth is absent exactly where it matters, build internal consistency checks that a *valid* decomposition must pass and an artifactual one will fail: split-half battery agreement (disjoint item halves must yield the same simplex point); scoring-rule swap (strict vs robust parser must change the *naive* attribution but not the model's); intervention consistency (wrapping v_old itself with an added format constraint must move only the format share); paraphrase stability.

**SP6 — Basis completeness and cross-vendor commensurability.** Estimate the residual/unattributed share; if it is large or structured (e.g., a verbosity/reasoning-style factor emerges from item-level residual clustering), the three-axis basis is misspecified and the taxonomy must grow rather than force-fit. Only *after* SP1–SP5 hold does the cross-vendor structure test (检验分解在厂商间是否有共同结构) become meaningful — and "policy" may not even be the same construct across vendors (one vendor's policy rule is another's output filter or system prompt).

**Critic:** (1) SP4's controls are seductive but **too clean**: a system-prompt policy change also changes format (refusal boilerplate is a style feature — ContrastiveOverRefusal shows policy and surface form are coupled *in training*), and format-constrained decoding can depress capability on hard items (constrained generation hurts reasoning). So the "known by construction" labels are really "dominant axis by construction, purity unknown" — if the pipeline is graded against impure labels as if pure, both false confidence and false failure are possible. (2) SP4's controls are all open-weight 7–8B-class interventions; the transfer assumption — leakage calibrated there transfers to frontier commercial pairs — is untestable in-protocol and is the single biggest inferential leap; it must be named as an assumption (A5), not buried. (3) SP5's intervention-consistency test is the strongest idea in the document and is underweighted: it is the *only* check that runs on the real commercial pairs themselves; it should be promoted from "a robustness test" to a headline experiment. (4) Cross-vendor W-tests at n≈6 pairs / 3 vendors are nearly powerless; SP6's structural claim risks being decoration.

**Refined:** (1) Control-pair labels are re-specified as **dominant-axis-with-measured-purity**: for each control, the intended axis's constructed strength is accompanied by measured side-effects on the other two axes (e.g., quantify boilerplate style shift induced by the policy prompt), and recovery is graded against the *measured* dominant axis, with a hardness ladder from clean (template swap) to entangled (safety-SFT). (2) A5 (calibration transfer) enters the assumption ledger with an explicit partial test: calibrate on small models, validate on the largest open-weight pairs available (e.g., 70B-class), and report leakage as a function of scale — if leakage grows with scale, the commercial-pair attributions get wider declared bounds. (3) SP5's intervention-consistency is promoted to a co-primary experiment (Stage 6's E3): every real-pair attribution ships with its self-intervention control. (4) The cross-vendor test is demoted to **exploratory** (stated in the pre-registration as descriptive with CIs, no binary claim) unless the pair count reaches ≥10; the seed's cross-vendor question survives as structure-*description*, not hypothesis-*confirmation*.

## Stage 5 — Methods

**(a) Decomposition model.** Per-item paired deltas y_i (k≥5 repeats per item per version, fixed decoding, interleaved query windows). Hierarchical variance-component model in G-theory form: facets = axis battery (fixed), item (random, nested in axis), replicate (random). Axis share = variance component attributable to the axis's battery **in excess of the within-version noise floor**, normalized on the simplex together with two explicit remainder terms: **leakage share** (cross-axis regression: capability deltas explained by the item's format-feature deltas; policy classifications explained by surface lexical cues) and **unattributed residual**. Output per pair: (capability%, policy%, format%, leakage%, residual%, noise floor) with 1,000-resample bootstrap CIs over items. Battery design is pre-registered equal-axis (declared item counts/weights); share sensitivity to battery reweighting is a reported analysis, not a hidden vulnerability.

**(b) Identification assumption ledger (each assumption paired with its test):**
- **A1 (probe-axis alignment):** each battery loads primarily on its axis; leakage bounded by λ. *Test:* empirical confusion matrix + leakage shares on control pairs (E1); requirement λ < 15% mean.
- **A2 (additivity):** axis effects combine approximately additively at the item level. *Test:* interaction terms on mixed-dose controls; dose-response linearity R².
- **A3 (noise stationarity):** within-version variance is stable across query windows. *Test:* interleaved windows; same-version window-to-window drift must sit inside the repeat-based floor.
- **A4 (basis adequacy):** residual share small and unstructured. *Test:* residual size + clustering of item-level residuals; a structured residual factor triggers basis extension, not silent absorption.
- **A5 (calibration transfer):** leakage bounds estimated on open-weight controls transfer to commercial pairs. *Test (partial):* leakage-vs-scale trend across small→70B-class controls; declared as the untestable-in-full assumption, with attributions for commercial pairs reported as intervals widened by the worst measured leakage.

**(c) Validation via synthetic ground truth (construction-known pairs).** ≥9–12 control pairs, ≥3 per axis, on a hardness ladder: *format* — same weights + chat-template/JSON-forcing/markdown-forcing decoding, then style-SFT; *policy* — same weights + system-prompt policy tightening (the cheapest, purest lever), then safety-SFT per published recipes; *capability* — math/domain SFT (gain direction) and quantization (loss direction, stress case). Each control's **purity is measured** (side-effect deltas on the other axes) before grading. Then **mixed-dose pairs**: e.g., policy-prompt + format-template composed, at 2–3 relative strengths; the pipeline must recover *shares tracking dose*, not just the dominant label. Headline calibration metrics: blind dominant-axis recovery (binomial vs 1/3 chance), full 3×3 masquerade/confusion matrix, mean leakage, dose-response R².
- **Masquerade demonstrations (the critic's cases, made into experiments):** run the format-only control through a naive strict-parsing capability harness → quantify the fake "capability drop" (the 2307.09009 incident reproduced under controlled conditions); run the policy-only control through refusal-as-error scoring → quantify fake capability loss; run a capability-gain control against refusal metrics → quantify fake policy loosening. Then show the identified pipeline (format-robust scorers, 3-way comply/refuse/hedge classification, knowledge-conditioned refusal analysis) shrinks each masquerade below the noise floor — or report by how much it fails to.

**(d) Overidentification checks on every pair (real and control):** split-half simplex agreement (ICC over disjoint item halves); scoring-rule swap sensitivity; **self-intervention control** on real pairs (wrap v_old with an added format constraint; a valid pipeline moves only the format share); paraphrase stability of shares.

**Critic:** (1) The leakage term is estimated by *linear* cross-axis regression on hand-chosen surface features; nonlinear or unobserved-feature leakage passes straight through into the axis shares — the leakage estimate is a lower bound and must be labeled as such. (2) The near-ceiling pre-pass for format items creates a selection artifact: items at ceiling for *both* versions can under-sample exactly the regions where format and capability interact; format drift measured there may understate deployment-relevant format drift. (3) The 3-way judge classification in the policy battery imports LLM-judge bias (WildBench's own warning) into the instrument — judge drift between the two versions' response *styles* could differentially misclassify, i.e., format leaking into policy *through the judge*. (4) The quantization "capability control" is admitted to be impure (quantization measurably changes style too); if graded naively it deflates recovery accuracy for reasons the ladder already anticipates.

**Refined:** (1) Leakage reported as "≥" (measured lower bound), with a sensitivity analysis adding random-forest cross-axis predictors as an upper-bound probe of nonlinear leakage; the gap between the two bounds becomes part of the reported uncertainty. (2) The format battery adds a **mid-difficulty stratum** with capability held fixed *statistically* (item-matched accuracy across versions verified post hoc) rather than only at-ceiling by pre-pass; both strata reported. (3) Judge robustness protocol: judge sees **style-normalized** responses for the policy classification where feasible (boilerplate stripped, length-truncated), human validation on a 600-item stratified sample *per version* (not pooled), and judge-swap (two different judge models) agreement reported; policy shares only claimed where judges agree. (4) Quantization is retained but as a labeled *stress case* outside the headline recovery statistic, exactly as the hardness-ladder design intends.

## Stage 6 — Experiment Design

**Arms:**
- **E1 (identifiability calibration — gate experiment):** 9–12 construction-known control pairs + 4–6 mixed-dose pairs; blind recovery, confusion matrix, leakage bounds, dose-response. Runs *first*; everything else is conditional on it.
- **E2 (real-pair decomposition):** ≥6 version pairs, ≥3 vendors (snapshot-pinned commercial endpoints currently serving both versions, e.g., successive gpt-4o / Claude / Gemini snapshots [VERIFY current availability at study start]) + open-weight anchors (Llama-3→3.1, Qwen2→2.5); axis-share simplex per pair with full uncertainty accounting.
- **E3 (self-intervention controls — co-primary):** for every real pair, the wrap-v_old-with-format-constraint (and system-prompt-policy) interventions; attribution validity evidence *on the pairs of interest themselves*.
- **E4 (masquerade quantification):** the three masquerade demonstrations under naive vs identified scoring — the measurement-critique payload, publishable regardless of E1's outcome.
- **E5 (exploratory cross-vendor structure):** descriptive simplex comparison, rank-agreement (Kendall's W) with permutation CIs, clustering by vendor — explicitly exploratory unless ≥10 real pairs materialize.
- **Baselines:** blended aggregate delta (2307.09009-style single number); policy-only audit (refusal-rate delta); capability-only audit (benchmark delta); two-way ablation (capability+policy, no format axis) — tests whether the format axis changes attributions at all.

**Scale.** ~3,000 probe items (≈1,500 capability / 1,000 policy / 500 format) × 2 versions × 5 repeats ≈ 30k calls per pair; 6 real + ~15 control/dose pairs ≈ 400–600k generations. Cost class: $2–5k API (commercial pairs, all raw outputs cached for release) + ~250–350 GPU-hours (open-weight inference + ~12 small SFT runs for controls) + ~40 annotator-hours (judge validation). 1–2 researchers, 2–3 months. Feasibility risk #1: snapshot deprecation mid-study → mitigation: select only pairs with both endpoints live at start, query interleaved and immediately, cache everything; open-weight anchors carry the replicability burden.

**Metrics.** Dominant-axis recovery accuracy (exact binomial vs p₀=1/3, α=0.05); confusion-matrix off-diagonal mass; leakage lower/upper bounds; dose-response R² (mixed controls); split-half ICC; self-intervention specificity (share moved on the intended axis vs others); axis shares + CIs per real pair; residual share and residual-structure test; masquerade magnitudes under naive vs identified scoring; exploratory Kendall's W.

**Go/no-go gates (pre-registered, sequential):**
- **G1 (identifiability gate, after E1):** blind recovery ≥8/9 on the clean-ladder controls, mean leakage lower-bound <15%, split-half ICC ≥0.7, dose-response R² ≥0.6 on mixed pairs. **Pass → proceed to E2/E3.** **Marginal (recovery 6–7/9 or leakage 15–25%) → decomposition demoted to "bounded attribution" language; continue but with interval-only claims.** **Fail → pivot (see below), do not run E2 at full scale.**
- **G2 (real-pair signal gate):** on ≥4 of 6 real pairs, at least two axes exceed the noise floor and the self-intervention control (E3) is axis-specific. Fail → real drift is either noise-dominated or the instrument doesn't transfer (A5 broken); report as such.
- **G3 (structure gate for the seed's cross-vendor question):** only evaluated if pair count ≥10; otherwise E5 stays descriptive.

**Pivot on G1 failure (the internal risk made productive):** the paper becomes the **measurement critique**: "black-box three-way drift decomposition is not identifiable under assumptions A1–A5 — here is the masquerade matrix (E4) quantifying how format changes are booked as capability changes in current audit practice, and here are the minimal access conditions (e.g., logprobs, paired system-prompt control) under which identification would be restored." This directly corrects the reading of 2307.09009-class audits and remains a self-contained contribution.

**Merge-vs-standalone decision rule (relative to B3.3/B3.4):**
- **Strong G1 pass + E3 axis-specificity → STANDALONE mechanism paper** ([机制] label honored): the identification framework + calibrated instrument is the contribution; B3.3 and B3.4 then *cite and consume* it (B3.3: silent changes attributed by axis; B3.4: breakage waves classified by cause).
- **Marginal G1 → MERGE as analysis module into B3.3:** the bounded-attribution version isn't strong enough to carry a methods paper, but "silent changes concentrate in axis X (within stated bounds)" is exactly the category analysis B3.3's accountability story needs; B3.2 ships as B3.3's mechanism section, sharing the probe bank already co-built with B3.1.
- **G1 fail → the pivot critique** ships either as a compact standalone (if the masquerade quantification is striking, e.g., naive scoring misbooks >30% of drift) or as a cautionary section + released control-pair recipes inside B3.3.
- Timing trigger: G1 is scheduled before B3.3/B3.4's scale-up freeze so the merge decision lands while their paper structures are still open; the shared probe bank with B3.1 (Stage 3 refinement) means no duplicated querying either way.

**Critic:** (1) The G1 thresholds (8/9, 15%, 0.7, 0.6) are conventional round numbers; with 9 controls the binomial test is coarse and one hard control (safety-SFT, deliberately entangled) can flip the gate — the gate should be computed on the clean ladder and *reported* on the full ladder, which the current text half-says but must pre-register exactly. (2) E2's "≥3 vendors with both snapshots live" is an availability assumption about the world; if only one vendor qualifies at start, the cross-vendor component silently dies and the seed's second question with it — needs a declared minimum viable configuration. (3) The standalone trigger relies on E3 self-interventions, but a format-wrap intervention on a *commercial* endpoint changes the prompt, and prompt-sensitivity could itself differ across versions — the intervention control has its own confound. (4) Salami risk with B3.1/B3.3 remains if the marginal-G1 path and B3.1's merged path both land inside the same B3.3/B3.4 cluster with overlapping figures.

**Refined:** (1) Pre-registration fixed: G1's binomial computed on the ≥9 clean-ladder controls only (safety-SFT and quantization excluded as labeled stress cases); all thresholds carried with their CIs; a simulation-based power check sizes the control count before any SFT run. (2) Minimum viable configuration declared: the study is viable with ≥2 commercial vendors + 2 open-weight families; below that, E5 is dropped *in the pre-registration* and the paper claims only per-pair attribution — the cross-vendor question is explicitly deferred to a follow-up rather than answered weakly. (3) The self-intervention confound is handled by symmetry: the identical wrap applied to *both* versions, with the attribution test on the *differential* response to the wrap, plus a no-op wrap (whitespace/benign-prefix) as placebo — prompt-sensitivity differences then load on the placebo and are subtracted. (4) A portfolio figure-ownership map is written when G1 lands: B3.2 owns simplex/confusion/masquerade figures, B3.3 owns changelog-alignment figures, B3.1/B3.4 own prediction/breakage figures; one shared corpus, disjoint claims, cross-citation instead of duplication.

## Summary Position

B3.2's scientific center of gravity is **identifiability, not decomposition**: the naive variance split is unidentifiable (format↔capability↔policy masquerades are real and documented), so the contribution is an identification framework — assumption ledger, construction-known control pairs with measured purity, mixed-dose recovery, self-intervention and split-half overidentification tests — that either validates black-box drift attribution or quantifies precisely why it fails. Both outcomes serve the B3 portfolio: a validated instrument upgrades B3.3/B3.4 from "something changed / something broke" to "here is what kind of change did it"; a failed gate becomes a measurement critique that corrects how the field reads blended drift audits. The standalone-vs-merge decision is made by gate G1's outcome, on a schedule aligned to the Tier-1 leads.
