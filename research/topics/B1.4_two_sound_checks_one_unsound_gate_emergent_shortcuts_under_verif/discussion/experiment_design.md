## Datasets

- **Constructed bounded-DSL pilot suite** (this paper's own artifact, CO2). *Justification:* the only setting where per-verifier soundness is exhaustively decidable; required for RQ1 (existence), which is defined as a proof, not a rate, and therefore cannot be answered on any benchmark corpus.
- **HumanEval+ / MBPP+** (EvalPlus-amplified differential test suites). *Justification:* supply the ≥10⁴-input differential ground truth the scale-up certifier and attacker require, at the smallest per-task constraint complexity (single test-suite facet), forming the base cell of the scale-up grid.
- **BigCodeBench**. *Justification:* tasks natively carry multiple constraints per task, giving the conjunctive-gate (AND) conditions a realistic multi-facet setting rather than an artificially bolted-on second check. *Differential-corpus protocol (per `paper.md` Step 5 notation; design_review.md S-5 residual):* BigCodeBench (and LiveCodeBench) tasks ship no EvalPlus-amplified corpus, so D is generated by applying the same EvalPlus-style amplification to each task's reference tests; any task whose corpus cannot reach the ≥10⁴-input floor is excluded and counted toward RQ3's thin-cell accounting rather than silently retained.
- **LiveCodeBench (post-cutoff subset)**. *Justification (refined in Stage 6's refiner pass, replacing an earlier generic "contamination control" label):* isolates RQ3/`rq_prevalence` (H2) and RQ4/`rq_hardening_vs_adding` (H4) from a specific rival explanation — that an apparent "emergent shortcut" is really the attacker or verifier models reproducing a memorized near-solution rather than genuinely lacking a general one. Because LiveCodeBench's post-cutoff problems postdate the verifier and attacker models' training data, this cell rules out memorization as an alternative account of any prevalence finding that the other three benchmarks cannot rule out on their own.

## Baselines

Sanity-check comparators and rival-explanation controls, not competitors to beat — this is a measurement study, and no research question here is framed as "our procedure beats baseline X."

- **B0-C (current practice / "why do this at all" control).** Verifier A alone and verifier B alone, each attacked on its own task variant at the identical budget (N=500). If composed gates behave indistinguishably from their strictest single component everywhere, the motivating concern collapses (→ the global fallback F-G1 route). Every RQ3/H2 comparison is against B0-C by construction.
- **B0-P (null-by-construction placebo, evaluated first, before any RQ is scored).** The degenerate same-space configuration: both single verifiers and the composed gate certified and attacked over one shared space (pilot) or a self-composition of two independently seeded instances of the same check (scale-up). Because a ground-truth-failing composed accept in this configuration would contradict a same-space single-gate certificate by construction (Problem Definition), this arm must return exactly zero emergent shortcuts; any nonzero count marks the run invalid (ground-truth incompleteness, a certificate bug, or an attacker bookkeeping error), and no RQ is scored until the pipeline is re-diagnosed from the walking skeleton (paper.md Step 9).
- **Naive AND / Naive OR gates.** The compositions under direct test.
- **UTRL-style adversarially hardened single verifier.** Instantiated training-free as iterative adversarial test augmentation (repeatedly attacking the current suite and adding a killing test for every discovered unsound acceptance, cost-matched to the naive-AND arm), approximating UTRL's objective without its RL training cost. Tests RQ4/`rq_hardening_vs_adding` (H4): whether hardening one check is a substitute for adding a second.
- **SWE-RM-style thresholded execution-free reward model.** Adapted from SWE-RM by fixing a pre-registered acceptance cutoff, calibrated on a held-out split, not tuned against attack results — an explicit disclosed adaptation since the source model outputs a continuous ranking score rather than a Boolean verdict (Stage 3 refiner note). Instantiates one concrete heterogeneous verifier pairing (a test suite paired with a thresholded learned scorer) in the scale-up grid.
- **R2-Guard-style rule-linked aggregation.** A structured, non-naive aggregation scheme, tested at equal budget to isolate whether the composition gap comes from *naive Boolean* aggregation specifically, or from composition in general (the B-alt secondary rival-explanation control).

## RQ1 — `rq_existence`

**Tier:** Mechanism. **Target sub-problem:** `sp_nonvacuous_construction`.

**Target hypothesis (verbatim, matches the Stage 5 `m_bounded_dsl_generator` hypothesis exactly):**
> "For each composition rule (AND and OR, scored independently), at least 3 of 5 exhaustively certified pilot task families contain ≥1 emergent shortcut: a candidate accepted by the composed gate while failing the differential ground truth over the fully enumerated candidate space, with both single-verifier variants certified shortcut-free over that same space."

**Setup.** For each composition rule (conjunctive and disjunctive, scored independently), five hand-constructed pilot task families are exhaustively enumerated over their bounded candidate space. For each family and rule, the single-verifier-A and single-verifier-B variants are certified sound by full enumeration, and the corresponding composed variant is searched over the same fully enumerated space for a candidate the composed gate accepts and the ground truth rejects.

**Sample-size rationale.** 5 pilot task families × 2 composition rules. The pilot is exhaustive, not sampled — decision is exact via full enumeration of each variant's own bounded certification space (|S_v| ≤ 10⁷), so no power calculation applies; the "≥3 of 5" threshold is a pre-registered majority-of-five decision rule, not a sample-size-derived figure.

**Statistical test.** None — exact decision via exhaustive enumeration (a machine-checked binary existence bit per family/rule), not a statistical test.

**Expected outcome.** For each composition rule scored independently, at least three of the five pilot task families contain at least one such shortcut, with both single-verifier variants certified shortcut-free over the identical fully enumerated space.

**Metrics.** Binary per (task family, composition rule): existence of an accepted, ground-truth-failing candidate over the fully enumerated composed-variant space, alongside the corresponding single-verifier certificates.

## RQ2 — `rq_attribution_validity`

**Tier:** Mechanism. **Target sub-problem:** `sp_attribution_of_emergence`.

**Target hypothesis (verbatim, matches the Stage 5 `m_facet_certifier` hypothesis exactly):**
> "Re-attacking every emergent-labeled shortcut's single-gate variants at 10× the original certification budget will reclassify a nonzero but minority (<50%) fraction of Stage-1 emergent-labeled shortcuts as shared-blind-spot events — demonstrating the 10× re-attack control is load-bearing (it does catch some artifacts of under-budgeted certification) without being so large that it undermines the emergent-gap findings it is meant to validate."

**Setup.** Every shortcut provisionally labeled emergent in the scale-up sweep (RQ3) has its single-verifier-A and single-verifier-B task variants re-attacked at ten times the original certification budget, using the same fixed attacker configuration.

**Sample-size rationale.** N is whatever set of shortcuts RQ3's scale-up sweep discovers and provisionally labels "emergent" — this arm is not independently powered; it is a mandatory re-verification pass over RQ3's own output, applied to every qualifying shortcut rather than a subsample.

**Statistical test.** None formalized as a significance test; the decision rule is a fraction threshold applied directly to the reclassified count (0% reclassified = control is inert; ≥50% reclassified = control explains away the RQ3 finding).

**Expected outcome.** A nonzero but minority fraction — under 50% — of provisionally emergent-labeled shortcuts are reclassified as shared blind spots under the extended-budget re-attack.

**Metrics.** Count and fraction of provisionally emergent-labeled shortcuts reclassified after the 10× re-attack, reported per verifier-pairing cell.

## RQ3 — `rq_prevalence`

**Tier:** Mechanism. **Target sub-problem:** `sp_existence_to_prevalence`.

**Target hypothesis (verbatim, matches the Stage 5 `m_budget_matched_attacker` first hypothesis exactly):**
> "Under matched attack budgets, FAR(composed) exceeds FAR(strictest single gate) with McNemar exact test p<0.05 (Holm-corrected across the 12 primary comparisons) and a discordant-pair asymmetry ≥7 percentage points, in at least 4 of the 12 (6 verifier-pair types × 2 composition rules) primary comparisons, including at least one AND-gate comparison."

**Setup.** A grid of approximately 200 benchmark tasks across the four datasets above, crossed with approximately six verifier-pairing types, is attacked with a fixed comparison attacker 𝒜 (fixed model, fixed prompt scaffold, 500 attempts per gate) applied identically to every single and composed gate. Single-gate certification uses an independent certification attacker 𝒜_cert — a different model, prompt scaffold, and seed family from 𝒜 — so the comparison attacks never draw randomness used to select what counts as certified-sound in the first place (rules out the winner's-curse confound: gates certified against 𝒜 itself would show FAR(single)≈0 in 𝒜's comparison by construction).

**Sample-size rationale.** ~200 tasks per cell across 12 primary cells (6 verifier-pairing types × 2 composition rules). The 200-tasks/cell target is what powers the 7-percentage-point discordant-pair-asymmetry detection floor at 0.8 power. *Thin-cell policy:* if compute limits or verifier-determinism/attack-symmetry exclusions (A4/A5) leave any of the twelve primary cells below 150 tasks — the smallest n at which the same design retains 0.8 power for a 7pp asymmetry — that cell is pre-declared INCONCLUSIVE rather than scored as pass, fail, or silently pooled with an adjacent cell; the "at least 4 of 12" count is then evaluated only over non-INCONCLUSIVE cells, with the INCONCLUSIVE count reported alongside the headline. *Scorable-cell arithmetic (per `paper.md` E2; design_review.md S-8.4, pre-committed so the threshold is never improvised at verdict time):* with k < 12 scorable (non-INCONCLUSIVE) cells, the pass threshold scales proportionally to ≥⌈k/3⌉ of the k scorable cells (preserving the pre-registered 4-of-12 ratio); the requirement that ≥1 qualifying comparison is an AND-gate comparison is unchanged; and a minimum of k ≥ 6 scorable cells is required — below 6, RQ3 itself is declared INCONCLUSIVE rather than scored in either direction.

**Statistical test.** McNemar's exact test on discordant unsound-acceptance indicators, α = 0.05, with Holm–Bonferroni correction across the twelve primary comparisons.

**Expected outcome.** In at least four of the twelve primary comparisons, including at least one conjunctive-gate (AND) comparison, the composed gate's false-acceptance rate exceeds the strictest single gate's by a discordant-pair asymmetry of at least seven percentage points, significant after correction.

**Metrics.** Per-cell false-acceptance rate (FAR) and 95% confidence interval for each gate configuration; McNemar odds ratios with confidence intervals; count of significant cells after Holm correction.

## RQ4 — `rq_hardening_vs_adding`

**Tier:** Mechanism. **Target sub-problem:** `sp_existence_to_prevalence`.

**Target hypothesis (verbatim, matches the Stage 5 `m_budget_matched_attacker` second hypothesis exactly):**
> "For at least one verifier-pair type, FAR of a UTRL-style adversarially hardened single gate is lower than FAR of a naive AND gate of the corresponding unhardened pair by ≥5 percentage points (McNemar exact p<0.05, Holm-corrected within the secondary family), at equal total attack budget — i.e., hardening one check and adding a second check are not interchangeable defenses."

**Setup.** For each verifier-pairing type, an adversarially hardened single-verifier gate is attacked under the identical fixed-attacker configuration and compared, via a paired McNemar exact test, against the naive conjunctive gate of the corresponding unhardened pair, at equal total attack budget.

**Sample-size rationale.** Reuses the same ~200-task × 6-pairing-type grid and attack curves collected for RQ3 (the `m_budget_matched_attacker` shared-instrument design: "the same matched-budget attack curves ... are re-sliced, without any new data collection" — Stage 5 refiner pass), so this RQ is not independently powered with a separate sample; equal total attack budget is enforced specifically between the hardened-single and naive-AND arms for this contrast.

**Statistical test.** Paired McNemar exact test, α = 0.05, Holm-corrected within this secondary comparison family (a distinct correction family from RQ3's primary twelve).

**Expected outcome.** For at least one verifier-pairing type, the hardened single gate's false-acceptance rate is at least five percentage points lower than the naive conjunctive gate's, significant after correction.

**Metrics.** Per-pairing-type false-acceptance rate for the hardened-single and naive-conjunctive configurations, with paired confidence intervals and test statistics.

## RQ5 — `rq_complementarity`

**Tier:** Mechanism. **Target sub-problem:** `sp_emergent_vs_sharedblindspot`.

**Target hypothesis (verbatim, matches the Stage 5 `m_gap_classifier` first hypothesis exactly):**
> "The logistic-regression coefficient of κ on per-cell emergent-gap incidence, controlling for task difficulty and gate type, is positive with Wald p<0.05 (Holm-corrected within the secondary family) and an odds ratio ≥1.5 per 1-SD increase in κ."

**Setup.** For every (task, verifier-pairing) cell in the scale-up grid, a coverage-complementarity score κ = 1 − Jaccard(kill-set_A, kill-set_B), computed over a shared mutant pool, is regressed against per-cell emergent-shortcut incidence via logistic regression, controlling for task difficulty and gate type. *Task difficulty* is the pre-registered covariate from `paper.md`'s term table (design_review.md S-8.6): difficulty(T) = 1 − (fraction of n=50 independent, gate-feedback-free samples from 𝒜_cert's base model that satisfy GT on D), computed once per task and frozen before the Stage-1 sweep launches, so the covariate cannot absorb attack outcomes.

**Sample-size rationale.** Fit across all (task, verifier-pairing) cells in the same scale-up grid used for RQ3/RQ4 — not a separately sampled design. Statistical power depends on the number of cells with a computed κ score and observed emergent-gap incidence, which follows directly from the RQ3 grid rather than a distinct powering exercise.

**Statistical test.** Logistic regression of emergent-gap incidence on κ, controlling for task difficulty and gate type; Wald test on the κ coefficient, α = 0.05, Holm-corrected within the secondary family, with **cluster-robust (task-level) standard errors** (per `paper.md` E3; design_review.md S-8.5) — the same ~200 tasks recur across the six verifier-pair types, so per-cell observations are not independent and naive SEs would overstate precision.

**Expected outcome.** The complementarity coefficient is positive and significant, with an odds ratio of at least 1.5 per one-standard-deviation increase in the complementarity score.

**Metrics.** Logistic-regression coefficient and odds ratio for the complementarity score; Wald test statistic and corrected p-value.

## RQ6 — `rq_taxonomy_concentration`

**Tier:** Mechanism. **Target sub-problem:** `sp_emergent_vs_sharedblindspot`.

**Target hypothesis (verbatim, matches the Stage 5 `m_gap_classifier` second hypothesis exactly):**
> "At least 60% of OR-gate emergent shortcuts fall in the weak-branch taxonomy class and at least 50% of AND-gate emergent shortcuts fall in the constraint-displacement class (exact binomial CI excluding the uniform-over-four-classes rate of 25%, p<0.05)."

**Setup.** Every genuinely emergent shortcut discovered in the scale-up sweep (RQ3's output) is assigned a structural taxonomy label (weak-branch, constraint-displacement, facet-split, or threshold-interaction) from its ablation-triple pattern and verifier-pairing configuration, double-annotated on a human-adjudicated sample with inter-annotator agreement required before scale-up labeling proceeds. Per `paper.md`'s R2 instrument-validation protocol, the four-class taxonomy must itself clear five steps before Stage-1 scale-up: (1) grounding the four classes in the Step 0/C1–C4 case analysis rather than inventing them post hoc from data; (2) drafting wide and refining on a held-out 20-shortcut calibration set (disjoint from the adjudication sample) — with a pre-committed fallback source (design_review.md S-8.3): because the pilot's pass criterion requires only ≥1 shortcut per family in ≥3 of 5 families, the pilot may yield fewer than 20 shortcuts in total, in which case the calibration set is completed from a Stage-1 burn-in sample (the first scale-up-discovered shortcuts), kept disjoint from the 100-shortcut adjudication sample, before any scale-up labeling proceeds; (3) validating against 2 independent human raters on the 100-shortcut adjudication sample, Cohen's κ ≥ 0.7 required; (4) inspecting the confusion structure (disagreements should fall between adjacent mechanism pairs, not collapse onto one class); (5) freezing the taxonomy (hashing class definitions and calibration-set labels) before the scale-up sweep launches.

**Sample-size rationale.** N is determined by however many genuinely emergent shortcuts RQ3's scale-up sweep discovers — not independently set. Taxonomy labels are double-annotated on a human-adjudicated 100-shortcut sample (shared with the A1 oracle-validity check), requiring Cohen's κ ≥ 0.7 before scale-up labeling proceeds.

**Statistical test.** Exact binomial confidence interval per class proportion against the uniform-baseline rate of 25% (1-of-4 classes), α = 0.05.

**Expected outcome.** At least 60% of disjunctive-gate (OR) emergent shortcuts fall in the weak-branch class, and at least 50% of conjunctive-gate (AND) emergent shortcuts fall in the constraint-displacement class, each significantly above the 25% rate expected under a uniform distribution over the four classes.

**Metrics.** Per-class proportion of emergent shortcuts for each composition rule, with exact binomial confidence intervals against the uniform baseline.
