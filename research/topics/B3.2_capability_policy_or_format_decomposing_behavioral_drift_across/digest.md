## N5 Challenges

[HYPOTHESIS, tightly derived from the core question — problem-inherent obstacles, not prior-tool failures; ids C1–C4 stable from the previous digest revision, C5 added]

- **C1**: "Capability, policy, and format changes are entangled in any single observed output — the same behavioral delta (a different final answer, a new refusal) can be produced by any of the three causes alone or in combination — and no ground-truth label attaches a given output difference to a cause, so decomposition validity can never be checked directly on the deployed pairs of interest." — source=inferred
- **C2**: "Commercial version pairs are black-box: no weights, no logits, no training data, no changelogs, no policy documents. Causal attribution can only be inferred from input/output behavior, never verified against vendor internals." — source=inferred
- **C3**: "Probe orthogonality is itself an open measurement-design problem: a probe meant to isolate one axis (e.g., format) can leak another (e.g., verbosity changes altering apparent correctness under lenient scoring), and the leakage is invisible from probe outputs alone — it can only be bounded by construction (format-robust scoring) or estimated via calibration on pairs with known dominant axis." — source=inferred
- **C4**: "'Capability', 'policy', and 'format' may not be commensurable constructs across vendors — what one vendor implements as a policy-level refusal rule, another may implement as an output-filter or a system-prompt change — threatening whether any 'common structure' found across vendors reflects a real shared taxonomy or an artifact of how the axes were operationalized." — source=inferred
- **C5**: "Within-version behavioral variance (decoding stochasticity, server-side non-determinism, silent infra changes) can masquerade as cross-version drift: a decomposition is meaningless unless the between-version signal is first separated from the run-to-run noise floor of each version." — source=inferred

## N6 Methodology

[HYPOTHESIS — extrapolated from the seed's minimal experiment ("设计三类正交探针，对多个版本对做方差分解；检验分解在厂商间是否有共同结构"), made operational at the level of specificity of published decomposition studies (cf. the aptitude/unreliability split in "LLMs Get Lost in Multi-Turn Conversation" and the metric-swap design in "Are Emergent Abilities a Mirage?")]

**overview**: "**Staged plan.** *Stage 1 (pilot = the seed's minimal experiment):* build the three probe batteries (M1–M3), run them with k=5 repeated samples per item on 4 version pairs — one commercial pair with both snapshots still served (e.g., `gpt-4o-2024-08-06` vs `gpt-4o-2024-11-20`) and three open-weight control pairs with known dominant axis (M6) — fit the variance-decomposition model (M4), and check dominant-axis recovery on the controls. *Stage 2 (scale-up):* extend to ≥6 real version pairs from ≥3 vendors (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google snapshot-pinned endpoints; Llama-3→3.1 and Qwen2→2.5 as open-weight anchors), expand controls to ≥9, and run the cross-vendor structural comparison (M5). For every pair, each probe item is submitted identically to both versions; per-item behavioral deltas are aggregated per axis; M4 apportions total measured drift into capability/policy/format shares plus an explicit leakage/residual term and a repeated-run noise floor; M5 tests whether the axis-share structure is shared across vendors." — source=inferred

**key_components:**
- **M1** — "Capability probe battery": ~1,500 fixed items with objectively verifiable answers — 500 GSM8K math problems, 500 MMLU-Pro questions, 300 HumanEval + LiveCodeBench coding tasks (execution-tested), 200 SimpleQA-style factual questions — scored with *format-robust extractors* (code executed after fence-stripping; numeric answers matched after normalization; MCQ via constrained answer extraction) so the capability score is invariant to surface formatting by construction. Measured quantity: per-item paired correctness delta (v_new − v_old), k=5 samples per item per version. — source=inferred
- **M2** — "Policy probe battery": ~1,000 prompts stratified into (a) 250 safe-but-seemingly-unsafe items (XSTest safe subset), (b) 400 pseudo-harmful items (OR-Bench-Hard-1K sample), (c) 350 genuinely harmful requests (SORRY-Bench sample). Each response classified 3-way (comply / refuse / redirect-hedge) by an LLM judge with a fixed rubric, validated against human labels on a 600-item stratified sample. Measured quantity: per-stratum change in refusal/compliance rates; false-refusal change on stratum (a)–(b) vs true-refusal change on stratum (c) separates policy tightening from policy loosening. — source=inferred
- **M3** — "Format/style probe battery": ~500 prompts on which both versions are near-ceiling correct (verified in a pre-pass), plus the IFEval formatting-instruction subset for instructed-format compliance. Measured surface features per response: length in tokens, markdown density (headers/bullets/code fences per 100 tokens), list-vs-prose rate, hedging-phrase frequency (lexicon count), JSON-schema compliance rate, refusal-boilerplate templates. Measured quantity: standardized mean difference per feature between versions; format drift = norm of the standardized feature-delta vector. — source=inferred
- **M4** — "Variance-decomposition model with noise floor and leakage term": a hierarchical mixed-effects model over per-item deltas with axis (probe family) as fixed effect and item as random effect. The k=5 repeated samples per version estimate the within-version variance floor (addresses C5): an axis's contribution counts only in excess of that floor. Cross-axis leakage is estimated explicitly by regressing capability-item deltas on the item's format-feature deltas (and policy deltas on lexical surface cues); the explained portion is reassigned to a reported leakage/residual share rather than silently absorbed. Output per pair: (capability%, policy%, format%, leakage%, noise-floor) with bootstrap CIs (1,000 resamples over items). — source=inferred
- **M5** — "Cross-vendor structural comparison": each version pair is summarized as a point on the 3-simplex of axis shares; common structure is tested via (a) Kendall's W over the rank-ordering of axis dominance across pairs grouped by vendor, with a permutation test (10,000 permutations), and (b) hierarchical clustering of simplex points to check whether pairs cluster by vendor or mix. — source=inferred
- **M6** — "Ground-truth calibration on constructed control pairs": ≥9 open-weight 'synthetic version pairs' with dominant axis known by construction — capability controls (base vs the same model finetuned on domain data, e.g., math-SFT), policy controls (base vs safety-SFT per published recipes, following the training setups in [ContrastiveOverRefusal, OpenReview:GXcN0MuN1q]-style safety tuning), format controls (same weights, altered system prompt / template forcing style change). The full M1–M4 pipeline runs blind on these pairs; dominant-axis recovery accuracy calibrates the decomposition and upper-bounds probe leakage before any claim is made about real vendor pairs. — source=inferred
