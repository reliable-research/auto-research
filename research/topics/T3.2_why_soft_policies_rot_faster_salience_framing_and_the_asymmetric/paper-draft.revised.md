# Why Soft Policies Rot Faster: Attributing the Asymmetric Decay of In-Context Constraints to Salience, Framing, or the Alignment Prior

## Abstract

Deployed LLM agents carry two tiers of behavioral constraints: soft organizational policies (tone, scope, business rules) that live only in the context window, and hard safety norms that are additionally reinforced in model weights during alignment. Recent measurements report that soft constraints lose behavioral hold far faster than hard norms as interactions grow long and as context is compacted — Governance Decay reports an 8.3x soft/hard difference under compaction — but existing work measures or mitigates this asymmetry without explaining it. We propose the first controlled mechanism-attribution study of the soft/hard decay asymmetry. On a bank of soft/hard constraint pairs equated for length, specificity, and rule-checkable compliance — with class membership defined operationally by no-injection compliance rather than by wording — we factorially manipulate salience (position, repetition) and framing (advisory vs. imperative wording) in two regimes: natural context growth, where the constraint stays verbatim in context, and counterfactual compaction replay, where the same frozen histories are re-probed under keep-top truncation, unconstrained summarization, verbatim-pinning summarization, and deletion at matched token budgets. The alignment prior, which cannot be manipulated, is triangulated through three measured proxies (no-injection compliance floor, base-vs-instruct twin delta, and a preregistered compaction-deletion natural experiment: when constraint text is deleted, soft adherence should collapse to the no-injection floor while hard adherence should barely move). All headline compliance is scored by frozen rule-based parsers; probe reactivity is bounded empirically before the main run; and the study executes in preregistered stages with go/no-go gates and an explicit budget envelope. The outcome is a dominance decomposition of the soft/hard decay gap over salience, framing, prior, and compaction-operator survival, with every mitigation-relevant implication stated per mechanism: if salience dominates, pinning and restatement fix the rot; if the prior dominates, no prompt engineering can, and durable soft policy requires training-time support.

## 1. Introduction

Long-running LLM agents are governed by in-context instructions. Some of these instructions — refusal behaviors, harm-avoidance norms — are *hard*: they are reinforced in the model's weights during alignment training, and the context merely restates them. Others — "always disclose you are an AI to customers," "never quote prices without legal review," house style and scope rules — are *soft*: the context window is the only place they exist. Any operation on the context is therefore an existential threat to soft policies in a way it is not to weight-backed norms. This asymmetry is structural, and production systems live exactly where it bites: every agent that runs long enough must compact its history, and compaction operators delete or rewrite constraint text by construction.

The phenomenon is no longer folklore. Governance Decay [Anon., 2026] measures compaction-induced violation on its ConstraintRot benchmark and reports decay 8.3x larger for soft organizational policies than for hard safety norms; Omission Constraints Decay While Commission Constraints Persist [Gamage, 2026] documents a related class asymmetry under context growth, with prohibition compliance falling from 73% to 33% across conversation depth while requirement compliance holds; Lost in Compaction [Wang et al., 2026] shows that production compactors retain only ~17% of session constraints, with retention systematically sensitive to constraint phrasing and injection position. The mitigation response is equally established: pin the system prompt, re-inject policies after compaction, restate every k turns. What is missing is the *why*. No existing study attributes the soft/hard asymmetry to a mechanism, and the shipped fixes are therefore applied blindly: re-pinning treats a salience problem, but if the asymmetry is driven by wording register or by the alignment prior itself, salience fixes treat the wrong cause and will fail silently — while hard-norm audit signals stay green.

Three candidate mechanisms are on the table:

1. **Salience.** The constraint's contextual prominence — its position, repetition count, and, under compaction, the literal survival of its text through the operator — erodes as histories grow and are rewritten.
2. **Framing.** The wording register in which the constraint is stated (advisory "we prefer that you..." vs. imperative "you must never...") modulates how strongly it binds behavior over time.
3. **Alignment prior.** Hard norms are backed by weight-level reinforcement; soft policies are in-context only. The decayable surface of a soft policy is the whole constraint; for a hard norm it is only the contextual restatement.

These mechanisms imply different fixes, different failure modes, and different research programs. Yet they are confounded in every deployed setting: hard norms are typically imperatively worded *and* prominently placed *and* weight-reinforced. Only controlled manipulation on matched constraint pairs can separate them — and the third mechanism can never be manipulated, only measured, so the design must be explicitly asymmetric: two experimental factors, one triangulated covariate, with the attribution to the prior stated as a diagnosis of exclusion bounded by its strongest quasi-experimental evidence.

This paper contributes that design and its staged execution plan:

- **A two-regime, mechanism-factorial design.** Regime G (growth-only): the constraint stays verbatim in context while turns accumulate; decay reflects attention/behavioral drift. Regime C (compaction): the same frozen histories are re-probed after keep-top truncation, unconstrained summarization, verbatim-pinning summarization, or deletion at matched token budgets; decay additionally reflects textual survival through the operator — a property of the *operator*, which we log as a measured mediator rather than conflating it with model-side salience.
- **An operationally defined class contrast on a matched pair bank.** "Hard" is defined by measurable no-injection compliance (the model already enforces it without being told), not by wording; soft/hard pairs are matched on topic, length, syntax, and rule-checkable verifiability, so the class factor is not a surface-form confound. Wording is then manipulated *within* class, which is what makes framing separable from class membership.
- **The compaction-deletion natural experiment as co-headline.** When an operator deletes the constraint text outright, mechanism 3 makes a sharp, falsifiable prediction: soft adherence collapses to its no-injection floor; hard adherence barely moves. This is the cleanest available demonstration that the prior, not the visible text, carries hard norms — and it doubles as the required benchmarking of deletion against a measured no-injection baseline rather than an assumed floor.
- **Mitigation arms inside the mechanism design.** The pinning-repair fixes practitioners already ship (verbatim pinning, every-k restatement) are run as arms against the factorial, so the mechanism result directly re-prices existing mitigations.
- **A staged, gated, budgeted plan.** A 40-pair pilot on 2 models with four preregistered go/no-go gates (source verification and precedent search; probe-reactivity bound; directional gap replication; attribution stability) precedes any scale-up, inside an explicit envelope (≤$3k API, ≤10 A100-days, ≤60 annotator-hours). Probe reactivity — the instrument refreshing the salience it measures — is bounded empirically *first*, because it gates the validity of everything downstream.

We deliberately do not claim to point-identify the alignment prior. The prior is attributed only as bounded evidence: the residual class gap that survives salience and framing manipulation, corroborated by the no-injection floor, the base-vs-instruct twin delta, and the deletion natural experiment, with all unresolved states reported as unresolved rather than converted into support.

## 2. Related Work

**What decays.** Multi-turn degradation of instruction following is established in aggregate: Laban et al. [2025] show sharp multi-turn performance loss; Luz de Araujo et al. [2026] show persona fidelity and instruction following degrading over 100+ round dialogues; Li et al. [2024] measure system-instruction drift within eight rounds and associate it with attention decay; LIFBench [Wu et al., 2024] and Multi-IF [He et al., 2024] quantify long-context and multi-turn instruction instability; SysBench [Qin et al., 2024] measures multi-turn system-message constraint violation. None of these stratify decay by constraint class or attribute it to a mechanism.

**Class-asymmetric decay (the direct predecessors).** Gamage [2026] (arXiv:2604.20911) establishes omission/commission divergence under context growth and — via token-matched padding controls — performs a *partial* mechanism decomposition, attributing 62–100% of dilution to schema-semantic content over token volume. Governance Decay (arXiv:2606.22528) establishes the 8.3x soft/hard compaction asymmetry, the Compaction-Eviction attack, and Constraint Pinning as a defense. Lost in Compaction [Wang et al., 2026] (arXiv:2608.11242) measures session-constraint survival through production compactors and shows retention depends on phrasing and injection position; a compression-focused benchmark [2512.17920] separates constraint compliance from semantic accuracy under compression. These works measure the asymmetry, attack it, or patch it; none runs a matched soft/hard design that manipulates salience and framing factorially, none separates growth-only decay from compaction-induced loss on identical histories, and none tests the alignment-prior account against the alternatives. The closest partial overlap — position/phrasing sensitivity of constraint *survival through the compactor* [2608.11242] — concerns the operator side of salience; our design logs operator survival as a mediator and asks the remaining, unasked question: why does the *model* hold hard norms and drop soft ones even when the text's fate is held fixed?

**Candidate mechanisms in isolation.** Salience: Lost in the Middle [Liu et al., 2023] (position); Prompt Repetition [Leviathan et al., 2025] (repetition); attention-decay accounts of drift [Li et al., 2024]. Framing: politeness and register effects on model behavior [Yin et al., 2024]; framing-sensitivity studies in 2026 confirm wording register shifts model decisions. Prior: shallow safety alignment [Qi et al., 2024] and the Superficial Safety Alignment Hypothesis [Li & Kim, 2024] characterize how thin the weight-level prior is; the Instruction Hierarchy [Wallace et al., 2024] trains privilege ordering into weights. Each line studies exactly one mechanism and therefore cannot see, let alone explain, the class asymmetry.

**Apparatus.** IFEval [Zhou et al., 2023] supplies the rule-checkable verification pattern our mechanical scoring generalizes; MemGPT [Packer et al., 2023] and the context-compression literature define the operator families our Regime C instantiates; LIMA [Zhou et al., 2023] motivates the base-vs-instruct proxy for alignment-induced behavior.

**Gap.** Each ingredient exists separately — class-asymmetric decay measurements, single-mechanism studies, compaction operators, pinning fixes — but no work compares soft vs. hard decay in one matched design, manipulates salience and framing factorially, separates growth from compaction on identical histories, and tests the prior account with a no-injection-benchmarked deletion contrast. That conjunction is this study.

## 3. Problem Setup

Let a constraint pair $(c^S, c^H)$ be matched on topic, length ($\pm 10\%$ tokens), syntax, and verifier type, with class defined operationally: $A^{\varnothing}_H \geq \alpha_H$ and $A^{\varnothing}_S \leq \alpha_S$, where $A^{\varnothing}_k$ is no-injection compliance (the model's spontaneous adherence with the constraint never stated) and $\alpha_H, \alpha_S$ are preregistered thresholds. Each conversation is a turn sequence under condition $e = (m, i, k, r, s, f, q)$: model $m$, pair $i$, class $k \in \{S,H\}$, regime $r \in \{G, C_\kappa\}$ with compaction operator $\kappa$, salience $s$ (position $\times$ repetition), framing $f \in \{\text{advisory}, \text{imperative}\}$, and visibility $q \in \{\text{present}, \text{deleted}, \text{absent}\}$.

Compliance $d_i^k(y_t) \in \{0,1\}$ is scored by a frozen deterministic rule; the headline metric admits no judge model. With $A_e(t)$ the per-turn adherence probability, decay is $D_e(t_0,t) = A_e(t_0) - A_e(t)$ and the class asymmetry is $\Gamma = D_S - D_H$ on matched conditions. The incremental effect of compaction for class $k$ is the difference-in-differences $J_k = [A_{C,k}(t^-) - A_{C,k}(t^+)] - [A_{G,k}(t^-) - A_{G,k}(t^+)]$ across a compaction event, so $J_S - J_H$ isolates class-dependent compaction loss from growth decay. The deletion contrast $R_k(t) = A_{q=\text{deleted},k}(t) - A_{q=\text{absent},k}(t)$ is always interpreted against the measured no-injection baseline $A_{q=\text{absent},k}$, never an assumed floor. The attribution target is a decomposition of $\Gamma$'s variance over {salience, framing, prior-proxy covariates, operator survival, residual}, with a mechanism credited only when its controlled contrast is operative, distinguishable from the others, and consistent with its preregistered falsifiable prediction.

## 4. Method

*Figure 1: Two-regime mechanism-attribution architecture. A matched soft/hard constraint bank (arms: injected, cue-only null, no-injection) feeds two treatment regimes — growth-only and counterfactual compaction replay (keep-top, summarize, summarize+pin, deletion) — probed by frozen rule-based parsers; a mixed-effects attribution stage decomposes the class gap into salience, framing, and alignment-prior shares. The whole pipeline executes under preregistered gates G0–G3 within a fixed budget envelope.*

### 4.1 Matched constraint-pair bank (construct-validity linchpin)

The bank contains 40 soft/hard pairs (pilot: 20), drawn from operational-policy and preference-style soft constraints and standard refusal/format hard constraints, restricted to behaviors with rule-checkable compliance criteria in the IFEval style. Matching requirements: same topic domain, token length within $\pm 10\%$, same verifier type, and three wording variants per member (advisory / standard / imperative) screened for semantic equivalence by embedding threshold plus human audit. Acceptance gate: independent raters can identify each pair member's *class* but cannot distinguish their *difficulty*. Class labels are then validated operationally against the no-injection battery (Section 4.4); pairs whose members do not separate on $A^{\varnothing}$ are relabeled or dropped, because a "hard" constraint the model does not spontaneously enforce is just an imperatively worded soft constraint.

We state the known cost: restricting soft constraints to mechanically checkable predicates narrows the construct — graded tone policies are underrepresented. LLM-judged soft constraints are run as a separately reported secondary stratum with a 10% human audit at $\kappa \geq 0.8$, and never enter the headline number.

### 4.2 Treatments: two regimes, one harness

**Regime G (growth-only).** The constraint is placed at one of {system-head, system-tail, mid-conversation}, restated {1, 3, 5} times at scheduled turns, in one of the framing variants; conversations run 30 turns (pilot) / 100 turns (scale-up), padded with LMSYS-Chat-1M distractor turns; total context tokens are held constant across salience cells via length-matched filler. The constraint text remains verbatim in context throughout, so decay here is model-side drift.

**Regime C (compaction replay).** At scheduled checkpoints, the assigned operator rewrites the transcript: `keep-top` (retain system prompt + last k turns verbatim), `summarize` (fixed summarization prompt over the evicted span), or `delete` (constraint clause removed, everything else summarized identically). Compacted arms are matched in post-compaction token budget. Whether the constraint text survived each compaction verbatim / paraphrased / deleted is logged as the **textual-survival mediator**: under `summarize`, survival varies naturally within cell, and we exploit that variation in a mediation analysis rather than pretending the cell is homogeneous. This is the design's answer to the operator-vs-model ambiguity: salience-as-survival (operator property) and salience-as-attention (model property) are estimated separately.

**Mitigation arms (outside the factorial, analyzed against it).** `summarize+pin` (constraint clauses verbatim-pinned through summarization) and `every-k restatement` instantiate the two fixes practitioners already ship. Each mechanism outcome makes a prediction about them: salience-dominant decay is largely repaired by both; framing-dominant decay is repaired by neither unless wording is also strengthened; prior-dominant decay is repaired by neither.

### 4.3 Probing and mechanical scoring, with a measured reactivity bound

Adherence is probed every 5 turns with probe prompts authored under an anti-reactivity rule (no constraint keywords or topic lexemes). Because probing can itself refresh constraint salience, the reactivity calibration runs *first*: on 4 flagged cells (~480 conversations), we contrast within-conversation probing against between-conversation probing (fresh conversations truncated at each probe depth). If the delta is small and type-uniform, a global correction is applied; if it is type-dependent, the design escalates to between-conversation probing for the headline contrasts and the preregistered cut (drop the `summarize` operator from this submission) pays for it. Violation detection in the headline metric is rule-based only — format, lexical, and refusal-trigger checkers validated once against blinded labels ($\geq 0.95$ exact agreement) and then frozen.

### 4.4 Mechanism decomposition and the prior triangulation

The trajectory model is a discrete-time hazard (cloglog link; event = first sustained violation; right-censoring), with mixed effects: hazard ~ turn $\times$ class $\times$ framing $\times$ salience $\times$ operator + (1|pair) + (1|model), conditional on pair-level no-injection compliance. The soft/hard gap is the class$\times$turn coefficient; mechanism shares come from dominance analysis / partial-$R^2$ over the manipulated factors, with a mixed-effects logistic trajectory refit as a preregistered sensitivity analysis (the two frameworks must agree directionally; disagreement is reported, not resolved silently).

For interpretability we retain the decomposition identity of the cue-only null arm on a subset of pairs: with $C_{k,\text{inj}}$ and $C_{k,\text{null}}$ compliance under full injection and under a class-cue-only scaffold, $G_{\text{raw}} = G_{\text{null}} + G_{\text{lift}}$ splits the raw class gap into cue-associated class support and visible-instruction lift. $G_{\text{null}}$ is reported strictly as a deontic-cue-inclusive *upper bound* on prior support.

The alignment prior itself is triangulated through three measured instruments, none manipulable, each with a falsifiable signature:

1. **No-injection floor $A^{\varnothing}_k$.** Also the operational class definition; entered as a continuous covariate, so "prior strength" is per-constraint, not per-label.
2. **Base-vs-instruct twin delta.** On one open-weights pair, $A^{\varnothing}_{\text{instruct}} - A^{\varnothing}_{\text{base}}$ isolates alignment-induced spontaneous enforcement from pretraining-corpus regularity.
3. **Compaction-deletion natural experiment (co-headline contrast, preregistered).** After the `delete` operator removes the constraint clause: prediction under mechanism 3 is $A_{\text{deleted},S} \rightarrow A^{\varnothing}_S$ (soft collapses to its no-injection floor) while $A_{\text{deleted},H} \approx A_{\text{present},H}$ (hard adherence barely moves, because the prior — not the text — enforces it). If instead hard adherence also collapses toward its floor, the prior account is falsified and the asymmetry must be carried by salience/framing; if soft adherence stays elevated after deletion, residual-context leakage is diagnosed before any mechanism claim.

Attribution to the prior is a diagnosis of exclusion — the class gap that survives after manipulated factors and operator survival are partialed out — stated as an upper bound and corroborated (or not) by instruments 1–3. We do not deploy a cue-free affirmation tower with unverifiable identification; where the evidence cannot separate prior from residual predicate structure, the ledger says "unresolved," and that word appears in the abstract of the eventual paper if it is the outcome.

## 5. Experimental Design: staged, gated, budgeted

**Pilot (weeks 1–3).** 2 models (1 frontier API snapshot, 1 open-weights instruct) $\times$ class(2) $\times$ framing(2: advisory/imperative) $\times$ position(2) $\times$ repetition(2: 1/3) $\times$ operator(2: none/keep-top) = 64 cells, 20 pairs nested (10 per half-fraction), 3 seeds, 30 turns — about 1,900–2,300 conversations per model. Two- and three-way interactions with class are estimable; higher-order interactions are aliased by declared design.

**Gates (preregistered, with named cuts).**
- **G0 (week 1):** trace the reported 8.3x/asymmetry sources (now resolved: arXiv:2606.22528, 2604.20911) and complete the precedent search; verifier suite $\kappa \geq 0.8$ on the audit sample; 5-pair smoke run.
- **G1 (weeks 2–3):** reactivity calibration. Small + type-uniform delta → within-conversation probing stands. Type-dependent → escalate to between-conversation probing and cut the `summarize` operator from this submission.
- **G2 (weeks 3–4):** directional gap replication in growth-only cells (soft half-life < hard half-life) and at least one detectable factor$\times$class interaction. Fail → the paper pivots to "the asymmetry folklore does not survive matched measurement," itself a publishable null.
- **G3 (week 5):** attribution stable under Weibull refit and leave-one-pair-out.

**Scale-up (weeks 4–6).** 3 models (2 dated API snapshots + 1 open-weights instruct with base twin), framing 3-level, repetition {1,3,5}, `summarize` operator with textual survival as measured mediator, mitigation arms, 100-turn horizon on a fractional subset; ~20k conversations total. Family-level generalization beyond 3 models is explicitly deferred.

**Budget envelope.** ≤$3k API + ≤10 A100-days + ≤60 annotator-hours. All analysis code, the pair bank, thresholds, and gate criteria are frozen and hashed before scale-up; every cell is reported.

**Research questions.** RQ1: Does the soft/hard decay asymmetry replicate directionally on matched, mechanically scored pairs (growth-only)? RQ2: How much of the asymmetry do salience and framing manipulations close (can a boosted soft constraint match a standard hard one)? RQ3: Does compaction add class-dependent loss beyond growth ($J_S - J_H > 0$), and how much of it is mediated by textual survival through the operator? RQ4: Does the deletion natural experiment show the prior signature (soft → floor, hard ≈ unchanged)? RQ5: Which mechanism shares dominate the fitted hazard gap, and are they stable (G3)? RQ6: Do the pinning-repair mitigation arms behave as the winning mechanism predicts?

## 6. Threats to Validity

**Construct.** Mechanically checkable soft constraints under-cover graded tone policy; the LLM-judged stratum is reported separately, never in the headline. Class is operational (no-injection compliance), so "hard" is model-relative; per-model class labels are reported. **Internal.** The prior is correlational by construction; we bound it with three instruments and state attribution as exclusion. Probe reactivity is measured, not assumed away, and gates the design. Textual survival under `summarize` is operator-random; it is modeled as a mediator, not averaged over. **External.** Three models, two families; production black-box compactors are out of scope (Tier-3, stated); scripted operators are chosen to bracket the production behaviors (truncation, summarization, pinning). **Statistical.** The factorial is fractional and declared; hazard and logistic frameworks must agree directionally; all thresholds preregistered; every unresolved state is published as unresolved.

## 7. Conclusion

The soft/hard constraint-decay asymmetry has been measured, attacked, and patched — but never explained. This study turns it into a mechanism-attribution problem on matched pairs, with two experimental factors (salience, framing), one triangulated covariate (the alignment prior), two regimes (growth, compaction) on identical histories, mitigation arms priced by the result, and a staged execution plan whose gates make each failure mode a stated pivot rather than a silent one. Whichever mechanism dominates, the answer is actionable: placement and pinning guidance if salience; wording guidance if framing; and, if the prior dominates, the negative result that prompt-level policy is intrinsically second-class — in which case durable soft policy requires training-time reinforcement, and every context-only governance stack in production is operating on borrowed time.
