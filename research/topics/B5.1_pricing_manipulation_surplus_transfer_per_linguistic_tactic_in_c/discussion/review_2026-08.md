## 3. Feasibility findings (se-paper-reviewer lens)

- **[moderate] F1 — paraphrase random effect unidentifiable at ≥2 levels.** `paper_draft.md` RQ6 / Methodology and `experiment_design.md` A6 specify "two or more phrasing variants and two or more intensity levels, modeled as random effects." A variance component estimated from 2 levels is statistically meaningless (standard mixed-model guidance wants ≥5 levels); RQ6's within-vs-across-tactic variance-ratio verdict would be built on an unestimable quantity. Fix: raise to ≥5 phrasing variants per tactic, or pre-commit to fixed-effect phrasing modeling. **Applied to draft (md+tex).** `experiment_design.md` §RQ6/A6 should be updated to match in the next spec pass (out of this pass's edit scope — flagged here).
- **[moderate] F2 — interaction tests powered on a main-effect basis; no total budget stated.** A4's n≈200/cell is derived for detecting ΔS≈0.10 as a *main effect* at 80% power. RQ4 and RQ7 are decided by *interaction* LRTs (tactic × model; sophistication × tactic); an interaction of the same magnitude needs roughly 4× the per-cell sample. Also, no total dialogue/token budget appears anywhere: the implied grid (4 tactics × ~6 models × 200, ×3 arms, + 3 sophistication × 4 tactics × 200, + paraphrase variants) is on the order of 2×10⁴–3×10⁴ dialogues ≈ low-10⁸ tokens — feasible but must be stated for a pre-registration to be auditable. **Applied to draft (md+tex): scale-and-power paragraph added.**
- **[minor] F3 — RQ5's scale/alignment-tier claim rests on ~6 models.** Six points cannot support a "systematic relationship to scale/alignment" inference; the draft's falsification wording is acceptable but the result should be reported as descriptive ranking, not a fitted trend. Not revised (minor).
- **[minor] F4 — GLEE substrate may not satisfy the concealment constraint natively;** the draft correctly designates the synthetic substrate as the only by-construction-concealed one, but RQ1's leakage probe is specified only over "all tactic conditions," not per substrate. Not revised (minor; walking-skeleton gate (i) covers the primary substrate).
