## 3. Feasibility + staleness findings (se-paper-reviewer lens)

| ID | Severity | Finding |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | **[major]** | **Unattributable headline premise.** The abstract's first sentence asserts "a widely cited report puts the former's behavioral decay at roughly eight times the latter's." That figure traces only to the internal seed (`38_topics.txt` line 265: "原论文报告约 8 倍差异") where the source paper is unnamed; no repo file names it, and targeted arXiv searches found no such report. Calling an untraceable number "widely cited" is a claim–evidence gap on the paper's opening line, and the entire estimand is anchored to it. The design already contains the right remedy (the no-manipulation replication arm) — but the prose treated the premise as settled fact rather than as a first-class replication target. **Fix applied** (see §5): premise re-hedged in abstract, intro, and conclusion; gap replication made an explicit precondition for every mechanism claim; source listed under citation debt. |
| F2 | **[moderate]** | **Related-work staleness / missed neighbor.** Prompt Design at Scale (arXiv:2607.19257, 2026-07-21, verified against the live arXiv API) is a controlled crossing of instruction count × format × placement × context length on instruction adherence — the nearest *interventional* neighbor to this paper's salience factor block, and it post-dates the draft. Without a concede-and-claim sentence, a reviewer finds it first. **Fix applied:** concede-and-claim sentence added to Related Work (and mirrored in intro's nearest-work framing is unchanged — PrefEval/TimeToInconsistency remain the two closest on the estimand); jsonl record appended (`verified.id_resolves = true`, checked 2026-08-21). |
| F3 | **[moderate]** | **RQ6 calibration arm is underpowered for its own equivalence bound.** The gate that everything else waits on runs a TOST with bounds [0.80, 1.25] on a regime-by-type interaction hazard ratio estimated from ≈480 single-probe conversations (≈120 per regime×type margin). Back-of-envelope: with per-cell event rates in the 30–50% range, the log-HR standard error for the interaction is ≈0.3–0.4, giving a 95% CI of multiplicative width ≈ exp(±0.6–0.8) ≈ [0.5, 2.2] around any point estimate — it cannot fit inside [0.80, 1.25] (half-width log 1.25 ≈ 0.223) regardless of the true effect. As written, the gate fails by default and the pre-registered consequence (rerun everything between-conversation) fires spuriously — a budget catastrophe, since the between-conversation design is the expensive arm. **Fix applied:** power arithmetic acknowledged in RQ6's setup; calibration n re-labeled a floor with a pre-committed sequential escalation (up to ~10× via group-sequential expansion, pooling over probe turns) and an explicit distinction between "equivalence rejected" (CI outside bounds because the estimate is far from 1) and "underpowered width" (CI wide but centered near 1) — only the former triggers the between-conversation rerun. |
| F4 | [minor] | `stage_review.md` Stage 5 quotes a "quotable contribution sentence" ("...three physically distinct carriers") that does not appear anywhere in `paper_draft.md`/`.tex` — the stage review over-claims what the draft contains. Provenance nit in a review artifact, not in the paper; no revision manufactured. |
| F5 | [minor] | No dedicated Threats-to-Validity section; threats are folded into the conclusion's two-limitation paragraph and per-RQ falsification conditions. Acceptable for a pre-registration spec; a venue submission will need the construct/internal/external/conclusion taxonomy made explicit (material already exists in digest N10). |
