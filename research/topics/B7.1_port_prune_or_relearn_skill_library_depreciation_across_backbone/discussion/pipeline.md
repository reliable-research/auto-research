## Citation grounding policy (deviation record)

Consequently: (a) every 2026 work is cited **exactly as recorded in this repo's verified scan artifacts** (`README.md` lines 24–35; `trend_scan_2026-08.md` §2.3/§6; the fuller title of arXiv:2605.10990 resolved from sibling topic B3.1's verified source list, which fetched it when network was available) — shorthand titles preserved verbatim with `Anonymous` authorship and a bib `note` marking them scan-record entries; no author lists, venues, or expanded titles were invented; (b) works the scan mentions but never names were dropped from the citable set entirely (fewer real citations beat many fabricated ones); (c) the 7 non-2026 references are canonical published works cited from stable knowledge (all pre-cutoff, all with their real arXiv IDs).
