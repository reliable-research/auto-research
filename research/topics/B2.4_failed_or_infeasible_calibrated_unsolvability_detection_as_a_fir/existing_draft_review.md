## Architecture (canonical = ZB2.4_e)

Three stacked stages, explicitly a *measurement instrument*, not a new attributor:

1. **Certified pair bank.** For each task: apply exactly one typed mechanical mutation to machine-readable environment artifacts — `drop_schema_field`, `deregister_tool`, `delete_resource`, `inject_contradiction` — with the NL task prompt byte-identical across pair members. Solvable member = same-type *distractor* mutation on a goal-irrelevant, locus-matched element, certified by re-executing the reference solution post-distractor (witness). Infeasible member certified per-member: Tier-1 graph-cut proof over a dependency graph compiled from the same declarative action spec as the executor (soundness contingent on an over-approximation invariant that is itself regression-checked); Tier-2 empirical (conformance suite + closure probes + mandatory manual audit); contradiction mutations via CSP/SMT UNSAT (Z3) on a decidable fragment. Fatal elements must sit at dependency depth ≥ 2 with no lexical mention in the goal. Leakage audit: bag-of-n-grams LR, frozen-encoder probe, fine-tuned LM, goal-lexical-overlap classifier, plus an oracle-feature positive control (must exceed 0.90), on goal-masked and goal-included surfaces, gated at AUROC < 0.60 with a single permutation-calibrated confidence level controlling family-wise false-invalidation ≤ 0.10. Scale: D ≥ 3 domains (≥ 2 Tier-1), ≥ 50 pairs/domain floor, calibration domains extended toward ≥ 250 infeasible tasks; 100-pair two-annotator blinded audit with 0-error gate.
2. **Frozen trace bank + attribution harness.** Executor agents (pool ≥ 3, disjoint from judges) run both members; controls require ≥ 100 *genuine* failures on solvable members per cell-domain (round-robin replacement ladder, disclosed attrition). Then M = 4 strategy archetypes (single-pass, CoT-then-verdict, self-critique, evidence-citing; binary-search deliberately excluded with rationale) × K = 5 judge backbones (≥ 3 open-weight, ≥ 2 families, ≥ 1 OOM scale span), two label conditions differing by one enumerated line (`unsolvable` present vs absent = B0-a control). Metrics: **FBR-with-label**, CoR, **net selectivity S = (FBR_without − FBR_with) − CoR_with**, McNemar per cell; room-level verdict mapped into the five disjoint-exhaustive regions to adjudicate *label-space defect vs capability defect*. Pre-registered thresholds: FBR floor 20%, S ≥ 40 pts, CoR median < 15% / ceiling 30%.
3. **Calibration layer.** Three co-registered confidence signals (k=20 self-consistency vote share, directly elicited 0–100 P(unsolvable), full-option softmax logprobs on open-weight judges); per-domain frozen threshold C*_d selected on a calibration split, evaluated held-out for ≥ 50% coverage at ≤ 5% risk; a strictly **pre-verdict hidden-state probe** (leave-one-domain-out + mutation-type holdout) tests for a decodable-but-unexpressed infeasibility signal (probe − strongest expressed signal ≥ 0.10 AUROC).

Ground-truth mechanism is fully mechanical (spec-only certificates; no human labels at scale, no agent-derived labels), exactly matching the audit's reason for KEEP. Experiment arms: with-label vs without-label (the core contrast), Tier-1 confirmatory vs Tier-2 replication, goal-masked vs goal-included audit surfaces, pre- vs post-verdict probe position. All numbers are bracketed placeholders — nothing has been run.

## Novelty highlight

- **Sharpest claim:** every strong attribution formulation defines blame as "the earliest step whose counterfactual correction flips failure to success," a counterfactual that *provably does not exist* on infeasible tasks — so attributors over-blame by construction, and whether an added `unsolvable` label fixes it (label-space defect) or not (capability defect) is measurable only with agent-independent solvability ground truth. The FBR-vs-CoR *directional* framing (over-blame vs cop-out) plus the label-space/capability dissociation via net selectivity S is the paper's own contribution, not borrowed.
- **Nearest prior work:** Who&When / AgenTracer / Famas / AEGIS / DRIFT-TELBENCH (all presuppose a culpable step or inject agent-side faults into solvable tasks); ImpossibleBench (certifies impossibility via spec-test contradiction but measures the *acting agent's cheating*, not third-party attribution); FeasiGen / AbstentionBench / "Recognizing Limits" / SQuAD 2.0 (first-person abstention, with trace-circular or human ground truth). The related-work analysis in all three drafts correctly shows no prior work poses the third-party false-blame question.
- **Operational delta:** (i) spec-only oracle certificates dissociating "unsolvable-for-anyone" from "unsolved-by-this-agent"; (ii) matched pairs as surface-statistic equalizers with a gated leakage audit; (iii) the `unsolvable` verdict channel injected symmetrically with an explicit forcing control; (iv) the ceiling/hidden-state probe locating the defect in judgment vs trace information.
- **Load-bearing?** Yes. The certified-pair instrument is not a garnish — every downstream rate is uninterpretable without it, and it is precisely the "mechanical ground truth" that distinguishes this from the LLM-judge-labeled attribution wave. The Tier-1 soundness story and the leakage audit are what a hostile reviewer will attack first; v3 already carries the defense.

## Freshness check vs 2026-08

Drafts froze 2026-07-09/10; the field moved. Agent-trajectory failure attribution is now the largest arXiv tag (53.8% of recent papers) and crowding fast. Consequences:

1. **Positioning must lead with the anti-LLM-judge argument.** The crowd's benchmarks (Who&When annotations, AEGIS's LLM-manipulator labels, TracerTraj's counterfactual replay) all bottom out in an LLM or human reading the trace. B2.4's certificates are the rare ground truth that is *independent of any model's judgment* — this should be promoted from a constraints paragraph to the headline positioning ("the only attribution question you can score without an LLM in the loop"). The current intros still position mainly against "attribution assumes solvability," which by now several concurrent papers may also observe informally; the *certified instrument* is the defensible moat.
2. **Compose with B2.1 (family lead).** B2.1's injected-fault causal-attribution benchmark and B2.4 should share environments, executor pools, the verdict ontology, and the harness: unsolvability-by-mutation is naturally the "no culpable component exists" outcome class appended to B2.1's fault taxonomy, and B2.4's FBR is then "attribution accuracy on the null-fault stratum." Shipping them as siblings (shared infrastructure, cross-cited) both halves engineering cost and pre-empts the "yet another attribution benchmark" objection.
3. **Freshness of citations:** the drafts already cite the 2026 wave (AgenTracer, Famas, AEGIS, DRIFT/TELBENCH, ImpossibleBench, FeasiGen, agentic-abstention). A re-sweep for July–August 2026 arXiv is mandatory before submission — the specific risk is a concurrent "infeasible-task attribution" paper; the mitigation is the certification + leakage-audit rigor no fast-follow will match.
4. **Scope pressure:** v3's Stage-3 (risk-coverage gating + hidden-state probe) is scientifically nice but not needed for the core claim, and the five-classifier/permutation-calibrated audit is heavy. Given the crowding, shipping fast with Stages 1–2 (+ the simple v2-style trace probe) beats shipping late with everything.

Verdict: **no re-framing of the research question needed; re-positioning of the pitch and aggressive de-scoping needed.**
