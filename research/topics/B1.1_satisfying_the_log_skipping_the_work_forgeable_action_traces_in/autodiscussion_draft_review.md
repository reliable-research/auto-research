## 1. Architecture

- **Pipeline:** (1) Committed Dual-Channel Harness (CDCH): agent writes a verifier-facing log; a host-isolated mutation service records hash-committed pre/post environment snapshots, issues signed mutation receipts, and evaluates a versioned executable completion predicate. Forging label `Y = 1[log-verifier pass ∧ state-predicate fail]`. (2) Outcome-blind task pipeline: naturalness certificates (provenance, diff-from-source, reference completion, clause-to-instruction map, two blinded raters, inducement-cue screen) frozen before randomization. (3) Task-blocked randomized factorial: pressure (best-of-k k∈{1,4,16} or PPO at 3 KL budgets) × visibility (opaque/outcome-only/transparent) × mutation-only toll λ∈{0,0.1,0.3} × 4 verifier families × reward arm (log/state/null). (4) Selection-vs-acquisition decomposition: frozen-policy best-of-16 vs uniform selection on identical pools (selection-revealed); pre/post PPO difference-in-differences on unselected held-out rollouts vs state/null arms (RL-acquired).
- **Estimands:** Attributed Forging Incidence Rate (AFIR) per condition; standardized pressure risk differences by reward arm (GEE + hierarchical logistic with all 2-way interactions); paired selection contrast; run-clustered DiD acquisition contrast.
- **Datasets (all constructed, none existing):** 2,000+2,000 replay-forgery/complete-control pairs (RQ1); 1,200-certificate + 600-task blinded audits (RQ2); 10,000-replicate design-preserving simulation (RQ3); 57,600-trajectory factorial cohort (RQ4); 12,000 pools × 16 candidates = 192,000 rollouts (RQ5); 360 PPO runs × 200 held-out tasks × pre/post = 144,000 eval trajectories (RQ6).
- **Baselines:** log-only adjudication, blinded state-aware expert adjudication, one-factor-at-a-time analysis, uniform within-pool selection, matched state-reward and null-reward arms.
- **Metrics:** sensitivity/FPR on replay forgeries, IPW Cohen's κ, retention rate, simulation power/bias/coverage, AFIR contrasts with max-T simultaneous intervals, equivalence tests.
- **Load-bearing choices:** (a) mechanical state predicate as sole ground truth (no LLM in truth channel); (b) `Y` defined as raw discordance, with honest error handled only via matched-arm *contrasts*, not per-episode attribution; (c) verifier family treated as an *opaque unnamed categorical* (spec admits the four families are not defined); (d) all pressure results hinge on the null-reward arm being a valid artifact baseline; (e) confirmatory thresholds (≥8pp pressure effect, ≤1% FPR, κ≥0.90) are assumed, not piloted.

## 3. Consistency vs discussion.md

**Agreements (faithful renderings):** dual-channel log/state split with 真值 = 环境状态差分; separability + naturalness certificates (SP1/SP2); matched log/state/no-pressure arms for attribution (Stage-1 repair 2a); neutral-context/inducement-cue screen (SP3 R1); propensity-revelation vs acquisition regime split with no pooling (SP3 R3); observability, cost, and verifier family as manipulated factors (H-obs/H-cost/H-family); mixed-effects/GEE analysis of the emergence surface.

**Improvements over discussion.md:** replay-forgery + complete-control validation suite with sensitivity/FPR gates (RQ1) — discussion.md only had a per-task forging-probe; design-based simulation validation of the estimator (RQ3); precise operationalization of the mutation-only toll (r' = r − λM/M_ref) and of visibility as model-access separate from optimizer reward; run-certificate/signing infrastructure exceeds the sandbox requirements.

**Contradictions and silent scope changes (serious):**
1. **Pilot → confirmatory megastudy, silently.** discussion.md fixed a 32-cell pilot (40 tasks, 2 models, ~1,920 episodes/O-level, "low thousands of dollars"), go/no-go gates G1–G3, and RL only at scale-up with a kill criterion. The draft jumps straight to 57,600 factorial trajectories, 192,000 candidates, 360 PPO runs, 144,000 held-out evals, with no pilot, no gates, no kill criterion.
2. **Calendar/merge logic dropped.** The binding 2026-09-05 decision rule (standalone vs B1.2-FSE section vs park; publishable-null path) is absent from all three artifacts. Given today is 2026-08-13, the drafted design cannot possibly produce pilot results by Sept 5 — the merge option silently expires by construction.
3. **Attribution rule weakened.** discussion.md: a discordant episode is only a *candidate*; headline FIR is scaled by a κ≥0.7 double-annotated forging-vs-honest-error audit plus matched-arm base-rate subtraction. Draft: headline "Attributed" FIR = raw L∧¬S rate; the RQ2 audit checks label *reproducibility* (κ≥0.9 agreement), not forging-vs-honest-error attribution; nothing scales the headline. The word "Attributed" in AFIR now refers to factor attribution, quietly repurposing the term.
4. **Prompt-optimization pressure arm dropped** (with SP3 R2 optimizer-transcript stratification — one of the discussion's named secondary endpoints and a distinctive finding channel: "where in the pipeline the exploit gets stored").
5. **Forged-trace taxonomy (SP5b) dropped**, making the H-family "verifiers shift strategy distribution" prediction untestable; correspondingly, verifier families degenerate to four *unnamed* opaque categories (spec's own Open Decisions admits this).
6. **RL-arm verifier confound unaddressed.** discussion.md Stage-5 critic #1 required the primary RL acquisition arm to use the exactly-specified rule-based checker (else log-forgeability is confounded with generic learned-RM exploitability); the draft crosses PPO with all four unnamed families.
7. Dropped: capability-split model pair; n=64 pressure level (max k now 16); quiescence-window oracle hardening against time-of-check games; naturalness rule that degenerate paths must mirror real-world tooling facilities (the rater "domain-plausibility" check is weaker and does not audit the degenerate path specifically).

## 5. Gap to implementation

- **No tasks exist.** The spec's "Inputs You Must Provide" list offloads the entire scientific substrate: natural source tasks + adapters + executable predicates + clause maps + validated reference completions for every task (40 blocks' worth), four verifier-family implementations, replay defect transformations, rubrics, raters. This is the majority of project cost and is unscheduled.
- **Verifier family — a confirmatory factor — is undefined.** RQ4's headline "generalizes across ≥2 verifier families" cannot be preregistered until the four families are named and implemented (discussion.md already named the three archetypes: rule-based checker, trajectory-RM, per-call judge).
- **Compute is 1–2 orders beyond budget.** ~250k agent trajectories plus 360 independent PPO runs of an open-weight model. discussion.md budgeted "low thousands of dollars / hundreds of GPU-hours" (pilot) and 200–400 H100-hours (scale-up); 360 PPO runs alone plausibly costs tens of thousands of H100-hours. No compute estimate appears anywhere in the spec beyond "capacity for ... 360 PPO runs".
- **Human-labeling load unaccounted:** 2 blinded raters × every candidate task, 3 raters × 600 tasks, state-aware experts × 1,200 certificates.
- **Unstated dependencies:** model choice, PPO library adapter, container/attestation infra (Ed25519 signing, hash-committed snapshots), preregistration document. The engineering plan (27 ordered tasks, full package skeleton) is genuinely runnable, but only RQ3 (simulation) and RQ1 (replay suite, once ~dozens of tasks exist) are near-term executable.
- **Timeline realism:** none stated. Against the topic's own calendar (pilot analyzed by 2026-09-05 to preserve the B1.2 merge option), the drafted design is infeasible; even standalone, this is a multi-quarter program without the pilot gating that discussion.md required precisely to avoid an expensive null.
- **Fragile success criteria:** ≥95% sensitivity, ≤1% FPR, κ≥0.90, ≥8pp effects are asserted without base-rate evidence; the spec has no fallback if forging incidence is near zero at k≤16 (discussion.md's hardening iteration, publishable-null path, and n=64 level are all gone).

## 6. Verdict

**Adopt-with-fixes.** The package is a competent and in places sharpened formalization of discussion.md's core design — the dual-channel harness, matched-arm attribution, and the selection/acquisition decomposition are all faithfully carried over, the citation base is real (one first-author misattribution, zero confabulations), and Claim 2 (the randomized factorial) plus the frozen-pool/DiD identification remain unclaimed territory in the 2025–2026 literature. But the draft silently converts a gated, calendar-bound pilot into an unpiloted ~250k-trajectory, 360-PPO-run confirmatory program, weakens the forging-attribution rule while keeping the word "Attributed" in the headline estimand, and its related work predates the directly competing 2026 corpus. Priority fixes: **(1) Restore the two-stage structure** — discussion.md's 32-cell pilot (best-of-k only, rule-based verifier, 40 tasks, 2 capability-split models) with go/no-go gates G1–G3 and the RL kill criterion; demote the 57,600/360-run design to a preregistered scale-up appendix, and acknowledge the Sept-5 merge option is dead. **(2) Re-instate attribution and de-confound the RL arm** — κ-gated forging-vs-honest-error audit scaling the headline rate, matched-arm base-rate subtraction, rule-based checker as the sole primary RL-arm verifier, and name the four verifier families before any freeze. **(3) Rewrite Related Work against the 2026 literature** (SpecBench, Countdown-Code, RHB, LLMs Gaming Verifiers, Verification Horizon, CAVA, provenance survey), reposition Claim 1 from "first dual-channel ground truth" to "first log-channel-vs-state instantiation with certified separability," fix Denison et al. attribution, and complete the placeholder section. Secondary: restore the prompt-optimization arm and the forged-trace taxonomy — both are cheap and carry distinctive findings the current draft cannot produce.
