## Architecture

(Design skeleton of canonical `a95544c3` as drafted.)

**Pipeline — three stages feeding forward:**

1. **M1 — Certified design-mutant generation.** Category-typed AST operators produce behavior-preserving-by-construction edits: `ConsistentRebindRename` (naming), `CallgraphInvariantMove` (layering), `DependenceGatedReorder` (temporal). A candidate is admitted **iff the project's own machine-readable rule checker flips PASS→FAIL** (naming: Checkstyle/regex; layering: ArchUnit / dependency-cruiser; temporal: an executable checker *compiled mechanically from shipped ordering artifacts* — `@Order`/phase declarations, lifecycle config — a project enters the temporal category only if such an artifact exists). Each design mutant is paired with a **convention-neutral control** of equal edit-distance and location that leaves the checker PASSing.
2. **M2 — Calibrated behavior-preservation oracle.** Ensemble of differential testing (inputs only from suite-independent fuzzing + property/type-directed generators; the studied suite's inputs are excluded) + an LLM behavioral judge. Threshold frozen on a calibration split of a hand-adjudicated gold set; **residual false-equivalence rate measured out-of-sample** on a disjoint split (no shared mutants or projects), reported with Clopper–Pearson CI (target ≤5%).
3. **M3 — Three-contrast blind-spot decision rule.** A category is a blind spot iff, jointly: (i) DMSR − FSR gap with non-overlapping bootstrap CIs; (ii) design-mutant survival > matched control survival (design-specificity); (iii) surviving design mutants **persist after design-blind capability-matched augmentation** (post-aug DMSR ≥ 0.5), where the augmentation is UTBoost-style coverage-driven synthesis that provably cannot query rule checkers, and its coverage power is validated on a separate *behavior-altering* capability-probe mutant family against an empirical "design-blind ceiling" (within 10%).

**Testbed:** ~12 mature open-source Java/Python systems shipping machine-readable convention configs, with their unmodified test suites.

**IVs:** design-property category (naming / layering / temporal ordering); mutant kind (design / functional-probe / neutral control); augmentation arm (none / design-blind / rule-checker-aware); gating (dependence-gated vs ungated).
**DVs:** per-category DMSR, FSR, DMSR−FSR gap CI, control survival, post-augmentation DMSR, measured false-equivalence rate, binary per-category blind-spot verdict.

**Ground-truth mechanism:** dual suite-independent oracles — the project's own rule checker certifies "design-violating"; the calibrated differential ensemble certifies "behavior-preserving". The suite under study never decides admission (non-circularity is the central constraint; pre-registration of all thresholds).

**Main experiment arms (6 RQs):** RQ1 admission validity (blinded audit, ≥90% confirmed violations); RQ2 gated vs ungated operators (≥2× preservation rate); RQ3 oracle false-equivalence ≤5% and beats differential-testing-alone; RQ4 recall ≥80% on semantics-altering transformations, +15pp over uncalibrated LLM label; RQ5 the three-contrast rule fires non-trivially for ≥1 category (predicted layering or temporal); RQ6 attribution — rule-checker-aware augmentation collapses post-aug DMSR by ≥30pp, proving persistence is design-blindness, not coverage weakness.

**Where `32840ac2` differs:** it drops the augmentation contrast (M3-iii) and instead builds a metrology stack — per-category verdicts in {present, absent, undetermined}, decided by whether the gap's CI clears conservative additive floors (q_surv/q_killed censoring charges with worst-case tails, cross-category leakage c_leak, design behavioral impurity e_design, sham-footprint s_sham, operator-family killability envelope ρ_kill = ρ_D + ρ_F with leave-one-family-out transport validation), plus frozen A(C) positive-control mixtures for directionally coupled categories (difference-in-differences Gap_c|P) and a strict-priority verdict engine. 26 RQs, all validating the instrument itself against planted-effect benchmarks. Also extends categories to lifecycle/dynamic-trace predicates via runtime automata.

## Novelty highlight

**Sharpest claim:** the *expressiveness boundary of the test-suite gate itself* is made measurable, per design category, via mutants dually certified as convention-violating (by the project's own shipped rule checker — not researcher-authored rules) and behavior-preserving (by a suite-independent oracle whose residual error is *measured, not assumed*). The three-contrast rule — and especially contrast (iii), a design-blind augmentation whose coverage power is separately validated on behavior-altering probes — separates "the suite structurally cannot see this" from "the suite happens to be weak here."

**Nearest prior work (as the draft states):** equivalent-mutant detection (Just et al. state-infection; Tian et al. LLM-EMD) — treats design-wrong-but-equivalent variants as noise to discard, by definition; oracle-adequacy metrics (Hossain & Dwyer survey; "Mind the Gap" oracle gap) — behavior-scoped, no design categories; coding-agent test benchmarks (UTBoost, SWT-Bench, Defects4J) — behavior-altering targets only; architecture-conformance tooling (ArchUnit-style, Styler, DCLfix, erosion-symptom mining) — studied as separate machinery, never as a probe of what suites express. All classified as "not_considered", zero candidate baselines, zero high motivation threats.

**What the draft's related work misses (my knowledge):** (a) property-based mutation testing (Bartocci et al., covered in 32840ac2 but absent from a95544c3's deep comparisons — the closest structural relative: adequacy w.r.t. a supplied property, though behavioral/STL only); (b) the 2025–26 "constraint decay" / AGENTS.md-compliance cluster (ContextCov, Satriani et al.) is cited as motivation but the draft should state explicitly that these measure *agent violation rates*, whereas T5.2 measures *gate expressiveness* — the mechanism upstream of them; (c) pseudo-tested methods / Descartes (extreme mutation) as another "suite checks less than believed" instrument; worth a sentence. Also one broken related-work entry (a "kicked rotor" physics paper with corrupted metadata) must be dropped before submission.

**Operational delta:** prior instruments either discard the equivalence class (mutation testing) or never touch the suite (conformance checkers); this work crosses them: rule-checker-certified violation × oracle-certified equivalence × survival measurement, with calibrated admission error. **The novelty is load-bearing** — this is a [机制] measurement paper; if the dual-certified-mutant instrument is not new, nothing else carries the paper. The topic README's "minimal experiment" (design-violating functionally-equivalent mutants, measure kill rate) is exactly what both drafts deliver, so the drafts are faithful to the topic charter.

## Freshness check vs 2026-08

Drafts are from ~2026-07-09; motivation is argued almost purely from first principles ("a green build is misread as a certificate") plus the constraint-decay/ContextCov cluster.

- **Production acceptance-gap evidence — not used, should be added.** Neither version cites ReactBench (60.8% of green-tests patches fail static analysis), Faros (31.3% zero-review merges; defect rate 9%→54%), or METR merge-rate data. These are exactly the missing empirical premise for the paper's motivation: they document that green-but-structurally-deficient changes are *already being merged at scale*, which converts the intro's hypothetical ("can slip through unnoticed") into a measured deployment fact. The ReactBench number is especially on-point: green tests + failing static analysis is precisely a design-category blind spot in the wild. One paragraph of intro rework, high leverage.
- **RLVR/verifier-gap crystallization — anticipated in spirit, needs explicit positioning.** The subfield has crystallized around *verifier weakness exploited by the agent* (reward hacking, test gaming — that side of the topic map belongs to B1.2). T5.2's differentiation must be stated: it measures the *structural expressiveness ceiling of the verifier itself*, independent of any adversarial agent — a property of the gate, not of the optimizer. This both defends against "crowded area" reviews and cleanly divides labor with B1.2 ("Adequacy Is the Attack Surface"). Neither draft currently draws this line; add it to intro + related work.
- The core design needs no change from these updates — they strengthen rather than threaten the motivation.
