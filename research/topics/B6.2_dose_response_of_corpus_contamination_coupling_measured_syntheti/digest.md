## N1 Problem Formulation

- **objective** [HYPOTHESIS]: "measure, via controlled continued-pretraining experiments on
  small language models, the causal functional (dose-response) relationship between the
  exactly-known fraction of synthetic (LLM-generated) text mixed into a fixed-size training
  corpus and the resulting degradation in downstream capability and output diversity — and
  determine, with pre-specified statistical tests, whether a safety threshold exists below
  which contamination is statistically indistinguishable from harmless or whether
  degradation is monotonic from the first synthetic token, including how much that verdict
  degrades when the dose axis is estimated by a real AI-text detector instead of being
  known." Self-check: kind of task = measurement/dose-response curve fitting; domain = LLM
  pretraining-data contamination; outcome shape = a fitted curve family + a
  threshold-vs-monotonic verdict + a detector-noise sensitivity analysis. All three land.
  status PARTIAL (inferred full predicate, not an author's own sentence).
- **inputs** [HYPOTHESIS]:
  1. a human-authored reference corpus guaranteed contamination-free by provenance (a
     pre-2022, pre-ChatGPT web/curated corpus slice, e.g. C4 or The Pile),
  2. a pool of synthetic text generated by an open instruction-tuned LLM, matched to the
     reference corpus in domain, topic, and length distribution,
  3. a fixed pretrained backbone checkpoint (same starting weights for every condition),
  4. a fixed continued-pretraining token/compute budget and hyperparameter set,
  5. a log-spaced grid of synthetic-fraction mixing ratios with known ground truth,

## N5 Challenges

**challenges** (problem-inherent obstacles; N4-vs-N5 test applied — these concern the
problem itself, not what a prior tool fails to do; ids stable from v1):

- **C1** [HYPOTHESIS]: "Isolating the causal effect of synthetic fraction from confounds of
  token budget, compute, data ordering, and generator quality" — any dose-response design
  must hold total token count, optimization hyperparameters, starting checkpoint, and
  document-shuffling policy fixed across ratios, and must match the synthetic pool to the
  human pool in topic/length/register, or an observed 'degradation' could reflect a
  distribution-shift or budget confound rather than syntheticness itself.
- **C2** [HYPOTHESIS]: "Defining capability and diversity metrics that are comparable,
  sensitive, and aggregable across the full ratio grid at small model scale" — a
  dose-response curve is only interpretable if the same metric is monotone-interpretable
  across checkpoints; few-shot accuracies are noisy and near-floor at sub-1B scale, and a
  discontinuous metric can fabricate an apparent threshold that a continuous metric on the
  same outputs would dissolve.
- **C3** [HYPOTHESIS]: "Real-world corpora do not come with a known ground-truth synthetic
  fraction" — the controlled design injects known ratios, but applying any resulting
  dose-response curve to naturally contaminated corpora requires estimating the actual
  fraction with an imperfect detector, so measurement error on the dose axis is intrinsic
  to the deployed use-case.
- **C4** [HYPOTHESIS]: "Distinguishing a genuine change-point (threshold) from gradual
  monotonic degradation under realistic seed noise" — 'small but non-zero effect at low
  dose' and 'true zero effect below a threshold' are statistically adjacent hypotheses;
  separating them requires log-spaced low-dose levels, multiple seeds, pre-specified
  equivalence margins, and model comparison across nested and non-nested curve families,
  otherwise the two verdicts are indistinguishable.
- **C5** [HYPOTHESIS]: "Generalizing a small-model, fixed-backbone continued-pretraining
  dose-response curve to full-scale pretraining-from-scratch" — the cost-saving proxy
  design (same pretrained starting checkpoint + short continued pretraining) is explicitly
  a controlled-cost stand-in, and whether curve shape and threshold location transfer
  across model scale and training regime is itself an open question the design does not
  resolve.

**conflation_check**: true — each challenge targets a distinct obstacle (confound isolation,
metric validity at small scale, dose-axis measurement error, statistical threshold
identifiability, scale transfer); none are near-duplicates.

## N6 Methodology

**overview** [HYPOTHESIS]: "Starting from a fixed pretrained backbone, the method continues
pretraining across a log-spaced grid of exactly-known synthetic-fraction mixtures at fixed
total token and compute budget, evaluates every checkpoint on a pre-registered capability
and diversity battery, fits four candidate response shapes per metric with equivalence
tests at low doses, and finally re-estimates the dose axis with a black-box AI-text
detector to quantify how forensic measurement error propagates into the threshold verdict.
**Staged plan** — *Stage 1 (pilot = the seed's minimal experiment)*: one backbone
(Pythia-410M), ~1B continued-pretraining tokens per run, 7 doses × 2 seeds (14 runs), full
metric battery, used to estimate seed-level variance and run a power analysis. *Stage 2
(scale-up)*: two backbones (Pythia-410M and Pythia-1.4B, or OLMo-1B), ~2B tokens per run,
11 doses × 3 seeds per backbone (66 runs), a second generator to vary synthetic-text
provenance, the detector-coupling analysis (M6), and the mitigation baselines." location:
n/a (hypothesized, no paper exists).

**key_components:**
- **M1** — "Provenance-Controlled Corpus Pair" [HYPOTHESIS]: human reference pool = a
  fixed slice of a pre-2022 (pre-ChatGPT) corpus (e.g. C4 or a Pile subset), guaranteeing
  0% synthetic ground truth by provenance; synthetic pool = an open instruction-tuned LLM
  (e.g. Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct; second generator Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct in Stage 2) prompted to
  regenerate/continue documents drawn from a *disjoint* slice of the same corpus, with
  rejection filtering to match the human pool's document-length and topic-cluster
  distributions (measured at document granularity via embedding-cluster histograms).
  Instrument: vLLM generation logs + distribution-match report per pool.
- **M2** — "Log-Spaced Dose Grid Mixer" [HYPOTHESIS]: training mixtures at fixed total
  token count with synthetic fraction ρ ∈ {0, 0.5, 1, 2, 5, 10, 20, 35, 50, 75, 100}%
  (pilot uses the 7-point subset {0, 1, 5, 10, 25, 50, 100}%), mixed at document
  granularity with a fixed shuffling-seed policy so token order statistics are identical in
  distribution across ratios; ground-truth ρ recorded per run. Log-spacing at the low end
  is what gives C4's threshold test its resolution.
- **M3** — "Fixed-Budget Continued-Pretraining Harness" [HYPOTHESIS]: every run starts from
  the identical pretrained checkpoint (Pythia-410M / Pythia-1.4B, chosen because their
  pretraining data — the Pile — predates ChatGPT), uses identical hyperparameters
  (sequence length, LR schedule, batch size), a fixed token budget (1B pilot / 2B
  scale-up), and k random seeds per dose (2 pilot / 3 scale-up) controlling data order and
  dropout. Instrument: training-config hashes + loss curves logged per run.
- **M4** — "Degradation Metric Battery" [HYPOTHESIS]: measured on every checkpoint:
  *capability* — (a) primary endpoint: perplexity on a clean held-out human validation set
  (held-out C4/Pile slice + WikiText-103), (b) few-shot accuracy via lm-eval-harness on
  LAMBADA, HellaSwag, ARC-Easy, PIQA; *diversity* — on 5,000 sampled continuations of
  held-out human prompts: distinct-2/distinct-3, Self-BLEU, mean pairwise embedding
  distance (coverage); *tail preservation* — rare-token (bottom-decile frequency) recall
  and perplexity on the rare-n-gram tail of the validation set, plus corpus-level entropy
  of generated text via nearest-neighbor differential-entropy estimation, directly reusing
  the indicator of [CloserLookCollapse, OpenReview:6xCcjYa97j]. Each capability construct
  is paired with a continuous metric so a threshold verdict never rests on a discontinuous
  metric alone.
- **M5** — "Curve Fitting & Threshold Adjudication" [HYPOTHESIS]: per metric, fit four
  response models over (dose × seed) observations — flat null, linear, convex power-law
  (y = a + b·ρ^γ), and segmented change-point regression; compare by AICc; test the
  change-point's existence with a Davies test; test monotone trend with Jonckheere–Terpstra
  (seed-level); declare a dose "statistically harmless" only via TOST equivalence testing
  against ρ=0 with a pre-registered margin (δ = 0.5 × seed-level SD of the ρ=0 condition);
  Holm–Bonferroni correction across the metric battery, α = 0.05.
- **M6** — "Forensics-Coupling Module" [HYPOTHESIS]: run a black-box AI-text detector (a
  recoverability-style single-call detector in the spirit of [DandR,
  OpenReview:FiMZSxo4DO], plus a Binoculars-style score as a second instrument) over a
  fixed random sample of documents from every mixture; aggregate per-document scores into
  an estimated corpus fraction ρ̂; report the calibration curve ρ̂(ρ) and its error; re-fit
  all M5 curve models against ρ̂ instead of ρ and report the shift in estimated threshold
  location and curve-shape verdict. This is the "coupling measured synthetic fractions"
  half of the title made operational.
