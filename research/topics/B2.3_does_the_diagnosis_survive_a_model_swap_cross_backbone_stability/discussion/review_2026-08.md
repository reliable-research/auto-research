## 3. Feasibility findings (se-paper-reviewer lens)

Graded [major]/[moderate]/[minor]:

1. **[major] Staleness — related work frozen at a two-paper cluster while the cluster
   industrialized (the fleet-wide ~1-month-stale signature; here ~6 weeks).** The pre-revision
   draft's nearest-work framing named only Who&When + AgenTracer; Who&When Pro (the named nearest
   neighbor's own successor, 12,326 injected trajectories, cross-model-family attributor analyses)
   was absent, as was the entire 2025–26 attribution wave (CHIEF, FAMAS, DoVer, AgentErrorBench,
   VerifyMAS, MARS/StateMAS, HPFA, SearchAuditBench, LongRCA). A reviewer who knows any of these
   reads the draft as uninformed; the injection-suite machinery also needed an explicit corpus
   concession to Pro. **Fixed** (concede-and-claim rewrite of intro nearest-work paragraph +
   Related Work; 7 verified citations added).
2. **[moderate] Judge-axis universal negative false at t_audit.** "None measures judge disagreement
   on long agent traces" was contradicted by arXiv 2604.04532 (six judge backbones, Fleiss' κ ≤
   0.231 on agent-trajectory requirement judgments). **Fixed** (concession + delta statement in
   intro and Related Work).
3. **[moderate] RQ7 power arithmetic broken.** The pre-registered compound criterion "Spearman
   ρ ≥ 0.5 **and** p < 0.05" is unconfirmable by construction at K = 6–8 backbones: the exact
   two-sided α = 0.05 critical values are ≈0.89/0.79/0.74 at K = 6/7/8, so an observed ρ of 0.5
   can never be significant. A pre-registration containing an unsatisfiable confirmation condition
   is a real defect, not a style issue. **Fixed** (K-adjusted exact threshold as confirmatory bar;
   ρ ∈ [0.5, threshold) pre-declared suggestive-INCONCLUSIVE; mirrored in `experiment_design.md`).
4. **[moderate] Rewriter-fidelity gate missing on the style-normalization arm.** The paraphrase
   rewriter is the one Stage-2 instrument with no validation gate (skeleton checks ν idempotence
   only): a rewriter that flattens genuinely divergent evidence deflates normalized-arm
   disagreement and *inflates* the style-share, biasing RQ5 and the RQ3 factorial's style
   apportionment. **Fixed** (pre-registered content-fidelity gate: injected-fault manifestation
   must survive rewriting; judge ground-truth accuracy on rewritten traces within a pre-fixed
   margin of raw; exclusion rate reported; gate failure → RQ5 INCONCLUSIVE).
5. **[moderate] No Stage-2 cost ceiling.** The walking skeleton is costed (<$25) and the pilot's
   Ω ≥ 0.5 gate is a genuine kill-test, but the full grid (≈16,200–21,600 agent executions;
   ~90k judge calls over WebArena-length traces, i.e. judging-dominated, plausibly
   billions of input tokens) had no budget gate anywhere. **Fixed** (pre-registered
   pilot-extrapolated hard budget ceiling + tie-in to the existing pre-approved shrink axis; no
   speculative dollar figure invented).
6. **[minor] RQ6's within-family arm is thin by design** (3–4 within-family pairs at 3–4 families
   × 2 tiers); the INCONCLUSIVE pre-declaration already handles it, and pair-level dependence
   (pairs sharing a backbone) is partially addressed by task-level permutation. Logged, not
   revised — a backbone-level permutation scheme is worth considering at Phase B.
7. **[minor] Concurrent-window works not individually positioned** (MARS/StateMAS, HPFA,
   SearchAuditBench, VerifyMAS, DiagEval): all single-stack attribution/repair systems; the revised
   wave sentence covers the class, and adding five more citations would bloat a spec draft. Named
   here for the Phase-B related-work refresh.
8. **[minor] Portfolio-consistency tension** (see cross-topic note above): standalone B2.3 vs the
   portfolio's merge-into-B2.1 disposition needs a supervisor decision before submission planning;
   nothing in the draft is wrong, but the two-paper strategy must be made explicit if both ship.

Feasibility positives worth recording: the pilot→scale gate structure (Ω gate, B0 premise gate,
INCONCLUSIVE pre-declarations, pre-approved shrink axis, F-C0 extreme-censoring pivot) is the
strongest kill-test discipline in the B2.x cluster; power arithmetic for the κ contrasts (RQ1/RQ2)

trajectories could seed pilot fault payloads (flagged as an option, not spliced into the
