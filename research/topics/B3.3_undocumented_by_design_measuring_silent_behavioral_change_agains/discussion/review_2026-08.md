### Feasibility (se-paper-reviewer lens)
- **F-1 [moderate] Logprob-eligibility and snapshot-acquisition constraints unstated (fixed).** The fingerprint module requires top-k logprobs; the nearest neighbor's own field data show only **~23% of reachable endpoints expose logprobs**, and vendors can obstruct (documented ≥16-token minimum-output enforcement during that study). The "≥3 vendors" design was silent on this screening, and the draft never said where paired pre/post snapshots come from (dated snapshot endpoints vs prospective polling — present in root `discussion.md`, missing from the spec). Fixed: eligibility screening sentence in Boundary Fingerprinting + a "Snapshot acquisition and study-window gate" paragraph in Experimental Setup.
- **F-2 [moderate] No kill-test for the prospective arm (fixed).** Clean transitions arrive on vendors' schedules; a study window could yield zero clean or zero quarantined transitions, leaving RQ5 unanswerable on an underpowered split. Fixed: pre-registered gate (≥2 clean and ≥2 quarantined transitions accumulated before RQ5 is scored; otherwise reported pending) — a design target, not a fabricated result.
- **F-3 [minor] RQ4 power arithmetic is steep.** ~200 pilot probes / 6 categories ≈ 33 probes per category; the ≥20-detected-diffs-per-category test threshold implies ≥60% within-category detected-diff rates at pilot scale. Mitigated by the existing pooling-of-underpowered-categories rule and the scale-up stage; no change manufactured.
- **F-4 [minor] Annotation budget plausible.** ≥200 diff/claim pairs × 2 annotators with κ ≥ 0.6 gate — days of work, standard; fine as specified.
- **Budget sanity (no finding):** pilot ≈ 200 probes × 50 samples × 2 snapshots ≈ 20k calls per transition plus continuous 1-token fingerprint polling — low cost; the binding resource is calendar time (F-2), not tokens.
