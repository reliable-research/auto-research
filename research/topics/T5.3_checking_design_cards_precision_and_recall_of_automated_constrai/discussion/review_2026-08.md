## 4. Feasibility findings (se-paper-reviewer lens)

| # | Severity | Finding |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | **[major]** | **Staleness / missing nearest neighbor.** Related Work and Intro name defect-focused code review as "the closest prior work"; as of 2026-04 that is false — 2604.05955 is closer on every axis (design constraints, agent patches, SWE-bench substrate, LLM verifier). Draft's reference cache was frozen 2026-07-04 and the 2026-08-13 portfolio decay note postdates it. Fix: concede-and-claim in Intro nearest-work paragraph + Related Work lead + corpus section. |
| F2 | **[moderate]** | **Power arithmetic broken in RQ1.** The 30-violation floor cannot support the pre-registered Wilson 95% CI lower bound of 0.75: at n=30, observed recall 0.80 gives a Wilson lower bound ≈ 0.63, and even observed recall 0.90 gives ≈ 0.74 — RQ1 would be auto-refuted by sample size, not verifier quality. Reaching a 0.75 lower bound at observed recall 0.85 needs ≈ 80 violations per category. Fix: two-tier target (lower bound ≥ 0.60 at the 30-violation floor; ≥ 0.75 pre-registered only for categories retaining ≥ 80). |
| F3 | **[moderate]** | **RQ1 internal inconsistency.** Setup says compute "jointly" across the four structural categories; expected outcome and falsification condition are per-category. Per-category is the design's own commitment — make the setup say so. |
| F4 | **[moderate]** | **RQ5 equivalence claim has no test.** "Patterns agree within 0.05" is an equivalence claim asserted on point estimates; at n≈30–80 violations per category, per-category F1 noise alone exceeds ±0.05, so the falsification condition would fire on noise. Fix: TOST-style equivalence with a ±0.05 margin on a bootstrap CI (clustered by card), with an explicit underpowered-inconclusive outcome when the CI is wider than the margin. |
| F5 | **[moderate]** | **No annotation-budget arithmetic.** The design needs ≥3 reviewers × every (card,patch) pair + pre-screen + adjudication + double-coded error taxonomy, but no reviewer-hours estimate exists anywhere in the draft — for an annotation-dominated study this is the first feasibility question a PC will ask. Fix: add explicit budget sketch to Experimental Setup. |
| F6 | [minor] | Non-obviousness operationalized solely by reviewer judgment time — confounded by pair length/reading time; should be normalized (e.g., time per changed line) or paired with a self-reported difficulty rating. Logged, not revised (a design-parameter choice for the pilot). |
| F7 | [minor] | The "fewer than one in ten review comments high quality" figure is cited from the defect-focused code-review line without a named source in prose; acceptable at spec stage given the no-bibliography drafting convention, but should carry a citation at venue-formatting time. Logged. |
