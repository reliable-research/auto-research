## The eight `\wj{}` standpoints (what each hypothesis commits to)

RQ1 — expect H1's both conjuncts (nonzero AND non-uniform) for ≥2/3 families; failure ⇒ pre-registered pivot to robustness certification.
RQ2 — expect the slope to survive ≤2018 with the ≤2016 cut agreeing in sign; failure ⇒ leakage attribution, style claim (only) withdrawn.
RQ3 — expect monotone rise with the ≥1 pp floor; significance without magnitude pre-registered as a fail.
RQ4 — assumption gate, not a drift hypothesis: expect impurity UCB < 1% (n=2,000 powered for it); can weaken conclusions, never silently strengthen them.
RQ5 — expect survival under four named matched covariates; failure ⇒ composition mechanism, anchor still unsafe as used.
RQ6 — CO-PRIMARY: expect ≥2 pp propagation; BOTH branches publishable (propagation indicts / invariance = self-robustness co-headline); own INCONCLUSIVE band.
RQ7 — expect the confidence-bound envelope to differ from the naive rule somewhere; coincidence everywhere ⇒ honest negative on our own machinery.
RQ8 — expect survival in the pipeline-constant arm; vanishing ⇒ provenance-condition re-attribution, still decision-relevant.
